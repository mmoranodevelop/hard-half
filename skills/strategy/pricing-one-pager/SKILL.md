# Pricing One-Pager

**Price Architecture — Metric / Fences / GBB** — one page: named NBA, floor/ceiling band, metric, ladder or fences, discount policy, ASK to lock. Cost is the floor, not the price. Default: venture offer.

Method origin: Nagle public (cost-plus is a delusion) + Mohammed GBB (HBR 2018). Van Westendorp only if they ran the four questions.

If they want a value-based-pricing lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A new offer, a re-price, or "we charge what we charged last year"
- The room says cost-plus
- Need a metric, fences, and a ladder
- Before a list-price conversation with sales (policy, not a one-off discount)

## When not to use

- Contribution physics — [Unit Economics](../../strategy/unit-economics/SKILL.md); this uses it as the floor
- The job is unknown — [Jobs to Be Done](../../strategy/jobs-to-be-done/SKILL.md) first
- NBA candidates unclear — [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md)
- Future press release — [PR/FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md) may announce a number; this designs it
- Capital case — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md) uses price as an input
- One live extra off list — [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md)
- Non-standard price + legal + delivery as a package — [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Price-architecture page + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a markup sheet | Rebuild from NBA + value; kill cost-plus |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, fake survey, or collusion | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Cost is the floor, not the price.** Do not write a markup formula as the method.
2. **Start from the named NBA** (including do-nothing). A labelled band beats a fake single number.
3. **Metric tracks value.** A metric they can game is a future discount war.
4. **Fences, not favours.** What they give up to pay less. Discounts without fences leak.
5. **GBB is a default when WTP is mixed**, not a religion. They cannot take Best outcomes at Good price.
6. **Van Westendorp only with data** — range-finder, not the price.
7. **Name list vs pocket.** Unmanaged discounting makes architecture theatre.
8. **Do not invent WTP.** Holes stay holes. Illegal price-fixing / collusion / RPM → refuse.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake WTP.

1. Offer (what is sold) — load-bearing
2. Who pays (buyer, user, economic customer — may differ) — load-bearing
3. NBA, including do-nothing — load-bearing
4. Cost floor (from unit-economics or unknown)
5. Any actual WTP evidence (win/loss, pilots, survey — or none)
6. Current discounts + constraints (regulation, live lists, MFN)
7. Decision owner / date

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK + owner + date. Cost-plus is not the method.
2. NBA is named (including do-nothing).
3. Ceiling/floor are bands with assumptions, not fake precision.
4. Metric tracks value, not internal cost.
5. Fences exist if more than one price. Discount policy named or "no discount".
6. Van Westendorp only with data.
7. No invented WTP. One page.

If 1, 2, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Van Westendorp without survey data → refuse the chart.
- Cost-plus as the method (unless regulated) → refuse.
- Collusion / RPM → refuse.
- The only live question is one extra off list → [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md).
