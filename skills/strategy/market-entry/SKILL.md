# Market Entry

**Yes / no / test 1-pager** on **one** named geo or segment: beachhead, how we enter, first cheque, dated kill. Default: venture or an F500 line taking an existing offer to a new market (Ansoff market development).

Method origin: Ansoff 1957 (HBR) market development vs diversification; public operator rule — win a beachhead before a blitz; hold the invariant, adapt the rest.

If they want a lecture on Ansoff: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "Should we enter [country / segment / vertical]?"
- Pick the first beachhead, not the map
- Set the first cheque and the kill test before leases and hires
- Rewrite a 40-slide "expansion strategy" into a decision

## When not to use

- 3–4 worlds under uncertainty — [Scenario Planning](../../strategy/scenario-planning/SKILL.md)
- Tear down a named rival — [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md)
- Full capital options + NPV — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md) (attach this page as the entry exhibit)
- Dated tests for a bet already in market — [Kill Criteria](../../strategy/kill-criteria/SKILL.md)
- Source the capability (make / buy / ally) — [Make vs Buy](../../strategy/make-vs-buy/SKILL.md)
- Mode is "ally with X" as the paper — [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md)

If they will not name the geo **or** the segment, stop.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Enter / test / no 1-pager |
| **redline** | They pasted an expansion deck | Same facts; one beachhead; kill test; kill the map |
| **refuse** | No name, or two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One target.** "EMEA" is not a beachhead. "UK SMB for product X" is.
2. **Yes / test / no.** A test is a reversible first cheque. If the first step is a long lease and a subsidiary, you already said yes — call it that.
3. **Beachhead before blitz.** Win one narrow ICP. Do not open three countries to "learn in parallel" unless each has its own cheque and kill.
4. **Name the invariant.** What must travel unchanged. What must localise. If you cannot split them, you are hoping.
5. **Right to win in one sentence.** Why *this* offer in *this* beachhead, with evidence we have (not TAM theatre).
6. **Entry mode is a choice:** direct, partner, acquire, digital-only, do-nothing.
7. **Kill test dated before money:** metric, threshold, date, caller. Sunk cost will not be a reason to stay.
8. **Ansoff risk named.** Existing product + new market (this skill) vs new product + new market (diversification — heavier evidence).
9. **One ASK.** Enter test / enter full / do not enter. Owner, date.
10. **One page.** Country encyclopedia goes nowhere. Do not invent TAM as the reason to enter.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 3, 6 are missing after one round: issues list.

1. Named geo or segment and the offer we would take there — load-bearing
2. Why now (pull vs our ambition)
3. First cheque we will actually spend — load-bearing
4. What "win the beachhead" means in a named horizon
5. What would make us walk
6. Who has the D — load-bearing

```
RECOMMENDATION (few lines)

BEACHHEAD

RIGHT TO WIN / HOLES
- Evidence we have: [ ]

- Spend: [$] buys [test] for [n] months
- Stop if [metric] [threshold] by [date], called by [name]

```

## QA (must pass)

1. Named beachhead, not a region collage.
2. Yes / test / no is a complete sentence.
3. Invariant vs localise split.
4. Entry mode named.
5. First cheque in $.
6. Kill: metric, threshold, date, caller.
7. One ASK.
8. Ansoff box named if the product is also new.
9. No invented TAM as the reason to enter.
10. Not a scenario set, not a teardown.

If 1, 5, 6, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want four futures — [Scenario Planning](../../strategy/scenario-planning/SKILL.md).
- They want NPV of a plant — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md); keep this as the beachhead exhibit.
- Sanctions, licence, data-residency, export control — counsel / compliance; do not enter on a slide.
- They already signed leases → this is now a kill/stay decision ([Kill Criteria](../../strategy/kill-criteria/SKILL.md)).
