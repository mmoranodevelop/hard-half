# Jobs to Be Done

**Job-story 1-pager + stop-building list.** When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome] — plus three or more things we stop if that job is true. Without the kill-list it is a slogan. Default: venture offer.

Method origin: Christensen HBR (job not demographic) + Moesta switch interview + Intercom job story.

If they want to be taught JTBD: one paragraph then produce or stop. Do not dump Competing Against Luck. Do not steal the milkshake as their research.

## When to use

- "Why do they actually buy / churn / switch?"
- A roadmap full of requests that do not cohere
- Before pricing or a PR/FAQ (value is downstream of the job)
- Designing a switch-interview guide or coding interviews already done
- The team is arguing personas or "users like sleek UI"

## When not to use

- Future press release — [PR/FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md) after the job is true
- Category feature matrix — [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md)
- Price architecture — [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Diagnosing a messy internal problem — [Issue Tree](../../strategy/issue-tree/SKILL.md)
- Persona workshop, NPS theatre, UX copy polish — refuse
- Inventing a job from the founder's mouth with no customer contact — hypothesis only, labelled
- Minors / medical / other sensitive jobs beyond the brief — refuse

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Notes or interviews exist | Job story + stop-building 1-pager |
| **redline** | "we already know the job" / a persona deck | Fail QA; rewrite the story; force the kill-list |
| **refuse** | Brainstorm declared as the job, or they will not kill anything | Issues list. Stop |

Interview-guide-only (nobody scheduled): still **produce**, labelled **hypothesis**, plus who to call. Do not present it as research.

## Hard rules

1. **Job is not a product, persona, or feature.** Circumstance is load-bearing.
2. **Competitors are what they hired last** (spreadsheet, agency, do-nothing), not the pitch-deck peer set.
3. **Switch interviews beat hypotheticals.** Timeline: first thought, looking, decision, first use. "Would you use" is not JTBD.
4. **Four forces:** push of the current, pull of the new, anxiety of the new, habit of the old.
5. **Stop-building list is mandatory** (three or more real items). If you cannot name any, you have not chosen a job.
6. **n is honest.** A handful of switch interviews can identify a job; they do not prove prevalence. Do not fake a TAM from the calls.
7. **Hypothesis must be labelled.** Do not present founder fiction as research.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are blank after one pass: issues list, not a fake job.

1. The offer or bet — load-bearing
2. Evidence (interviews, tickets, win/loss — or none) — load-bearing
3. Decision owner / date (what will change on the roadmap) — load-bearing
4. Who we can still call (people who switched recently)
5. Roadmap items they are willing to kill (if "none", still name candidates)
6. Language

## Output shape

```
JOB  |  [offer or bet]  |  [date]
ASK: [owner] kills [items 1-3] by [date] / books [n] switch interviews by [date]
n = [ ] switch interviews / notes     hypothesis only: yes / no

Job story:
When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome].

Functional job (verb + object + context): [ ]
NBA they fired / still hire on bad days: [ ]

Forces: push [ ] / pull [ ] / anxiety [ ] / habit [ ]

Evidence (short, cited; no quote dump):
- [ ]

STOP BUILDING (if this job is true)
1. [ ]
2. [ ]
3. [ ]

KEEP / BUILD ONLY BECAUSE OF THIS JOB
- [ ]

INTERVIEW GAPS / WHO TO CALL NEXT
- [ ]

Not a persona. Not a PR-FAQ. n does not become TAM.
```

Switch-interview guide when they need to call: `assets/switch-interview.md`. Timeline, not preference. Do not pitch.

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK + owner + date. Job story has situation, motivation, outcome.
2. Circumstance is specific. Competitors include workarounds / previous hires.
3. Evidence labelled (n, or "hypothesis").
4. Stop-building list has three or more real items.
5. Forces include anxiety or habit, not only push/pull.
6. No stolen milkshake as their data. No TAM invented from a handful of calls.
7. Not a persona sheet. Not a PR-FAQ. One page.

If 1, 3, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want a press release → [PR/FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md).
- They will not kill anything on the roadmap → refuse; a job that does not change the roadmap is a poster.
- Quantitative ODI survey they are not resourced to run → name the hole; do not fake scores.
- Sensitive jobs beyond the brief → refuse.
