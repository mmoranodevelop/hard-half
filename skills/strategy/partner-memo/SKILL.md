# Partner Memo

**Partner Memo — Hire / Vendor / Partner / Walk** — one page: named counterparty, job in one sentence, three-way choice, two-way assets, governance D, exclusivity cost, exit in 12 months, one ASK. Default: venture; same spine for F500.

Method origin: BCG public alliances (non-monetary assets, shared goal) + BCG 2026 tech-AI (risk, governance, upside vs a PO) + P&G Connect+Develop public (still own the consumer need).

If they want an alliances lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A named counterparty (or "hire a team") is on the table
- A vendor is asking to be called a partner
- JV / alliance / exclusive is proposed
- Hire vs contractor vs firm for a capability we will need for years

## When not to use

- Capability source with make / buy / partner / kill still open — [Make vs Buy](../../strategy/make-vs-buy/SKILL.md)
- The contract after vendor is chosen — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)
- One role's interview card — [Hiring Scorecard](../../management/hiring-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- Hire a team as headcount — [Headcount Plan](../../management/headcount-plan/SKILL.md)
- The partner *is* the geo entry mode — [Market Entry](../../strategy/market-entry/SKILL.md)
- NPV of a plant — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named counterparty + job | Hire / vendor / partner / walk page + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a "partnership" deck | Force the three-way choice; add exit; kill PO-as-partner |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a lecture on alliances | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Three options on the page.** Hire (we employ), Vendor (money for a defined result), Partner (shared goal, both put in non-money assets, shared risk/governance/upside). A nicer PO is a vendor — say so.
2. **Name the job** in one sentence. Collecting logos is not a memo.
3. **What each side puts in.** Talent, brand, data, distribution, capital, IP. A one-way put is a vendor or a donation.
4. **Governance.** Who has the D on roadmap, brand, customer, price. No D = not a partner.
5. **Exclusivity is a cost.** What we cannot do with others, for how long, kill fee.
6. **Exit.** How we leave in 12 months with data, customers, and IP we need.
7. **Do not outsource the differentiator** and still call it a partnership.
8. **One ASK:** partner / vendor / hire / walk. Owner, date.
9. **Do not invent savings %.** Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake memo.

1. Named counterparty (or "hire a team" as the alternative) — load-bearing
2. Job to be done in one sentence — load-bearing
3. What we would put in and what they would — load-bearing
4. Why not just PO / why not just hire
5. Exclusivity or lock-in already proposed
6. Who has the D on this choice

## QA (must pass)

1. Named counterparty and named job.
2. All three options on the page.
3. "Partner" requires two-way assets + governance D + exit.
4. Exclusivity cost named or "none".
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not an SOW, not a make-vs-buy of an unnamed capability.
7. No invented savings %.
8. Differentiator not given away without a sentence that admits it.
9. One page.

If 1, 2, 3, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They already want the paper → [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md) if vendor; counsel if JV.
- Capability itself unchosen → [Make vs Buy](../../strategy/make-vs-buy/SKILL.md).
- Antitrust / exclusive dealing / export → counsel; do not ship a fake exclusivity.
