# Term Sheet Read

**Business read 1-pager + waterfall** of a named term sheet or SAFE: who owns what, who controls the board, who gets paid at three exits. Default: venture. Counsel drafts clauses; this page decides whether the **economics and control** are acceptable.

Method origin: NVCA model documents (industry norms, not clause clones); YC post-money SAFE primer; YC "standard and clean" Series A. Reconstruct the operator read. Do not practise law.

If they want a SAFE lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A term sheet or SAFE just arrived (or we are about to issue one)
- Dilution / control / a named exit must be a number, not an adjective
- Compare two sheets on economics and control
- Founder or MD about to sign under time pressure

## When not to use

- The **process** to raise (amount, list, timeline) — [Fundraising One-Pager](../../strategy/fundraising-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- The monthly investor letter — [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md)
- Whether the *company* should spend — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)
- An F500 vendor MSA — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)
- The email to the board / co-founder after the read — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)

If they have no paper (or a verbal "we'll do a SAFE later"), stop. Issues list.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Paper in hand | 1-pager: dilution, control, waterfall, ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a sheet | Issues by load-bearing term; rewrite the business spine |
| **refuse** | No paper, or "explain SAFEs" as a class | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Not legal advice.** First line of the page says so. Flag legal; do not write indemnity prose.
2. **Three questions only:** dilution, control, liquidation. Everything else is a flag for counsel or a dirty-term list (YC public: >1x pref, participating, cumulative dividends, warrant coverage).
3. **Model the cap table.** Pre / post, option pool **who pays**, SAFE stack additive on post-money SAFEs. Do not invent a valuation or share count.
4. **Three exits.** Preference overhang vs as-converted: a low exit, a mid exit, a good exit. A sheet without a waterfall is a vibe.
5. **Board + protective provisions = control.** Who can fire the CEO. Which acts need preferred consent.
6. **SAFE ≠ priced.** Post-money cap locks a % *before* new money; more SAFEs stack on common; no board until conversion.
7. **One ASK:** sign / negotiate these 1–3 terms / walk. Owner, date (no-shop clock if any).

## Intake

Refuse a FOR-SIGNING read if the paper is missing **and** the fully diluted count is missing. Two load-bearing holes → issues list.

1. The paper (term sheet or SAFE) and instrument — load-bearing
2. Amount, cap / pre-money, pool, existing convertibles
3. Current cap table (founders, pool, prior) — load-bearing
4. Board today and what the paper does to it
5. Decision: we are issuing or receiving; date we must answer
6. Counsel already engaged? (if no, say so on the page)

## QA (must pass)

1. "Not legal advice" on the page.
2. Dilution % after close, or a labelled hole for FD count.
3. Board / protective / who fires CEO named.
4. Three-exit waterfall.
5. Instrument classified (SAFE vs priced).
6. Pool refresh payer named.
7. One ASK: sign / negotiate / walk, date.
8. No invented share counts.
9. Dirty terms listed or "none — 1x non-participating, standard".
10. Not a pitch, not a process plan.

If 2, 3, 4, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want you to draft the SPA / COI → counsel; this skill stops at the business read.
- Securities / tax / QSBS / 83(b) → counsel.
- They want a lecture on "what is a SAFE" → one paragraph then produce or stop.
- No-shop about to expire → say so in the ASK; do not slow the page for colour.
