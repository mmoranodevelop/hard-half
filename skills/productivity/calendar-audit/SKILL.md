# Calendar Audit

**Cut list + two protected 90-minute blocks** — every recurring meeting gets kill / delegate / keep / convert, with a killer and a date. ASK: cancel the named kills this week. Default: venture MD inheriting a meeting estate unless they say F500.

Method origin: meetings are a cost with a cascade (Bain/Mankins public); cognitively demanding work needs unbroken time. Reconstruct the verbs. Do not quote textbooks.

If they want a deep-work lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "I have no time to think" / calendar is other people's
- New chair inheriting a meeting estate; after a re-org or remote-work bulge
- Someone asks for kill / delegate / keep plus protected blocks
- Price one recurring meeting before installing it

## When not to use

- Designing the default week (spine objects) — [Weekly Cadence](../../productivity/weekly-cadence/SKILL.md) first
- Team's metrics meeting design — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- Too many projects, not too many meetings — [WIP Limit](../../productivity/wip-limit/SKILL.md)
- Written update that replaces a broadcast — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- Mission meeting that *should* exist — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- A single 1:1 agenda — [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md)

If there is no spine, say so and offer to design it first. A cut list without a spine just refills.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Calendar dump | Cut list + protected blocks + ASK |
| **redline** | "Is my week strategic?" | Fail QA against last 2 weeks *actual* |
| **refuse** | No calendar evidence, or they want a monastic day | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Every recurring item gets one verb: kill / delegate / keep / convert. "Maybe" is not a verb. Convert = written update, 1:1, or office hour.
2. Do not steal the spine. 1:1s, one staff slot, one market contact, the close — keep unless they have no reports.
3. After the cut, place ≥2 unbroken 90-minute blocks named for an output. If the cut does not create them, it failed.
4. Killer + date on every kill/convert. A meeting without a killer is still there later.
5. Direct cost = people × duration. Cascade if known; else write "unknown". No invented hours.
6. Authority respected: recommend-only if they do not own the meeting. Default is decline, not "make the meeting better".

## Intake

If **last 2 weeks of actual meetings** and **whose meetings they can cancel** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake audit.

1. Last 2 weeks of actual meetings (export, list, or honest recitation) — load-bearing
2. Role, direct reports, board cycle
3. Spine already designed? (yes / no)
4. This week's contribution sentence (so blocks have a purpose)
5. Authority: whose meetings can they actually cancel? — load-bearing
6. Language

## QA (must pass)

1. Every recurring item has a verb.
2. Spine objects were not accidentally killed.
3. ≥2 × 90-minute protected blocks, named for an output.
4. Killer + date on every kill/convert. ASK has owner + date.
5. No invented cascade number. Authority respected.
6. Broadcasts converted to writing or explicitly kept as broadcasts.
7. One page (long tail appendix). Governing thought is a complete sentence.

If 1, 3, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Focus-app stack / Pomodoro religion / copied monastic day → refuse; give the cut list and blocks.
- Delete the team's operating review from here → no; you may *price* it.
- Calendar evidence refused → stop. Classification rules, not a fake audit.
- Real problem is too many projects → [WIP Limit](../../productivity/wip-limit/SKILL.md).
