# Weekly Cadence

**Week-spine 1-pager + Friday-close card** — named recurring blocks, contribution sentence, kill rule, ASK to protect this week's contribution block. A successor could run the chair next week. Default: venture MD with direct reports unless they say F500.

Method origin: manager output = org output (Grove public); start with time then contribution (Drucker public). Reconstruct the objects. Do not copy a celebrity timetable.

If they want a lifestyle-OS lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "Design my week" / calendar full of other people's meetings, no spine
- New MD / GM / program lead needing a default week
- Reset after travel, fundraising, or a fire
- Busy every day and cannot name what the org produced

## When not to use

- Team's weekly/monthly metrics meeting — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- One-off calendar audit, kill/delegate/keep — [Calendar Audit](../../productivity/calendar-audit/SKILL.md)
- A single 1:1 agenda — [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md)
- Inspect promises at the close — [Commitment Register](../../productivity/commitment-register/SKILL.md)
- How many contributions may be in flight — [WIP Limit](../../productivity/wip-limit/SKILL.md)
- Mission meeting that should exist — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- ELT fiduciary paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)

If they want to cut meetings, design the spine first, then route to calendar-audit.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Spine 1-pager + close card + ASK |
| **redline** | Pasted calendar / "is my week sane?" | Fail QA against last week's *actual* calendar |
| **refuse** | No contribution sentence, or they want a 4am protocol | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Output, not occupancy. Test: what did the *organisation* produce because of this week?
2. Spine before decoration. Mandatory objects: contribution block (named output), 1:1s (report's agenda), one staff/coordination (not an operating-review), one market/customer contact (or a dated waiver), commitment pass, weekly close.
3. Contribution is a block, not a leftover. Unbreakable as a board slot. Not "catch up on email".
4. Process meetings are recurring; mission (decision) meetings are rare. If mission eats the week, do not add a huddle — the org is malorganised.
5. Kill rule: a new recurring meeting replaces a named spine slot. No silent adds.
6. Cascade: estimate prep hours below this chair, or write "unknown". Never assume zero. No copied 5am club. IC week: drop 1:1s and staff; do not fake a management cadence.

## Intake

If **contribution sentence** and **who reports** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake spine.

1. Role and span (who reports, who they owe upward) — load-bearing
2. Contribution this week / this month (one sentence output) — load-bearing
3. Hard constraints (board day, site, timezone, travel)
4. Current recurring meetings (list)
5. 1:1 rhythm today (exist / fake / absent)
6. Operating-review already exists? (so we do not duplicate it)

## Output shape

```
WEEK SPINE  |  [role]  |  week of [date]
Owner: [MD]   Contribution this week (one sentence output): [ ]

| Day | Block | Duration | Who | Purpose | Done = |
| [ ] | Contribution | 90–180m | solo | [named artefact] | [ ] |
| [ ] | 1:1 [name] | 30–45m | report | their agenda | [ ] |
| [ ] | Staff / coordination | 25–45m | directs | facts + conflicts + next 7d | [ ] |
| [ ] | Market contact | 30–60m | customer/site | what we heard | [ ] |
| [ ] | Commitment pass | 15–20m | self/CoS | on track / at risk / renegotiate / done | [ ] |
| [ ] | Weekly close | 30–45m | self/CoS | produced / slipped / abandoned / next contribution | [ ] |

Protected (nobody else books): [days/times]
Kill rule: a new recurring meeting replaces [ ]
Cascade: ~[n] person-hours of prep below me / unknown
Mission meetings this week (named, or none): [ ]
Not spine (candidates for calendar-audit): [ ]

FRIDAY CLOSE (fill at close)
Produced: [org output]
Slipped (promise, owner, new date): [ ]
Abandoned: [ ]
Next week's contribution: [ ]
Asks (owner, date): [ ]

ASK: [MD] protects the contribution block this week as a board slot by [date]. Protect / waiver (reason) / refuse lifestyle-OS.
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Contribution is a complete-sentence output, not "be available".
2. Contribution block exists and is dated. Close exists.
3. 1:1s on the spine and belong to the reports (or IC waiver).
4. At most one staff slot; it is not an operating-review in disguise.
5. Market contact or a dated waiver. Kill rule named.
6. ASK has owner + date. Cascade estimated or "unknown".
7. One page. No celebrity routine.

If 1, 2, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Colour-coded life-os / second-brain / 4am protocol → refuse; give the spine table.
- Team KPI meeting designed here → [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md).
- No reports and no contribution → IC week or refuse.
- Real problem is meeting load, not missing spine → [Calendar Audit](../../productivity/calendar-audit/SKILL.md) next.
