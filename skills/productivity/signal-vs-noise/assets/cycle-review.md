## Periodic check — every fifth cycle or so

- **Is the mission still the mission?** Changing daily means it was never a mission — zoom out one level and find the thing that held still.
- **What has the Batch column cost this week?** Four hours a day of mandatory Noise is not a scheduling problem. Something in there needs delegating, automating, or refusing.
- **What is on the list only out of momentum?** Items survive because they were on yesterday's list, not because anyone re-decided them.
- **Is anything being built for scale before it has users?** If so, the manual version is faster and teaches more.
