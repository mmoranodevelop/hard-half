## problem-solving

_Problems that are stuck — derive rather than retrieve. Also: candidates that look like value and may not be. Also: the five ways a default solver still fails after those three — wrong problem, eaten fix, empty AND, adaptive player, missing coordinate._

- `first-principles` — Attack a problem that is stuck — every known approach tried, the accepted answer is "impossible with current technology", or the cost, physics, or scale numbers refuse to work.
- `value-problem-solver` — Run a product idea, feature, PRD, vendor proposal, or initiative through seven value filters and produce an Audit verdict or a Design walk before anyone writes the spec.
- `verified-hard-problem` — Attack one locked unsolved claim (millennial, prize-grade, safety, legal-grade) with lemma swarm and a verification gate; refuse unverified "solved".
- `ghost-problem` — Prove the stated problem is load-bearing, or name the problem whose death would make this one evaporate.
- `cobra-equilibrium` — Before a metric, incentive, or agent reward ships, write how the measured system will eat it, then survive or refuse.
- `empty-and` — Prove or kill the claim that several locally-working solutions can coexist; find the empty intersection.
- `red-queen` — Treat the problem as a player that updates when you move; name half-life, then equilibrium or an arms-race stop.
- `missing-axis` — Find the measurable coordinate the current search space does not have, so every incumbent option is the same option written differently.

_Voice, tone, and documents as prose._

- `all-hands` — writing the company or program all-hands narrative after a decision: what we decided, why, what it means, what we will not do, Q&A, manager cascade.
- `board-qa-prep` — prepping hostile or real board questions plus 30-second answers before a board or IC sitting.
- `crisis-holding` — Use in the first 60–90 minutes of a live issue: holding statement (known, unknown, doing, next-update clock) plus who must know.
- `difficult-conversation` — preparing a hard conversation (performance, conflict, a no): purpose, facts vs story, 30–60s opening, one ask, fallback.
- `investor-update` — Use for a monthly letter to investors: miss first, same metrics, cash/burn/ runway, specific asks.
- `meeting-notes-to-decisions` — Use after a meeting to turn notes into a sendable decision log: decided, pending, parked, each with owner and date.
- `offer-conversation` — closing or walking a named offer: comp, scope, start date, fallback.
- `principal-brief` — a staffer must brief a principal on one page: question, recommendation, three facts, ask.
- `pyramid-principle` — writing or rewriting an email, Slack/chat message, verbal update, or argumentative deck with Barbara Minto's pyramid: answer first, grouped key lines, MECE, SCQ opening.
- `voice-blueprint` — Map a person's real writing voice into versioned blueprints other agents can load when writing email, messages, or documents.
