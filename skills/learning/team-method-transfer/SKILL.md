# Team Method Transfer

**Session run-of-show + 14-day loop + pass bar** — one named method, live artifacts, named coach, dated reps, day-14 closed-book pass. ASK: lock the coach and the loop dates. Default: MD / program lead transferring one operator method to a small team.

Method origin: cognitive apprenticeship (Collins, Brown & Holum, AFT 1991 public) — make thinking visible; named-error feedback. Operator-sized: session + 14 days. Do not claim 10,000 hours.

If they want a learning-science lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "Teach the team this method" / get them running pyramid / RAID / stage-gate on live work
- A method exists and the team still cannot *do* it
- After a training day that produced slides and no change

## When not to use

- Encode a book / video into concept objects — [Source to Competence](../../learning/source-to-competence/SKILL.md)
- Compile an AI skill — [Competence to Skill](../../learning/competence-to-skill/SKILL.md)
- Person-in-role ramp — [Ninety-Day Onboarding](../../learning/ninety-day-onboarding/SKILL.md) (may *include* a transfer)
- How a single piece is ordered — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md) (example *method* this skill might transfer)

If they want "just the workshop": produce the session and write **incomplete** until loop dates exist — or **refuse** "done".

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Session + 14-day loop + pass bar + ASK |
| **redline** | Pasted workshop deck | Fail QA (no think-aloud, no live rep, no loop) |
| **refuse** | No live artifact, no named coach, or "certify this afternoon" | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One method**, named in one line. Not "agile". Not "how we do product".
2. Competence is observable on live work by day 14. "Understand the method" fails.
3. Session sequence (do not skip): Model (think aloud on a live artifact) → Coach (team attempts; expert hints, no keyboard) → Scaffold (checklist that will fade) → Articulate → Reflect → Explore (assign the loop).
4. Loop is the transfer: dated live reps in 14 days, named coach, named-error feedback.
5. Live artifacts, not fictional cases. Irreversible sends modelled on a draft until pass bar.
6. Pass bar: new live artifact, expert watches, does not type. Do not encode the book in the room. Do not compile an AI skill here.

## Intake

If **live artifact** and **named coach** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake workshop.

1. Method (name + the skill/playbook it lives in, if any) — load-bearing
2. Team (roles, size, who is the expert)
3. Competence they actually need on the job (observable)
4. Live artifacts available this week — load-bearing
5. Session slot + who coaches in the 14 days (named person) — load-bearing
6. What they must not send live until the pass bar

## QA (must pass)

1. One named method; competence is observable.
2. Session includes think-aloud on a live artifact and a team attempt in the room.
3. 14-day loop has dated live reps and a named coach, or the pack is labelled INCOMPLETE.
4. Feedback names errors. Pass bar is a new artifact, expert off the keyboard.
5. Not a book encoding; not an AI-skill compile.
6. ASK has owner + date.
7. Scaffold has a fade date. Irreversible sends protected.
8. Not a slide outline. Not "practice on your own".

If 1, 2, 3, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- "Just a brown-bag" with no artifact → refuse or session-only labelled incomplete.
- Method is a book they have not encoded → [Source to Competence](../../learning/source-to-competence/SKILL.md).
- They want an agent skill → [Competence to Skill](../../learning/competence-to-skill/SKILL.md).
- No coach for 14 days → refuse the loop, or name a coach.
- "Certify the team this afternoon" → refuse the certificate.
