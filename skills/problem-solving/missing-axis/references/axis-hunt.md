# Axis hunt

An axis is a **coordinate**: a unit or a comparison, and a split. Themes are not axes.

## What counts

| Passes | Fails |
|---|---|
| Time-until-the-customer-can-leave-you (days), vs switching-cost-as-feature-count | "Stickiness" |
| Who holds the write-lock (named role), vs how clean the data looks | "Data quality" |
| Energy per useful output (joules), vs cost at today's tariff | "Efficiency" as a vibe |
| Whether the failure is visible to the person who can fix it (yes/no), vs how severe it is | "Observability" as a product category |
| Reversibility window (hours until the move cannot be undone) | "Agility" |

The split test: take two options the room treats as the same (or as opposites on a current axis) and say how they would rank differently on the candidate. If they would not, you have renamed a current axis.

## Mute-reasons (pick one)

Why the field is silent. Each suggests a different confirmation.

| Mute-reason | What confirmation looks like |
|---|---|
| **Unmeasured** | The instrument does not exist or is not in the dashboard. Confirmation: score two collinear options on a cheap manual version of the unit |
| **Not in the ontology** | The field's papers, RFCs, or strategy decks have no cell for it. Confirmation: find one outsider domain that already measures it and import the unit, then score |
| **Unspeakable** | Someone would lose if it were on the axes (a budget, a myth, a hero narrative). Confirmation: the ranking reverses when the coordinate is forced onto a closed-door page |
| **Newly possible** | An instrument, a right, a material, or a compute regime did not exist when the current axes froze. Confirmation: the new instrument actually moves, in a probe, independently of the old axes |

If you cannot pick a mute-reason, the candidate is probably a current axis with a new nickname.

## Ideological camps

When the room is camp A vs camp B, they have usually collapsed several coordinates onto one fight (control vs speed, safety vs capability, central vs local). The missing axis is often **orthogonal** to that fight: a coordinate both camps are silent on because it does not help them win the current argument.

Do not offer a synthesis ("a bit of both"). That stays on the line. Find a split that makes two A-like options look different.

## "Culture" and "quality"

Refuse these as axes until they have a unit. Culture-as-axis becomes "who can say no without a meeting" or "time until a dissent is cheap." Quality-as-axis becomes a named failure the customer sees, with a rate. Until then they are sediment.

## Relation to first-principles

After confirmation, the invariants may change because a new measurable entered the ledger. Run `first-principles` *then*, on the enlarged space. Running it first reproduces the collinear options with better stationery. That is the failure this skill exists to catch.
