# Editorial Calendar

**4–8 week assignment desk** — beat (thesis sentence), slot grid (angle, format, owner, draft due), kill list, ASK to freeze week 1. A PA or ghostwriter can run it. This skill does **not** write the posts. Default: venture MD on LinkedIn, 6 weeks, 2 slots/week, unless they say F500 / other edition.

Method origin: newsroom assignment desk / forward planner (AP public craft) — if there is no story, there is no slot.

If they want a how-to-post lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "LinkedIn calendar for the next six weeks"
- Thesis exists; posting is ad hoc
- Ghostwriter/PA needs assignments, not vibes

## When not to use

- No thesis yet — [Thought Leadership Thesis](../../branding/thought-leadership-thesis/SKILL.md)
- Brand system / exclusions — [Positioning House](../../branding/positioning-house/SKILL.md)
- Write the carousel — assign it; [Social Carousel](../../branding/social-carousel/SKILL.md)
- Write the prose post — assign it; [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- Bios — [Bio Pack](../../branding/bio-pack/SKILL.md)
- Company brand grid, 12-month content-marketing calendar, "make me go viral", daily posting as a goal — refuse

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Desk + assignment grid + ASK (6 weeks unless they pick 4 or 8) |
| **redline** | Existing calendar | Kill filler; restore thesis; holes labelled |
| **refuse** | No thesis, or they want daily filler | Issues list. Stop |

Floor 4 weeks. Ceiling 8. Unclear → produce at 6.

## Hard rules

1. **No thesis, no calendar.** Route. Do not invent a beat from a job title.
2. Every remaining slot has an angle (complete-sentence claim), not a theme word. "Week 3: leadership" fails.
3. Empty weeks are **kill** or labelled reply-week. Never quote-cards. A 6-week grid with 4 real pieces beats 6 weeks of sludge.
4. Default cadence: 2 LinkedIn slots/week, or 1 LinkedIn + light X. Daily is a different job. Slot count must match hours they will actually give.
5. Formats point at other skills: prose → pyramid; carousel → social-carousel; thread → carousel x-thread pack; silence → explicit kill.
6. Pegs are real (travel, a number they will publish, a conference they are at) or "evergreen beat". No fake awareness days.
7. Owner is a name. Byline = MD. Comments in the first hour = MD or the slot is not worth posting.
8. Virality is not a KPI. Do not write ranking folklore. Do not invent angles.

## Intake

If **thesis** is missing after one round: issues list and route.

1. Thesis (paste) — or route — load-bearing
2. House this/not-that (topics they will not post), if any
3. Horizon (4 / 6 / 8 weeks) and capacity (hours, who drafts, who posts, who comments)
4. Editions (LinkedIn, X)
5. Real pegs already known + quiet periods (legal, M&A, vacation)
6. Language / voice overlay for later production

## QA (must pass)

1. Thesis is a disagreeable sentence at the top — not an essay about "personal brand".
2. Every remaining slot has an angle, owner, format, and draft due date.
3. Empty weeks are killed or labelled reply-week.
4. Horizon 4–8 weeks. Slot count matches capacity.
5. Formats point at carousel or pyramid; this skill did not write the pieces.
6. Pegs are real or evergreen beat. Kill list ≥3.
7. ASK has owner + date (freeze week 1).
8. No best-time-to-post claims. No invented angles.

If 1, 2, 3, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- No thesis after one ask → refuse. Thesis skill, not a grid of topics.
- 90-day daily calendar → refuse daily; offer 6 weeks at 2/week.
- Filler because "the algorithm hates gaps" → refuse; label silence.
- "Write all the posts in this skill" → produce the desk, then run pyramid/carousel per assignment.
- Crisis / legal hold → freeze the desk; do not newsjack.
