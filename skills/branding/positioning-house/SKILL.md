# Positioning House

**Messaging House — Roof / Three Pillars / This-Not-That** — one page: one identity, one audience, roof as a disagreeable sentence, three pillars with checkable proof, ≥5 this/not-that pairs, one-line, ASK to lock exclusions. Default: venture MD / personal brand unless they say company or F500.

Method origin: Ries/Trout public (own a slot in a mind) + Neumeier public onlyness + messaging-house craft (roof, pillars, proof).

If they want a positioning lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- One identity must say one thing across decks, bios, posts
- Category is fuzzy or proof is "world-class"
- Every page currently says something different

## When not to use

- The thinker's one idea — [Thought Leadership Thesis](../../branding/thought-leadership-thesis/SKILL.md)
- Bios / speaker intro / LinkedIn About — [Bio Pack](../../branding/bio-pack/SKILL.md)
- Swipeable slides — [Social Carousel](../../branding/social-carousel/SKILL.md)
- Posting grid — [Editorial Calendar](../../branding/editorial-calendar/SKILL.md)
- How it sounds — [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md)
- Offer architecture / list — [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | House + ASK to lock exclusions |
| **redline** | They pasted an about-page | Same facts; kill adjectives; holes labelled |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or will not exclude | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Roof is a complete-sentence claim** a named rival cannot say without lying. "We do X, Y, Z" is a brochure.
2. **One primary audience** (role + situation). Second audience is a pillar cut, not a second roof.
3. **Category is a slot they already have**, or the slot you replace. No neologism in sentence 1.
4. **≥5 this/not-that pairs.** Real exclusions. Virtue vs vice fails.
5. **Three pillars, each with checkable proof.** Leave holes. Do not invent numbers.
6. **Name the strategic enemy** (habit, convention, do-nothing). Give something up or refuse.
7. **Audience is the hero; this brand is the guide.** Plan ≤3 steps.
8. **Do not clone a competitor's line.** Voice is overlay, not position.

## Intake

If **audience** and **proof** are both missing after one round: issues list.

1. Identity (person or company — one) — load-bearing
2. Primary audience (role + situation) — load-bearing
3. Named alternatives, including do-nothing
4. Proof they actually have (numbers, named work, method) — load-bearing
5. What they will give up (audience, offer, or claim)
6. Existing house / language

## QA (must pass)

1. Roof is a disagreeable complete sentence.
2. A named rival cannot say it without lying.
3. ≥5 real this/not-that; something was given up.
4. One picturable audience.
5. Three pillars; each has a number, named proof, or labelled hole.
6. ASK + owner + date.
7. No voice-adjective position.
8. One page.

If 1, 3, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Will not exclude after one ask → refuse; fact sheet, not a fake position.
- Clone a named brand's line → refuse the clone.
- Numbers you do not have → hole, not invention.
- Carousel / thesis / bio / calendar / voice → sibling skills.
