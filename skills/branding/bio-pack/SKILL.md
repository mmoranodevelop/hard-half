# Bio Pack

**Bio Pack — Fact Sheet + Five Public Lengths** — one pack: canonical fact sheet, then one-line, short (50–80w), long (180–280w), speaker intro (45–75s), LinkedIn About (first person). ASK which cut ships. Default: venture MD / operator; same spine for F500.

Method origin: public executive-bio craft (third person for web/press/stage) + LinkedIn Help (About is first person; first ~300 characters work; practitioner cap ~2,600 characters). Facts only.

If they want a "how to write a bio" lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "Write my bio" / speaker intro / LinkedIn About
- Conference, podcast, board pack, or leadership page needs a version
- Existing bios contradict (dates, titles, numbers)
- Same facts, new audience or event

## When not to use

- Positioning / this-not-that — [Positioning House](../../branding/positioning-house/SKILL.md)
- The one idea they are known for — [Thought Leadership Thesis](../../branding/thought-leadership-thesis/SKILL.md)
- How it sounds after facts are true — [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md)
- Posting cadence — [Editorial Calendar](../../branding/editorial-calendar/SKILL.md)
- Designed posts, not a bio — [Social Carousel](../../branding/social-carousel/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Fact sheet + five lengths + ASK |
| **redline** | Pasted bio | Strip adjectives; rebuild from facts; list kills |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, adjectives only, or ATS ask | Issues list. Stop |
| **diagnose** | Contradictory public bios | Diff the facts; do not average |

## Hard rules

1. **Facts only.** If they cannot point to a source, it does not go in. Banned: passionate, visionary, seasoned, dynamic, results-driven, self-awarded "thought leader", "helping leaders unlock".
2. **One canonical fact sheet.** Variants are cuts. Two variants disagree on a number → pack is broken.
3. **Sheet does not change by audience.** The *cut* does (which proof leads).
4. **Lengths:** one-line ≤22 words third; short 50–80 third; long 180–280 third; speaker intro 80–140 words, 45–75 seconds, spoken; LinkedIn About first person, ≤2,600 chars, first 300 = role + why-care.
5. **Numbers need denominator and period**, or they are cut.
6. **Inherit house one-line / thesis if they exist.** Do not invent strategy here.
7. **Human line only if user-supplied.** Do not invent a hobby.
8. **Speaker intro is for a named room**, or labelled generic.
9. **Do not invent titles, dates, or numbers.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1–2 are missing after one round: issues list, not a likability story.

1. Name, current role, employer, dates — load-bearing
2. Proof they will stand behind (numbers, named work) — load-bearing
3. Affiliations they want public (boards, degrees)
4. Primary audience for the default pack (clients / peers / press / this event)
5. Positioning one-line / thesis if they exist
6. Forbidden facts
7. Human line (optional, their words) / language

## QA (must pass)

1. Every claim is on the fact sheet with a source or a labelled hole.
2. Variants do not contradict.
3. No banned adjectives.
4. One-line is one breath; speaker intro 45–75s; About first person, first 300 works, ≤2,600.
5. Numbers have denominator + period, or are cut.
6. Strategy inherited, not invented. Human line only if supplied.
7. ASK names which cut ships, owner, date.
8. Not an ATS resume.
9. No invented facts.

If 1, 2, 4, or 9 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- "Make me impressive" without facts → refuse. Fact-sheet interview, not a draft.
- ATS / "optimise for recruiters" → refuse this skill (different product).
- Contradictory numbers they will not resolve → ship holes, not a blend.
- Regulated claims (performance, medical, financial) → approved wording or cut.
- Full CV / publication list / ghostwritten article as the job → refuse.
