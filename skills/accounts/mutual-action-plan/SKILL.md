# Mutual Action Plan

**Joint 1-pager the customer can co-own:** outcomes, their owners **and** ours, dates, **the ASK to confirm**. Default: professional-services / program book. Same spine for agency or SaaS CS (close-plan / go-live plan).

Method origin: MAP craft (Clari/Recapped-public) — mutually agreed next steps with owners and dates through the next gate; HubSpot-public: decision process and paper process become a shared list, not a vendor Gantt.

If they want a lecture on MAPs: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Customer and we must share one dated plan (renewal, go-live, expansion, recovery)
- Champion needs a page they can forward internally
- Our plan exists; they have not signed the steps
- Close / kickoff / save is slipping because owners are only on our side

## When not to use

- Internal 12-month plan **we** own — [Account Plan](../../accounts/account-plan/SKILL.md)
- Writing the MSA / SOW — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)
- Quarterly sitting pack — [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md) (the MAP may be the next-90 appendix)
- Promises **we** made, integrity register — [Commitment Register](../../productivity/commitment-register/SKILL.md)
- Living program RAID — [RAID Register](../../management/raid-register/SKILL.md)
- Angry client this week, facts first — [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md)
- The gate is the renewal save — [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md)

If they will not name the customer owner who must confirm, stop.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Joint MAP 1-pager + confirm ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a vendor Gantt or "close plan" | Same steps; force *their* owners; kill one-sided dates |
| **refuse** | No customer owner, or two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Joint or it is not a MAP.** Every live row has a customer owner *or* is explicitly ours. A page of only our tasks is an account plan — refuse the MAP label.
2. **Outcome at the top**, in their words. Not our milestone name.
3. **Next gate dated.** Close / go-live / next verified outcome / renewal decision. Open-ended "ongoing partnership" is a fail.
4. **Owners are named people**, not "client" / "us".
5. **Customer must confirm.** ASK is their owner to yes the plan by a date. Unconfirmed = DRAFT.
6. **Few live rows.** Rest is backlog. A 40-row Gantt is not this page.
7. **Not commercial paper.** Fees, liability, SLAs belong in [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md).
8. **Slip rule.** A missed date is renamed and re-dated, or the gate moves. Silent slip is a fail.
9. **SaaS CS skin:** go-live / adoption milestones instead of delivery gates. Spine stays.
10. **One page.** Do not invent dates they did not agree (draft may propose; must say so).

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 6 are missing after one round: issues list.

1. Account, our owner, **their confirming owner** — load-bearing
2. Joint outcome and the next gate + date — load-bearing
3. Steps already agreed (or "none yet — draft")
4. Paper / legal / security still open (dates only)
5. What happens if a date slips
6. The ASK (confirm by) — load-bearing

## Output shape

```
MAP  |  [ACCOUNT]  |  gate [CLOSE / GO-LIVE / OUTCOME / RENEWAL] [date]
Status: DRAFT (unconfirmed) / CONFIRMED [date]

ASK: [their owner] to confirm this plan by [date]
Our owner: [ ]    Their confirming owner: [ ]    Economic buyer copied: yes/no
Joint outcome (their words): [ ]

LIVE STEPS
| # | Step | Whose | Owner (person) | Date | Done looks like |
| 1 | [ ] | them / us | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
| 2 | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

PAPER (pointer only)
Security / legal / SOW: owner, date. Not the contract.

IF A DATE SLIPS
Gate moves to [date] / we stop / we escalate to [person]

CUT
- Vendor Gantt / fee table / our internal RAID
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Named account, joint outcome in their words, next gate dated.
2. Their confirming owner is a person.
3. At least two live rows are *theirs*. Else it is not a MAP.
4. Owners are people, not functions.
5. ASK is confirm-by date; DRAFT labelled if unconfirmed.
6. Not a SOW. Not our account-plan.
7. Slip rule written.
8. No invented dates they did not agree (draft may propose; must say so).
9. One page.

If 1, 2, 3, or 6 fail: do not ship as a MAP.

## Escalate / stop

- They want us to write the contract → [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md).
- Only our side will act → [Account Plan](../../accounts/account-plan/SKILL.md).
- Angry client this week → [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md); MAP comes after facts.
- Legal holds the gate — name the paper date; do not hide it in "next steps".
