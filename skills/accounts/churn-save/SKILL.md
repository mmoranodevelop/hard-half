# Churn Save

**Churn Save — Leaving Now** — one page: why they are out (their words vs our story), save offer *and* dignified exit, what we will not promise, one ASK this week. Default: venture / professional-services book; same spine for F500 / SaaS CS.

Method origin: Gainsight-public churn craft (diagnose why; run a play or stop hoping) + HBR-public service recovery (act fast, do not over-promise). Operator card, not a CS playbook product.

If they want a save-play lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Named account said they are leaving, or cancellation / non-renew is in writing or in a real conversation
- Champion gone, buyer gone, or a competitor already picked
- Need a yes/no save offer *this week*, or a clean exit
- "Let's discount and see" is the current plan

## When not to use

- Renewal inside 2Q and they have not said out — [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md)
- One price yes/no as the whole job — [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md)
- The talk is the only artifact needed — [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md)
- After they already left — [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md)
- Angry client this week with no leave-signal — [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md)
- Company-wide incident — [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md)
- "No value" is the why as the whole job — [Value Realization](../../accounts/value-realization/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Leave-signal exists | Save-or-exit page + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a "save plan" or panic email | Force why + offer or exit; kill hope |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a GRR lecture | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **They are leaving now** (said, written, or equivalent). If the date is still ≤2Q and no leave-signal, use [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md).
2. **Why, in their words if we have them.** Value / sponsor / delivery / product / price / incumbent. Our story is labelled story, not fact.
3. **Economic buyer named.** A save sold only to the champion is theatre.
4. **Two options on the page: save offer *and* dignified exit.** A page with only "fight" is a fail. Exit terms: date, wind-down, what we hand back, who tells whom.
5. **Save offer is specific:** what they get, what we get, expiry of the offer (≤14 days default). Vague "more love" is a fail.
6. **Discount is not the default save.** If the offer is price, it must go through [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md). Write the pointer. Do not silent-yes.
7. **What we will not promise.** No fake roadmap, no free forever, no "your exec will call" without a named exec who agreed.
8. **One ASK this week.** Buyer meeting / accept exit / approve the offer. Owner, date.
9. **Do not invent a save % or a discount we cannot honour.** Verdict: attempt save / exit / reshape.
10. **One page.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake save.

1. Named account, $, end date, our owner — load-bearing
2. Leave-signal (quote, date, who said it) — load-bearing
3. Save offer we can actually honour, or "none — exit" — load-bearing
4. Why they gave vs why we think
5. Economic buyer, champion status
6. The ASK this week
7. What we will not promise

## QA (must pass)

1. Named account + leave-signal (quote/date/who).
2. Why: their words vs our story, labelled.
3. Economic buyer named or hole that blocks a save.
4. Save offer specific with expiry, **or** explicit none.
5. Dignified exit always written.
6. Discount pointer if price is the offer.
7. What we will not promise.
8. One ASK this week, owner, date.
9. Not renewal-risk (no leave-signal). Not a conversation-prep as the whole job.
10. No invented save % or unhonourable discount. One page.

If 1, 4, 5, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- No account or no leave-signal after one ask → refuse or route [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md).
- Already gone → [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md).
- Only move is off-list price → [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md) plus this page.
- Invented discount we cannot honour → refuse.
- Legal / reputational blow-up → counsel; this page still holds exit terms.
