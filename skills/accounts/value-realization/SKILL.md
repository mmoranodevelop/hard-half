# Value Realization

**Promised-vs-delivered 1-pager** for ONE named account this period: what we promised, evidence we have, the gap, next proof + ASK. Default: professional-services / program book. Same spine for agency or SaaS CS.

Method origin: Gainsight-public value realization — outcomes they bought vs verified evidence; a verified outcome beats a usage chart; do not invent ROI.

If they want an ROI lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Named account this period: did we deliver what we promised
- Renewal or QBR needs evidence, not a product tour
- Sales promised X; delivery has Y; someone must write the gap
- "We're creating lots of value" with no number and no proxy

## When not to use

- Quarterly sitting **with** the customer — [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md) (this page is an exhibit)
- Whether **one more unit** makes us money — [Unit Economics](../../strategy/unit-economics/SKILL.md)
- 12-month plan — [Account Plan](../../accounts/account-plan/SKILL.md)
- This month's health flags — [Account Health](../../accounts/account-health/SKILL.md)
- Capital / NPV for a new bet — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)
- Vendor acceptance of *our* supplier — [Vendor Scorecard](../../management/vendor-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- If the gap already lost the deal — [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md)

If they will not name the account *and* at least one promise, stop.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Promised-vs-delivered 1-pager + next-proof ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a "ROI" or value slide | Same claims; force evidence or hole; kill invented ROI |
| **refuse** | No account or no promise, or a lecture on ROI | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Promises from a source.** SOW, proposal, QBR last time, sales email. Quote or cite. "Everyone knows" is not a promise.
2. **Each promise: promised → evidence → gap.** Evidence is a date and an artefact. Usage ≠ outcome unless they named usage as the outcome.
3. **Do not invent ROI or $ value.** If unmeasured: `[not measured — agree proxy by DATE]`.
4. **Period named.** This quarter / this SOW / since go-live. Not "since the beginning of time".
5. **Gap owner.** Ours vs theirs vs joint. Blame without owner is a fail.
6. **Next proof is one thing**, dated. A dashboard they will ignore is not proof.
7. **One ASK.** Agree the proxy / accept the evidence / close the gap / stop claiming the unmeasured line. Owner, date. Economic buyer is the audience of the proof.

## Intake

Refuse a FOR-SENDING page if the account *and* the promises (or "none written — that is the finding") are both missing.

1. Account, period, our owner — load-bearing
2. Promises and where they were written — load-bearing
3. Evidence we actually have
4. Gaps we already know
5. Next proof we can produce
6. The ASK (agree proxy / accept / close / stop claiming)

## QA (must pass)

1. Named account, period, source of promises (or "none written").
2. Promises in their words, or "that's all that was written".
3. Evidence is artefact + date, or hole. No invented ROI.
4. Gap has whose (us / them / joint).
5. Next proof is one dated artefact.
6. One ASK, owner, date.
7. Verdict matches the table (not a happy average).
8. Not a QBR sitting pack. Not unit-economics of our unit.
9. Economic buyer named or holed as audience.
10. One page.

If 1, 3, 6, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want this *as* the QBR → [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md); attach this page.
- They want a $ ROI with no data → refuse the number; ship holes + proxy ASK.
- Gap is "they are leaving" → [Churn Save](../../accounts/churn-save/SKILL.md).
- Gap is our unit does not make money → [Unit Economics](../../strategy/unit-economics/SKILL.md).
- Counsel / audit needs a formal attestation — we provide the table, they write the opinion.
