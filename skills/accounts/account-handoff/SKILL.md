# Account Handoff

**Account Handoff — Named-Account Transfer** — one page: outcomes they bought, live promises, people, risks, intro plan, what must be true before the receiving owner accepts. Default: venture / professional-services book; same spine for F500. Skins: sales→AM, AM→AM, AM→CS.

Method origin: Gainsight-public sales-to-CS handoff — capture outcomes they bought, stakeholders, risks, then a live intro; knowledge does not live in a hallway.

If they want a handoff lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Named account must change owner (sales→AM, AM→AM, AM→CS, AM→ delivery lead)
- Incoming owner has CRM notes and a smile, not the promises
- Kickoff is booked and the giving owner has not written what was sold
- Coverage gap (parental, exit, reorg) with a live client

## When not to use

- First 90 days of a person in a role — [Ninety-Day Onboarding](../../learning/ninety-day-onboarding/SKILL.md)
- Who reports where — [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- 12-month plan after the transfer — [Account Plan](../../accounts/account-plan/SKILL.md)
- Joint dated plan with the customer — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- Integrity list of promises — [Commitment Register](../../productivity/commitment-register/SKILL.md)
- Account is mid-escalation — [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md) first
- Promises look like a SOW rewrite — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Account + both owners | Handoff page + accept ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a CRM dump or "transition deck" | Force promises, risks, intro; kill the dump |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a lecture | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Named account, giving owner, receiving owner, baton date.** Missing the receiving owner is not a handoff.
2. **Outcomes they bought, in their words.** If sales cannot say them, the handoff is not ready — hole, do not invent.
3. **Promises live on the page.** Commercial, delivery, roadmap. Pull [Commitment Register](../../productivity/commitment-register/SKILL.md) if it exists. Unwritten promises are a risk.
4. **People:** economic buyer, champion, day-to-day, blockers. Last contact dates.
5. **Risks with owner.** Delivery miss, single-thread, never-expires discount, angry thread.
6. **Intro plan:** joint meeting with the customer, date, who speaks. Silent swap (signature change only) is a fail unless the client forbade a meeting — write that.
7. **Receiving owner can refuse.** ASK is they accept by date, or list the holes that block accept.
8. **Not a 90-day person plan.** The receiving AM's ramp is [Ninety-Day Onboarding](../../learning/ninety-day-onboarding/SKILL.md).
9. **SaaS CS skin:** success plan + usage baseline instead of SOW burn. Spine stays.
10. **Do not invent promises or last-contact dates.** One page.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake baton.

1. Named account, $ / term, baton date — load-bearing
2. Giving owner and receiving owner — load-bearing
3. Outcomes they bought and live promises — load-bearing
4. Buyer / champion / day-to-day
5. Known risks and open issues
6. Intro meeting (date or "client forbade — write why")
7. The ASK (receiving owner accepts / blocked by holes)

## QA (must pass)

1. Named account, giving + receiving owners, baton date.
2. Outcomes they bought, or explicit "unknown — not ready".
3. Live promises listed or "none written — risk".
4. Buyer and champion named or holed.
5. Intro dated or forbidden-with-reason.
6. ASK is accept/refuse by date.
7. Not a 90-day person plan, not an org chart.
8. Risks with owners.
9. No invented promises.
10. One page.

If 1, 2, 6, or 9 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Account unnamed or receiving owner unnamed after one ask → refuse.
- Receiving owner is a new hire whose *role* needs a 90-day → [Ninety-Day Onboarding](../../learning/ninety-day-onboarding/SKILL.md) in parallel; this page is still the account baton.
- Account is on fire → [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md) first; do not swap owners in the fire without a line on the page.
- Silent swap with no intro and no client forbid-reason → refuse.
