# Account Plan

**Account Plan — 12 Months, One Named Account** — one page: outcomes they hired us for, 1–3 whitespace cells we will chase, will-nots, risks, next-30-day ASK. Default: venture / professional-services book; same spine for F500 / SaaS CS.

Method origin: Salesforce/GSP-public account plan (goals, kick-start, named stakeholders) + MEDDPICC letters as flags (buyer, champion, competition) + Challenger public (the next conversation teaches). Not a methodology dump.

If they want a MEDDPICC / account-planning lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Named account needs a 12-month plan an MD / AM can fly
- Kickoff after close, annual reset, or new AM taking the book
- Rewrite a 20-slide "account strategy" into one page + ASK

## When not to use

- Heatmap of all accounts this AM owns — [Book of Business](../../accounts/book-of-business/SKILL.md)
- Quarterly sitting with the customer — [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md)
- Joint dated plan they co-own — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- Where else we could sell, as the whole job — [Expansion Whitespace](../../accounts/expansion-whitespace/SKILL.md)
- Top-N firm revenue risk — [Customer Concentration](../../strategy/customer-concentration/SKILL.md)
- This month's health flags — [Account Health](../../accounts/account-health/SKILL.md)
- Buyer map is the problem — [Stakeholder Alignment](../../management/stakeholder-alignment/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named account | 12-month page + next ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted an account-strategy deck | Outcomes first; kill the catalog |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a lecture | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One named account.** Not a territory tour.
2. **Their outcomes**, 3–5, in their language. Not our utilisation vanity.
3. **Economic buyer and champion named**, or holed. Single-threaded = a risk on page 1.
4. **Whitespace is 1–3 cells we will chase this year**, not a product matrix dump. Rest is "will not chase" or hole.
5. **Risks with owner and date.** Champion leaving, delivery miss, competitive incumbent, commercial cliff.
6. **Next ASK is the next 30 days**, not a 12-month slogan. Owner, date. The year is the frame; the ASK is now.
7. **Do not invent pipeline $ or ROI.** Hole: `[not measured — proxy by DATE]`.
8. **Capacity this AM can actually spend** on this account (days/month). A plan that needs 8 days when the AM has 2 is a fail.
9. **SaaS CS skin:** swap delivery burn for product usage. Spine stays.
10. **One page.** Verdict: grow / hold / fix / exit — because.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake year-plan.

1. Named account, economic buyer, champion, our owner — load-bearing
2. Outcomes they hired us for (or "unknown — first task") — load-bearing
3. The ASK we need in the next 30 days — load-bearing
4. Contracted $ / term / renewal date
5. What we will chase this year (1–3 cells) and what we will not
6. Known risks
7. Days/month this AM can spend here

## QA (must pass)

1. Named account, our owner, 12-month frame dated.
2. Economic buyer and champion named or holed; single-thread flagged.
3. 3–5 *their* outcomes, not our vanity.
4. ≤3 whitespace cells with next conversation, or explicit "none this year".
5. Risks with owners and dates.
6. One ASK in the next 30 days, owner, date.
7. AM days/month stated; plan fits capacity or flags the clash.
8. No invented ROI / pipeline.
9. Not a book heatmap, not a QBR sitting pack, not a MAP.
10. One page.

If 1, 3, 6, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They will not name the account after one ask → refuse.
- They want the whole book ranked → [Book of Business](../../accounts/book-of-business/SKILL.md).
- Renewal inside 2 quarters is the live issue → [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md).
- Account is leaving this month → [Churn Save](../../accounts/churn-save/SKILL.md).
- Catalog tour with no outcomes → refuse; issues list.
