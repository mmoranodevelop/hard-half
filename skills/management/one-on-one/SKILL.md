# One-on-One

**This-week 1:1 card** — their outline, one direct challenge, actions with owner and date, next meeting on the calendar. Default: venture MD with a direct report. F500: same card; career/comp stay with the line manager; works-council/HR as a constraint line, not a lecture.

Method origin: Grove 1:1 (public) — their meeting, cadence by task-relevant maturity. Kim Scott public: care personally and challenge directly.

If they want a lecture on Grove or Radical Candor: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- This week's 1:1 with a named direct report or workstream lead
- Cadence is broken (cancelled, 15-minute status, manager hijack)
- You owe them a challenge and have not named it
- TRM on the current work has changed (new task, crisis, veteran)

## When not to use

- A named D must decide in a room — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- Team / org status — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- The hard talk that is not the cadence — [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Someone you do not 1:1 — [Stakeholder Alignment](../../management/stakeholder-alignment/SKILL.md)
- Conversation two layers down — [Skip-Level](../../management/skip-level/SKILL.md)
- Annual/cycle performance packet or PIP legal text — HR owns it; this card may still prep the human hour
- Therapy, investigation, or legal advice — stop

If they want you to fill *their* 1:1 with *your* task list: put your items last, timed. Do not hijack.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | This-week card (plus cadence row if they are resetting rhythm) |
| **redline** | They pasted notes or a manager-led list | Restore their agenda; name status-theatre or missing challenge |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing answers missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **It is their meeting.** Their outline first. Yours last and timed.
2. **One direct challenge.** Specific. Care + direct. If it is not yet earned, say so on the card.
3. **Cadence follows this task, not their grade.** New-to-this-work → weekly. Veteran on stable scope → less often. Crisis / reorg / new task → weekly.
4. **Do not cancel; reschedule inside the week.**
5. **PM is not their boss.** Career/comp belong to the line manager.
6. **Status dump belongs in operating review**, not this hour.
7. **Actions have one human and a calendar date.**
8. **Do not invent what they would put on the agenda.** Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **who** and **MD-line vs PM-workstream** are both missing: refuse.

1. MD-line or PM-workstream — load-bearing
2. Who — name, role, current work — load-bearing
3. TRM on that work — low / medium / high (or "unknown")
4. This meeting date and current cadence (honest)
5. Their last outline or last actions — paste or "none"
6. The challenge you owe them — or "I don't know yet"

## Output shape

```
1:1 CARD  |  [name]  |  [date]
Relationship: MD-line | PM-workstream
Current work: [ ]    TRM on this work: low | medium | high
Cadence: weekly | 2 weeks | 3–4 weeks     Length: 45–60 min
Next 1:1: [date] (do not cancel; reschedule inside the week)

SINCE LAST TIME — closed / not:
- [action] — [done | slip — new date]

THEIRS (most of the hour, their order)

- One thing they would not put in Slack: [ ]

YOURS (last 10 minutes, timed)
- Context they are missing: [ ]
- One challenge (specific, care + direct): [ ]
- One ask, if any: [what] by [date]

PM only — not their boss. Career/comp belong to [line manager]. Carry to the D: [ ]

ACTIONS TO READ BACK
| What | Who | Date |
| [ ] | [name] | [date] |

ASK: [Name] [does X] by [date]. Manager [does Y] by [date]. Next 1:1: [date].
Holes: [unknown — need …]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK, named person, next date.
2. Agenda is theirs, not the manager's 12-item list.
3. Direct challenge present, or a stated reason it is not yet earned.
4. Length is a real hour, not a 15-minute status slot.
5. PM does not impersonate their line manager.
6. Not a status dump the operating review already covers.
7. Not a Radical Candor lecture with no card.

If 1, 2, or 3 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Named D belongs in a room → [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md).
- Hard talk that is not the cadence → [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md).
- PIP / legal packet → HR; this card may still prep the human hour.
- Therapy / investigation → stop.
