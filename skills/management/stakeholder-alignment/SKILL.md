# Stakeholder Alignment

**Named-person map + belief gaps + 10-day conversation plan** — the map is diagnostic; the product is belief that produces action. Default: venture program; same spine for F500.

Method origin: power × interest grid (Johnson/Scholes public) + influence as joint problem-solving.

If they want a Mendelow lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- The work needs people who do **not** report to you
- Silence from a key player is a risk before a gate, decision meeting, or go-live
- A stakeholder "plan" is a 2x2 and a distribution list

## When not to use

- Who has the **D** — [Decision Rights](../../management/decision-rights/SKILL.md)
- The **room** that takes the call — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- Who does the **work** on a task — [RACI Delivery](../../project/raci-delivery/SKILL.md)
- Users doing the new behaviour — [Change Adoption](../../management/change-adoption/SKILL.md)
- Status dump to a steering group — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- Purpose, scope, success test — [Program Charter](../../management/program-charter/SKILL.md)
- Living risk register — [RAID Register](../../management/raid-register/SKILL.md)
- The stakeholder *is* the board paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Map + belief gaps + 10-day conversations + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a 2x2, RACI, or comms plan | Same humans; infer beliefs; add asks |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or "socialize" with no ask | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Shippable outcome, one sentence.** If you cannot say what "aligned" unlocks, you are networking.
2. **Named humans, not functions.** "Finance" is not a stakeholder. Quiet vetoes count.
3. **Power × interest allocates effort:** key player / keep satisfied / keep informed / monitor. Silence is a position. Do not invent power.
4. **Belief gaps** for every key player and keep-satisfied: currently believes / must believe / evidence that would change their mind / their currency / stance.
5. **Conversation plan:** who talks to whom, one ASK, date, observable yes, if-no. Sequence keep-satisfied before they are surprised.
6. **Cut the 40-person list.** Influence is diagnose-and-exchange, not a broadcast.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake matrix.

1. The outcome alignment unlocks (decision, resource, date) — load-bearing
2. Named people who can advance or block — load-bearing
3. What they have already said (yes / no / silence)
4. Action-forcing event — gate, board, go-live — load-bearing
5. Currencies you actually have (do not invent budget, access, or credit)
6. Who can have the conversation (often not you)

## QA (must pass)

1. Shippable outcome sentence.
2. Named humans, not functions.
3. Each key player / keep-satisfied has current belief, must-believe, and a conversation.
4. Each conversation has ASK, owner, date, observable yes.
5. The 2x2 is not the deliverable.
6. Not a RACI or RAPID map in costume.
7. List is short enough to act on in 10 days.
8. ASK + owner + date. No invented power.

If 1, 2, 4, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Unknown veto (Legal, regulator, works council, named customer) → find the human; do not decorate.
- Alignment as consensus of everyone → refuse; the people who can stop it are the job.
- "Socialize" with no ask → refuse the verb.
- Coercion / hidden agenda → stop.
- The D is unnamed → [Decision Rights](../../management/decision-rights/SKILL.md) first.
