# Hiring Scorecard

**Role scorecard** — one page the hiring D signs before sourcing: mission, 3–5 dated outcomes, must vs nice, walk-aways, evidence slots. Loop and debrief are the same card, extra tables, not a different method. Default: venture hire (MD's direct report or a workstream lead). F500: same card; HR/legal house rules as a constraint line, not a culture-fit essay.

Method origin: ghSMART Who scorecard (public) + Google re:Work structured interviewing (public).

If they want a structured-interviewing lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Opening a role before sourcing
- JD is a wish list (titles, slogans, no dated outcomes)
- Debrief with no scores and no named D

## When not to use

- Performance of someone already in seat — reuse the card; this skill does not run the review. After they start: [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md)
- Employer-brand copy or offer script — not this artifact; [Offer Conversation](../../writing/offer-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Legal call (discrimination, disability, immigration) — HR / counsel
- Brainteaser / beer-test as the method — refuse
- Role exists to run a program you cannot describe as outcomes — [Program Charter](../../management/program-charter/SKILL.md) first
- Debrief that is actually a decision meeting with no card — build the card, then [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Signed-ready scorecard + ASK; add loop/debrief tables if they asked |
| **redline** | They pasted a JD, loop, or debrief | Same card; split must/nice; kill vibe |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing answers missing, or beer-test as method | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Mission is a sentence**, not a title.
2. **3–5 dated 12-month outcomes**, observable, necessary and sufficient.
3. **Must vs nice** with evidence slots. Must = walk-away. Nice cannot rescue a must-fail.
4. **Named hiring D.** Panel chemistry is not the D.
5. **Same questions, score before talk.** Interviewers who have not scored do not speak.
6. **No protected characteristics** on the card.
7. **Do not invent bands or scores.** Constraints (location, band, timeline) are facts, not fake musts. Same-day minimum: mission + 3 outcomes + 4 musts + D.

## Intake

If **mission** and **12-month outcomes** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake card.

1. Role, one-sentence mission — load-bearing
2. 12-month outcomes they actually need — not a wish list — load-bearing
3. Why now / what failed last search
4. Must vs nice — or "help me split"
5. Who has the D
6. Constraints — location, band, timeline, internal-only — as facts
7. HR/legal house rules you must not violate — or "none stated"

Do not staff a loop with no scorecard.

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK + named D + date.
2. Mission is a sentence, not a title.
3. Outcomes are dated and observable — not slogans ("drive transformation").
4. Not everything is a must, and not nothing is.
5. Each must has an evidence slot.
6. No protected characteristics.
7. Panel chemistry is not the D.
8. Not a JD essay / culture-fit paragraph with no table. No invented bands or scores.

If 1, 2, 3, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Legal call → HR / counsel.
- Beer-test → refuse.
- Undescribed program role → [Program Charter](../../management/program-charter/SKILL.md).
- Debrief-as-meeting → card then [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md).
