# Succession Slate

**Critical-role succession one-pager** — role mission, ready-now, ready-1–2y, emergency cover (name + first 30 days), development moves, one ASK. Default: CEO/CHRO/board nominating for a named critical seat; same spine for BU-critical roles below C-suite.

Method origin: Spencer Stuart CHRO-as-architect succession practice + McKinsey structured succession (ongoing slate, internal/external benchmark, unthinkable emergency cover) + CHRO/board public succession process guidance.

If they want a succession-theory lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Board or CEO asks "who succeeds X if X leaves tomorrow / in 18 months?"
- Critical role has no named emergency cover
- Annual/semiannual succession review needs a decision pack, not a 40-name binder
- Development moves (rotation/stretch) must be approved to create ready-1–2y depth

## When not to use

- 90-day post-close flight watch — [Flight Risk 90](../../ma/flight-risk-90/SKILL.md)
- Deal retain / walk-away / stay bonus — [Key Talent Slate](../../ma/key-talent-slate/SKILL.md)
- Opening a role scorecard before sourcing — [Hiring Scorecard](../../management/hiring-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- FTE plan / freeze — [Headcount Plan](../../management/headcount-plan/SKILL.md)
- Closing a candidate offer — [Offer Conversation](../../writing/offer-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Performance PIP of the sitting incumbent — stop; manager + HR process

This page is **pipeline + emergency cover for a named critical role**, not retention theatre.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Role named + assessors | One-pager + one ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted an org chart labelled "succession" | Rebuild three buckets; kill title-only slates |
| **refuse** | Role not critical/named, or "list everyone Level 5" | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One critical role per page** (or tightly coupled pair). Mission of the seat in one sentence.
2. **Three buckets only:** Ready-now | Ready-1–2y | Emergency cover. Empty bucket = hole, not "TBD talent."
3. **Names over titles.** Anonymous "VP Finance bench" is not a slate.
4. **Evidence of readiness.** Outcomes/experiences that justify the bucket — not vibes or loyalty.
5. **Emergency cover ≠ successor.** Cover = who runs the seat for 30–90 days; may be different human.
6. **External benchmark when internal thin.** Say "external map needed" — do not invent candidate names you have not sourced.
7. **Development move on the page** for ready-1–2y (rotation, stretch, exposure) with owner/date.
8. **One ASK** — approve slate / name emergency cover / authorize development move / open confidential external search.
9. **Never invent readiness ratings or flight probabilities.** Holes stay holes.
10. **Confidentiality.** Distribution list on page 1; do not broad-cast a living CEO succession draft.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake slate.

1. Critical role + why critical — load-bearing
2. Time horizon (emergency / 12–24m / planned exit) — load-bearing
3. Assessor set (CEO, CHRO, board committee) — load-bearing
4. Known internal candidates or "none named" — load-bearing
5. Prior slate date / gaps from last review
6. Constraints (non-compete geography, dual-hat risk, diversity goals as constraints not essays)
7. The decision this pack unlocks

## QA (must pass)

1. One critical role; mission stated.
2. Three buckets present; empty = labelled hole.
3. Names (or explicit none) — no title-only theatre.
4. Emergency cover named or hole.
5. One ASK with owner, verb, date.
6. No invented readiness scores.
7. One page; confidential distribution noted.

If 1, 2, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Role not actually critical after one challenge → refuse / re-scope.
- Demand to invent ready-now names → refuse.
- Sitting CEO succession without board ownership → escalate to nominating/governance.
- Naming someone who does not know they are on the slate in a leaky culture → CHRO controls distribution.
- Discrimination / illegal criteria in "fit" language → stop; counsel + CHRO.
