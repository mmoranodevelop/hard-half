# AAR agenda — [event]
Date of event: [ ]    Date of session: [ ]
Facilitator: [ ]    Participants (who were there): [ ]
Formal | informal     Next event these actions must change: [ ]

Opening (say it): This is a professional discussion of the work. We are here to improve the next one. We are not here to rank people. Disagreement is expected.

Timed:
1. Rules and purpose (2 min)
2. What was supposed to happen — owner restates intent; leads restate their piece (5–10)
3. What actually happened — facts, chronological or by theme (bulk)
4. Why — went well / did not, with causes (bulk)
5. What we will do — owner, date, next event (10–15). Protect this block.
6. Close — read back 2–5 actions

Facilitator asks; does not brief the answers. Senior person does not speak first on "what happened" or "why."
