# Skip-Level

**One Skip-Level Conversation Pack** — purpose in one sentence, 3–5 questions sent ahead, what you will not do with what you hear, close. Default: MD with someone who reports to a direct. Same spine for a program lead talking to a workstream IC (no line power: say so).

Method origin: SHRM public (not a surprise inspection) + Culture Amp / Manager Tools public (do not replace their manager).

If they want a skip-level lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Calendar has a skip-level (or you should have one) with a named person
- You are two layers up and only hear filtered status
- After a reorg, a new manager, or a team that "feels off"
- A program lead who is not the line boss needs a private channel with an IC

## When not to use

- Recurring 1:1 with a direct — [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md)
- Performance, conflict, a no you already owe — [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Team operating review — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- Org boxes — [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Secret investigation, harassment, legal hold — stop; HR/counsel
- A skip-level town hall (many people) — this skill is one conversation

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named person | Purpose + questions + will-not-do + close + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted an agenda | Kill status-theatre, manager-rating, ambush |
| **refuse** | Secret from the skipped manager, or a PIP here | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Tell the skipped manager first.** If you cannot tell them, this is an investigation — not this skill.
2. **Tell the employee the purpose in the invite.** Not a review. Not a trap. 3–5 questions sent ahead.
3. **Purpose is one sentence on the page.** "Check up on their manager" is not a purpose you write down and then deny.
4. **Will-not-do is load-bearing.** No quotes to their manager. No work around their manager. Not their review. Themes, not attributed quotes, if anything goes back.
5. **Questions are open and non-evaluative of the person.** Do not run "rate your manager".
6. **You do not become their boss for an hour.** Program-lead variant: say you have no line power.
7. **Do not invent their issues.** If they have not spoken yet, the pack is questions + will-not-do, not a fake diagnosis.

## Intake

If **who** and **their manager** are both missing after one round: issues list.

1. Who (name, role) and their manager (the person you are skipping) — load-bearing
2. Have you told the manager this is happening? — load-bearing
3. Purpose in one sentence (or "I don't know yet")
4. Trigger (cadence / new manager / smell / program)
5. Anything you must not open (legal, HR, a live PIP)
6. Line power: MD-line or PM-no-line
7. Date of the conversation

## QA (must pass)

1. Named person and named skipped manager.
2. Manager has been told, or the page says DO NOT RUN.
3. Purpose is one sentence, not "catch up".
4. Will-not-do block is specific.
5. 3–5 questions, none a manager rating scale.
6. Not a 1:1 agenda for a direct. Not a PIP / investigation script.
7. PM variant does not impersonate their boss.
8. One page.

If 2, 4, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Secret from the manager → refuse the skip-level. Safety/HR issue → HR/counsel.
- Write the employee's review from this hour → refuse.
- Fix the manager by quoting the skip → refuse quotes; themes in the manager's 1:1 or [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md).
- Harassment, legal, crisis in the room → stop; HR/EA.
- The real job is their recurring 1:1 → [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md).
