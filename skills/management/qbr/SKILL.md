# QBR

**Quarterly Business Review — One Named Counterpart** — one page: last-quarter outcomes vs goals we set, risks on page 1, next-90-day ASK. Default: B2B customer sitting; same spine for one named internal sponsor of a program. Not the company MOR.

Method origin: Gainsight-public QBR (outcomes vs last goals, next 90 days) + buyer-in-the-room craft.

If they want a QBR lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Quarterly sitting with a named customer (economic buyer + user lead)
- Quarterly sitting with a named internal sponsor of one program
- Rewrite a 30-slide "value" tour into one page + ask

## When not to use

- Internal weekly/monthly ops of our P&L — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- This week's Commit — [Forecast Call](../../commercial/forecast-call/SKILL.md)
- Why we lost the deal — [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md)
- One renewal's save plan — [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md)
- Top-N revenue risk across customers — [Customer Concentration](../../strategy/customer-concentration/SKILL.md)
- Board paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- This month's three flags with no sitting — [Account Health](../../accounts/account-health/SKILL.md)

If they will not name the counterpart, stop.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | QBR page + next-quarter ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a QBR deck | Outcomes first; kill the product tour |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a region tour | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One named account or sponsor.** Not a region tour.
2. **Outcomes they named last time**, not our vanity usage. Actual vs goal. First QBR: say so.
3. **Risks on page 1.** A risk saved for renewal is a fail.
4. **One ASK for next quarter.** Their action and ours, owners, dates. Economic buyer in the room or the ASK is invalid.
5. **Pre-read ≥48h ahead.** The sitting is decisions, not a readout.
6. **Not a product roadmap dump.** Only what serves their next-quarter goals.
7. **Do not invent ROI.** Hole: value not measured.
8. **Health colour with because**, not a mystery score.

## Intake

If **account** and **last quarter's goals** are both missing after one round: issues list.

1. Account / sponsor, economic buyer, sitting date — load-bearing
2. Goals we set last QBR (or "first QBR — no baseline") — load-bearing
3. Actuals we actually have
4. Known risks (renewal, adoption, champion leaving)
5. The ask we want from them next quarter
6. Our owner of the account

## QA (must pass)

1. Named counterpart and sitting date.
2. Scorecard is their outcomes vs last goals (or explicit first QBR).
3. Risks on page 1 with owners.
4. One ASK, owner, date.
5. Economic buyer in the room or ASK flagged invalid.
6. No invented ROI.
7. Not an internal operating review.
8. One page.

If 1, 2, 4, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Weekly internal MOR labelled QBR → [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md).
- Renewal already lost → [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md).
- Champion gone and no economic buyer → do not run a user-only sitting as if it were executive.
- Named save plan is the job → [Renewal Risk](../../accounts/renewal-risk/SKILL.md).
