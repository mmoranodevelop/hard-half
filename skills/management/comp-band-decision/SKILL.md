# Comp Band Decision

**Comp band decision one-pager** — role/level, band (min–mid–max), proposed total cash + equity, market anchor, exception (Y/N why), fairness check vs peers, approve/deny ASK. Default: hiring manager + People/Comp for one named decision; same spine for promo in-band or off-band.

Method origin: Radford/Korn Ferry and Levels.fyi public compensation-philosophy concepts (bands, leveling, total rewards triangulation) + SHRM pay-equity practice (audit peers controlling for level/tenure/performance — not vibe fairness).

If they want a comp-philosophy lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- One hire or promo needs approve / deny / exception on pay
- Proposed number sits at top of band or off-band
- Peer equity risk ("same job, different pay") is the live issue
- Comp committee / CFO exception path needs a one-pager

## When not to use

- 12–18 month FTE plan — [Headcount Plan](../../management/headcount-plan/SKILL.md)
- Live offer call script — [Offer Conversation](../../writing/offer-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Can they do the job / musts — [Hiring Scorecard](../../management/hiring-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- Critical-role succession pipeline — [Succession Slate](../../management/succession-slate/SKILL.md)
- Company-wide salary structure redesign — stop; Comp/People project
- Legal pay-equity litigation defense — stop; counsel

This page is **one compensation decision**, not the conversation with the candidate.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named person/role + band | One-pager + one ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted "pay them whatever" | Rebuild band/market/peers; kill invented percentiles |
| **refuse** | No band, no market source, or "make it fair" with no peers | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One decision.** One named hire or promo (or clear role opening with finalist). Not a team raise pool.
2. **Band on the page.** Min–mid–max for the level; proposed placement (% of mid or position in band).
3. **Market anchor sourced.** Radford / Levels / other survey / offer intel — name the source and as-of. No invented "market is $X."
4. **Total rewards.** Base + variable + equity (grant value basis stated) + material premiums. Compare apples to apples.
5. **Exception explicit.** In-band / top-of-band / off-band. Off-band needs why (scarcity, counter, critical skill) and expiry/review date.
6. **Fairness check.** Named peers same level/scope; deltas explained by tenure/perf/geo/skill — or flagged. Do not invent protected-class analysis here; escalate if pattern risk.
7. **One ASK** — approve as proposed / approve with cap / deny / approve exception with review date.
8. **Never invent percentiles, survey medians, or peer salaries.** Unknown peer pay = hole.
9. **Budget line exists.** Headcount/comp budget owner noted; this page does not create FTE.
10. **One page.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake approve.

1. Role / level / hire vs promo — load-bearing
2. Current band (min–mid–max) — load-bearing
3. Proposed package — load-bearing
4. Market source (or "none — cannot decide") — load-bearing
5. Peer set for fairness check
6. Exception rationale if above mid/top
7. Approver (People/Comp/CFO/committee) and deadline

## QA (must pass)

1. One named decision; band min–mid–max present.
2. Market source named or hole — no invented percentiles.
3. Exception labelled if above mid/top/off-band.
4. Fairness peers table or explicit "no peers / hole."
5. One ASK approve/deny/cap/exception.
6. No invented peer salaries or survey numbers.
7. One page.

If 1, 2, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- No band and no market source after one ask → refuse.
- Demand to invent a percentile → refuse.
- Pattern of like-for-like pay gaps by protected class → People + counsel; do not "fix" on this page alone.
- Off-band without budget owner → refuse bind.
- Executive / proxy-disclosed role → comp committee process.
