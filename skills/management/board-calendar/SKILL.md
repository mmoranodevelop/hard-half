# Board Calendar

**Annual board rhythm — one page** — meeting dates, consent vs discuss split, pack owner, send deadline, dead zones (no-send / blackout). Default: private-company board (4–6 sittings); same spine for F500 with committee cycle mapped.

Method origin: NACD private-company board calendar + consent-agenda practice (Diligent/BoardPro public); Deloitte/PwC board-effectiveness public on agenda design and pre-reads that free discussion time.

If they want a governance lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- "What does the board cover this year, and when?"
- Consent vs discuss is muddy; every item eats live time
- Pack arrives late; directors cannot prepare
- New fiscal year / new chair / first PE-backed board calendar
- Dead zones (holidays, audit lock, fundraise quiet) not marked

## When not to use

- The decision paper itself — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- Spoken delivery in the room — [Board Spoken](../../comms/board-spoken/SKILL.md)
- Hostile Q&A rehearsal — [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md)
- New director 30-day kit — [Director Onboarding](../../management/director-onboarding/SKILL.md)
- Monthly sponsor flash — [PE Monthly Flash](../../finance/pe-monthly-flash/SKILL.md)
- Weekly ops KPI sitting — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)

This page is the **year rhythm**, not one memo and not one meeting script.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Year + cadence known or proposed | Annual calendar one-pager + one ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a date list or bloated agenda | Force consent/discuss; name pack owner; mark dead zones |
| **refuse** | No fiscal year, no chair/CEO owner, or "fill every slot with strategy" | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One governance year.** Dates or month+week; not a perpetual "TBD."
2. **Every sitting has a primary purpose** tied to fiscal / audit / strategy / talent cycle — not four identical "updates."
3. **Consent vs discuss is explicit.** Consent = routine, non-controversial, pull-rights intact. Discuss = decision or oversight that needs airtime.
4. **Pack owner named** (CorpSec / GC / CFO) and **send deadline** (≥5–7 calendar days before sitting, or hole).
5. **Dead zones marked:** holiday blackout, audit lock, quiet period, fundraise — no pack send / no sitting unless exception owned.
6. **Committee → full board handoff** dated when material (audit, rem, nom/gov).
7. **One ASK** — adopt calendar / move sitting / change pack owner / clear dead zone. Owner, date.
8. **Do not invent legal quorum rules or listing requirements.** Hole: `[counsel confirm by DATE]`.
9. **Not a memo, not a spoken script, not Q&A prep.**
10. **One page.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake year.

1. Governance year (start/end) and entity — load-bearing
2. Cadence (n sittings) or "propose private 4–6" — load-bearing
3. Chair + pack owner (CorpSec/GC/CFO) — load-bearing
4. Fiscal / audit / AGM / budget milestones
5. Known dead zones (holiday, quiet period, fundraise)
6. Committees that must report up this year
7. The ASK (adopt / shift / resource)

## QA (must pass)

1. Year and entity named; sitting count or proposed cadence.
2. Each sitting has a primary purpose (not four clones).
3. Consent vs discuss split present; pull-right stated.
4. Pack owner and send deadline named (or hole).
5. Dead zones marked or explicitly "none known."
6. One ASK with owner and date.
7. No invented listing / quorum law.
8. Not a memo, not a spoken script, not Q&A.
9. One page.

If 1, 3, 4, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want the decision paper written here → [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md).
- They want the room script / hostile Q → [Board Spoken](../../comms/board-spoken/SKILL.md) / [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md).
- No chair or pack owner after one ask → refuse.
- Counsel flags listing / open-meeting constraints → pack is exhibit; GC owns; do not invent rules.
- Calendar used to bury a material decision in consent → refuse the theatre.
