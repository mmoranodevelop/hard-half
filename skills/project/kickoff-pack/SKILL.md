# Kickoff Pack

**One-page kickoff pack** for the first sitting with **client + delivery team**. Confirms the signed charter. Starts the work. Does not re-authorise. Default: venture / program project. Same spine for F500.

Method origin: public kickoff craft (charter first, then room: purpose, fence, roles pointer, next 2 weeks). Not a training deck.

If they want to explain a kickoff / teach a workshop: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- First session with the named client and the named team
- Charter is signed (or will be signed *before* the room)
- Need purpose, in/out, who does what (pointer), next 14 days, one ASK to start

## When not to use

- Charter not signed / no outcome / no D — [Project Charter](../../project/project-charter/SKILL.md) first
- Program, not one project — [Program Charter](../../management/program-charter/SKILL.md)
- Full deliverable RACI (the matrix itself) — [RACI Delivery](../../project/raci-delivery/SKILL.md)
- Who has the D on a stuck call — [Decision Rights](../../management/decision-rights/SKILL.md)
- Who believes what / who talks — [Stakeholder Alignment](../../management/stakeholder-alignment/SKILL.md)
- Training users on the new behaviour — [Change Adoption](../../management/change-adoption/SKILL.md)
- First 100 days after go-live — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- Fence change after start — [Change Control](../../project/change-control/SKILL.md)

Kickoff ≠ charter ≠ training. Charter = signed authority. Kickoff = start work.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Session this week | One-pager + 60-min agenda |
| **redline** | They pasted a 40-slide "kickoff deck" | Same facts; purpose, fence, 2 weeks, ASK; list slides you killed |
| **refuse** | No charter, no named client, or they want a training day | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Charter exists.** Kickoff to "get alignment then we'll write the charter" is a workshop — refuse.
2. **Purpose and fence are read from the charter**, not reinvented in the room.
3. **RACI is a pointer** (who does the first named deliverables). Do not paste a 40-row wallpaper.
4. **Next 2 weeks: dated actions, each with a named owner.** "Mobilise" is not an action.
5. **One ASK** the sponsor or client D can close in the room: access, named counterpart, start date.
6. **Cut** icebreakers, training modules, vendor capability slides, three-year Gantt.
7. **Do not invent names, dates, or a signed charter.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 6 are missing after one ask: issues list, not a fake pack.

1. Charter signed? If no: stop and route. Quote outcome / in / out / D / dates / envelope if yes — load-bearing
2. Who is in the room — client names + delivery names — load-bearing
3. Purpose in one sentence (from the charter)
4. In / out that might still be fuzzy
5. RACI — pointer to a named matrix, or "none yet — produce a stub"
6. Next 2 weeks — what must be true, owners — load-bearing
7. ASK to start — access, names, decision, date

## QA (must pass)

1. Charter named (date or "not signed — routed").
2. ASK (named person, verb, date).
3. Purpose is the charter outcome, not a capability pitch.
4. Out of scope present.
5. Next 2 weeks have dates and named owners.
6. Not a training agenda or a 40-slide intro.
7. Does not rewrite the charter fence as if the room can.
8. RACI is not RAPID or a stakeholder 2x2.
9. No invented names or dates.

If 1, 2, 5, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- No signed charter after one ask → [Project Charter](../../project/project-charter/SKILL.md).
- They want a half-day training → refuse; this is 60 minutes to start.
- They want the full RACI built in the room → stub a few rows; route [RACI Delivery](../../project/raci-delivery/SKILL.md).
- Vendor pitch labelled "kickoff" → kill the pitch; keep purpose / fence / 2 weeks / ASK.
