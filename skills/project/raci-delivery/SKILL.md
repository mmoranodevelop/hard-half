# RACI Delivery

**RACI for Named Deliverables** — one page, ≤12 rows: Responsible / Accountable / Consulted / Informed. One A per row. Names, not functions. Horizon = this phase. Default: venture delivery; same spine for F500.

Method origin: responsibility assignment matrix public. Not Bain RAPID. Not a 2x2.

If they want a RACI / RAPID lecture: one paragraph (RACI = work; RAPID = decide) then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Named deliverables have no owner, two owners, or "the team"
- Kickoff needs a work matrix (not a session pack)
- A RAPID map is being used as a task list

## When not to use

- Who decides (one D) — [Decision Rights](../../management/decision-rights/SKILL.md)
- Who believes what / who talks — [Stakeholder Alignment](../../management/stakeholder-alignment/SKILL.md)
- First session pack — [Kickoff Pack](../../project/kickoff-pack/SKILL.md)
- Vendor commercial RACI inside a buy — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)
- Standing D for the project — [Project Charter](../../project/project-charter/SKILL.md)
- Period status — [Sponsor Status](../../project/sponsor-status/SKILL.md)

RACI ≠ RAPID ≠ stakeholder map.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named deliverables | One-page RACI + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted 40-row wallpaper or RAPID labelled RACI | One A per row; rest is backlog |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or two A's for harmony | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Rows are deliverables with a done-test**, not decisions, not meetings, not beliefs.
2. **Exactly one A per row.** Zero or two fail. A may also be R (write both).
3. **At least one named R.** "The team" / "Finance" is not a name.
4. **C is two-way and scarce.** C is not a veto.
5. **I is one-way.** Sponsor is often I, not A on every row.
6. **Page 1 ≤12 rows.** Rest = backlog (count, owner, next cut).
7. **Conflicts of "who decides"** route to RAPID. Do not mix letters.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake matrix.

1. Project and named deliverables — load-bearing
2. People who can be A or R — names, not functions — load-bearing
3. Client vs delivery — who sits on which side
4. Horizon — this phase / next stretch, not the whole life — load-bearing
5. Any stuck row they already fight about
6. Who has the D on conflicts (pointer only)

## Output shape

```
RACI  |  [project]  |  client: [name]  |  horizon: [phase / dates]
D (conflicts): [name — RAPID lives elsewhere]     PM: [name]
Page 1: deliverables that must move this horizon. Backlog: [n] rows.

ASK: [sponsor or named A] to accept this matrix (or row [id]) by [date].

| ID | Deliverable (noun, done-test in 5 words) | R (does) | A (one name) | C | I |
| 1 | [ ] | [name] | [name] | [ ] | [ ]

Rules: one A per row. C ≠ veto. I ≠ A.

CONFLICTS THIS MATRIX DOES NOT DECIDE
- [decision sentence] → decision-rights

CUT
Extra A's refused: [ ]     Rows that were decisions / beliefs: [ ]

NOT THIS PAGE
Who decides → decision-rights    Who believes → stakeholder-alignment    Vendor buy → vendor-sow
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Every row has exactly one A and at least one named R.
2. No row is a decision (approve / kill / fund).
3. Names, not functions.
4. ASK + who accepts + date.
5. ≤12 rows on page 1.
6. Not a RAPID map or a stakeholder 2x2.
7. Sponsor is not A on every row.
8. One page.

If 1, 2, 4, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Two A's for harmony → refuse; extra person is C or I.
- RAPID letters → [Decision Rights](../../management/decision-rights/SKILL.md).
- 40-person wallpaper → page 1 of ≤12 or stop.
- Vendor commercial responsibilities → [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md).
