# Cap Table

**Cap table / dilution one-pager** — as-of fully diluted, proposed round or SAFE stack ($, pre, post, % sold), option-pool now vs target and **who absorbs** the top-up, pro-forma %, who has **D** to accept / cut pool / walk. Default: venture. Same spine if an F500 program holds a venture stake (still pre/post/pool, still not tax).

Method origin: YC post-money SAFE math + Cooley GO option-pool (pool % of post-close FD counted in pre-close for price → dilutes **existing** holders). Carta / Pulley = **vendor education — label.**

If they want a cap-table lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop. No tax, 409A, ISO/NSO, or QSBS.

## When to use

- A priced round or SAFE stack is on the table and nobody has shown pre/post/pool on one page
- Pool top-up is the real price term and founders have not seen who is diluted
- Board asked “what % do we own after”

## When not to use

- Raise **process** (amount, list, weeks) — [Fundraising One-Pager](../../strategy/fundraising-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Economics **and control and waterfall** of a named paper — [Term Sheet Read](../../strategy/term-sheet-read/SKILL.md)
- Monthly letter — [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md)
- Insider cheque yes / partial / no — [Follow-On](../../finance/follow-on/SKILL.md)
- Bank / weeks of cash — [Cash Runway](../../management/cash-runway/SKILL.md)
- Invest / NPV — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)
- One more unit — [Unit Economics](../../strategy/unit-economics/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. As-of holders exist; round or “live snapshot” named | Dilution one-pager + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a spreadsheet or a term sheet labelled “cap table” | Force pre/post/pool/who-is-diluted; kill legal rewrite |
| **refuse** | Holders and round $ both missing; or they want tax / 409A / clause text | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. This is a **business** page: pre / post, pool, who is diluted, who has D. Not a term-sheet rewrite. Do not paste NVCA / YC legal text.
2. Sequence of a priced round: (1) today fully diluted (2) SAFE/note conversion if any (3) **pool top-up if pre-money** (4) new money (5) post %. Getting (3) and (4) wrong is the shuffle miss (Pulley vendor — label).
3. Fully diluted includes outstanding options, unissued pool, warrants, and modelled SAFE/note conversion. Investors negotiate FD, not outstanding (Carta / Pulley — vendor).
4. Cooley GO: pool is typically a % of **post-closing FD**, deemed part of **pre-closing** for price-per-share → dilutes existing holders, not the new money. Hiring-plan 12–18 months vs an abstract 20%. Worked table: same $1M in, **$15M pre / 20% post-money pool → common 67.05%**; **$12M pre / 15% pool → 70.28%**.
5. YC post-money SAFE: ownership ≈ amount / cap. $500k @ $10M cap = **5%**. Stack **adds**; each incremental SAFE $ dilutes **current** holders. “Post” = post-SAFEs, **not** post-Series A.
6. Carta Founder Ownership Report 2026 (**vendor data**): median founding team **~56%** FD after seed, **~36%** after Series A. Context only — do not invent this company’s %.
7. **No tax / 409A / ISO/NSO / QSBS.** If asked: hole + “counsel / valuation firm.” One line. Stop.
8. Do not invent conversion shares when SAFE terms are missing — hole. Do not invent names or %.

## Intake

If **as-of holders (or a live table)** and **proposed round / SAFE stack (or “live snapshot only”)** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Company; as-of date; live vs pro-forma (load-bearing)
2. Fully diluted now by class: founders, granted, unissued pool, investors, SAFEs/notes as **instruments** (load-bearing)
3. Proposed round or SAFE stack: $, pre, post, cap/discount — or “live snapshot only”
4. Pool now %; target %; pre-money or post-money top-up
5. Who has D on accept / pool / walk
6. Hiring-plan bottoms-up 12–18 months — or hole
7. Known holes (missing SAFE terms, unsigned consents) — or “none listed”

## QA (must pass)

1. As-of labelled live vs pro-forma. Sequence of pool vs new money is explicit.
2. Who is diluted (named classes + points) is on the page.
3. Who has D is a name, not “the board” as a hole-cover.
4. ASK + date.
5. No tax / 409A advice. No invented conversion shares. No legal-text rewrite.
6. Not a raise process, not a term-sheet control read, not a monthly letter.
7. One page.

If 2, 4, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Tax, 409A, ISO/NSO, QSBS → one line, counsel, stop.
- Rewriting NVCA / YC clause text → refuse.
- SAFE terms missing and they want share counts → hole, do not invent.
