# PE Monthly Flash

**Sponsor monthly flash — one page** — KPI strip vs plan/prior, cash line, 3 risks, one ASK. Default: PE-backed portco → operating partner / deal team; same spine for family office / growth equity monthly.

Method origin: PE portfolio monitoring public cadence — early flash vs full board pack (Allegro Grey / Pragmatic CFO public); McKinsey PE ops emphasis on standardized KPI + exception focus; flash templates concepts from public portco reporting practice (not a firm’s private pack).

If they want a PE reporting lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Monthly sponsor pack due (or weekly one-pager cousin with same spine)
- OP asks "what moved, what’s the ask?"
- Cash or a thesis KPI flipped; need a pulse before the board pack
- New portco: first flash format lock

## When not to use

- Internal ops KPI sitting with owners — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- External LP / investor letter — [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md)
- Customer quarterly sitting — [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md)
- 13-week bank strip as the whole job — [Cash Runway](../../management/cash-runway/SKILL.md)
- Lender covenant math as the whole job — [Covenant Compliance](../../finance/covenant-compliance/SKILL.md)
- Fiduciary board year rhythm — [Board Calendar](../../management/board-calendar/SKILL.md)

This is the **sponsor pulse**, not the ops review and not the LP letter.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Month + sponsor reader known | Flash one-pager + one ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a 20-slide "monthly" | Kill slides; force KPI strip + cash + 3 risks + ASK |
| **refuse** | No month, no owner, or "write the LP letter" | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One month. One entity (or named roll-up).** As-of date on every number.
2. **KPI strip ≤8** tied to value-creation / thesis — not a data dump. Each: actual, plan, prior, Δ, RYG.
3. **Cash on page 1** — closing cash (or net cash), Δ vs last month, near-term flag. Deep 13-week → cash-runway annex, not a novel here.
4. **Exactly 3 risks** — each with owner and next date. Not ten.
5. **One ASK** to the sponsor or from mgmt — decision / intro / capital / forgive lag. Owner, date.
6. **Variance needs a because (≤12 words)** on material misses. Colour without because is a fail.
7. **Do not invent bookings, EBITDA, or cash.** Hole: `[flash WD_ / final by DATE]`.
8. **Not the board pack, not the LP letter, not the ops sitting.**
9. **Same definitions month to month** — change of definition is a hole callout.
10. **One page.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1–3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake flash.

1. Entity, month, as-of / flash vs final — load-bearing
2. Sponsor reader (OP / deal team) and due day — load-bearing
3. KPI list in force (or thesis KPIs) — load-bearing
4. Cash balance and Δ
5. Material variances already known
6. The ASK this month
7. Covenant watch? (pointer only — detail in covenant-compliance)

## QA (must pass)

1. Month, entity, as-of, flash vs final labelled.
2. KPI strip ≤8 with plan/prior (or hole).
3. Cash on page; not a 13-week novel.
4. Exactly 3 risks with owners.
5. One ASK with owner, verb, date.
6. Material RYG has because; no invented numbers.
7. Definition changes called out.
8. Not ops review, not LP letter, not QBR.
9. One page.

If 1, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want the LP / external letter → [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md).
- They want the internal ops sitting pack → [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md).
- Covenant breach / watch is the story → [Covenant Compliance](../../finance/covenant-compliance/SKILL.md); flash points, does not replace.
- Cash cliff → [Cash Runway](../../management/cash-runway/SKILL.md).
- No month or no reader after one ask → refuse.
