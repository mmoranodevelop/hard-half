# Follow-On

**Follow-on / pro-rata card** — one page: yes · partial · no, $ vs pro-rata $, reserve remaining, re-underwrite (“would we buy today at this price if we held zero?”), signal, outside lead or inside-only conflict, ASK to IC / board / company. Default: fund-MD allocating reserve. Company-MD skin: who is in, who is passing, room for a new lead, ASK (waive / expand round / take the inside bridge). Same spine.

Method origin: Strebulaev (Stanford GSB) — pro rata is the term VCs are **least flexible** on; right not obligation; inside-only price conflict. Carta Learn follow-on = **vendor — label.**

If they want a follow-on lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Pro-rata notice is live (or about to be) and nobody has a yes / partial / no
- Reserve vs this cheque is the IC question
- A major insider passing will be read as a signal

## When not to use

- How to **run a raise** — [Fundraising One-Pager](../../strategy/fundraising-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Dated kill tests on **one bet** — [Kill Criteria](../../strategy/kill-criteria/SKILL.md)
- Internal Go / Kill / Hold / Recycle — [Portfolio Stage Gate](../../management/portfolio-stage-gate/SKILL.md)
- Who % after — [Cap Table](../../finance/cap-table/SKILL.md) (refresh it after a **yes**)
- Control / waterfall of named paper — [Term Sheet Read](../../strategy/term-sheet-read/SKILL.md)
- Invest / NPV of a project — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)
- Remaining FY vs original plan — [Reforecast](../../finance/reforecast/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Company / round and our % or pro-rata $ exist | Yes / partial / no card + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted an IC memo or treated pro rata as committed | Force re-underwrite + reserve + signal; kill “standard super pro rata” |
| **refuse** | Round and our % both missing; or they want a raise process / kill tests | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **this round, this decision window** (pro-rata notice). Not a second fundraising process. Not Go/Kill the company.
2. Decision is **yes $X / partial $Y / no**. Pro rata is a **right, not an obligation**. Not committed capital.
3. Re-underwrite as if **not already in** (Carta vendor — label). Escalation of commitment is why the partnership, not just the board partner, re-underwrites (Strebulaev).
4. Pro rata changes **allocation not size**. Super pro rata (increase %, or scoop unused) is not “standard” — Strebulaev: scary for almost everybody else. Put it on the page if anyone asked.
5. Inside-only round with no arm’s-length lead: **pricing conflict is on the page**. Do not hide it.
6. Signal: a major insider skipping pro rata is read by the next money (Carta vendor). Name what in / partial / out will say.
7. Reserve: $ left vs this cheque vs concentration limit. Carta vendor: pre-2022 vintages typically deployed **47–60%** in the first two years — remainder reserved. GoingVC 1:0 / 2:1 / 1:1 buckets are a **practitioner essay, not a survey** — do not cite as a firm statistic.
8. YC post-money SAFE: pro rata is **not** default; optional **Pro Rata Side Letter**, typically the conversion round.
9. Do not invent reserve $ or %. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **company + round** and **our % now or pro-rata $** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Company; round (priced / SAFE / inside bridge); as-of; notice deadline (load-bearing)
2. Our % FD now; cost basis; pro-rata $ to hold flat (load-bearing)
3. Super-pro-rata asked? Y/N — $
4. Reserve remaining; fund concentration limit — or hole
5. Outside lead (arm’s-length) or inside-only
6. Who has D (IC / GP / company board)
7. Skin: fund-MD allocating / company-MD running the round

## Output shape

```
FOLLOW-ON / PRO-RATA CARD  |  [company]  |  round: [named]  |  as-of: [date]
Vehicle: priced / SAFE / inside bridge     Skin: fund-MD / company-MD
Our % now (FD): [ ]     Cost basis: [ ]
Pro-rata $ to hold flat: [ ]     Super-pro-rata asked: Y/N — $[ ]
Reserve remaining: $[ ]     Concentration limit: [ ] or HOLE
Pro rata = right, not obligation. Not committed capital.

RE-UNDERWRITE: would we buy today at this price if we held zero?  Y / N / partial
Why (three lines max): [ ]
Outside lead (arm’s-length price)?  Y / N — if inside-only, conflict is on this page
Signal if we in / partial / out: [ ]

DECISION: YES $[ ]   /   PARTIAL $[ ]   /   NO
vs pro-rata $: over / at / under

NOT THIS PAGE: raise process → fundraising-one-pager; kill tests → kill-criteria; Go/Kill/Hold → portfolio-stage-gate; % after → cap-table

ASK: [IC approve $ / company waive / expand round for new lead / pass]
Owner: [ ]  Date: [pro-rata window]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Decision is yes / partial / no with a $.
2. Re-underwrite is answered. Inside-only conflict is named if there is no outside lead.
3. Reserve remaining is a number or HOLE (not silent).
4. Signal is named.
5. ASK + owner + date inside the notice window.
6. Pro rata is not treated as committed capital. Super pro rata is not labelled standard.
7. Not a raise process, not kill tests, not a stage-gate.
8. One page.

If 1, 5, 6, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Building the raise process → [Fundraising One-Pager](../../strategy/fundraising-one-pager/SKILL.md).
- “Should we kill the company?” → [Kill Criteria](../../strategy/kill-criteria/SKILL.md) / [Portfolio Stage Gate](../../management/portfolio-stage-gate/SKILL.md).
- Treating pro rata as committed capital → refuse / redline.
- Super pro rata treated as standard → redline.
