# Investor Update

**Monthly investor letter** — phone-length. Miss on the first screen. Same metrics as last send. Cash, burn, runway. Asks a reply can act on. Default: venture, monthly, cap-table blast. F500 / PE chairman: same spine, one judgement line; still not a board paper.

Method origin: YC / Visible.vc public craft (Aaron Harris) + Carta cadence.

If they want a lecture on investor updates: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- This period's letter to the cap table must go out
- A miss month they want to skip or bury
- Same metrics as last send must be comparable
- Asks need to be specific enough to forward

## When not to use

- Board / ELT decision paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- Investment / capital case — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)
- Staff-to-principal yes/no this week — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Internal weekly MOR — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- Fundraising deck or data room — [Fundraising One-Pager](../../strategy/fundraising-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Audited accounts / covenant certificate — counsel / finance own it

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Sendable letter (monthly; quarterly or chairman if they said so) |
| **redline** | They pasted a draft | Same numbers; un-bury the miss; make asks specific |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Miss on the first screen.** A win-only open after a miss month is a fail.
2. **Same metrics every time.** North star, cash, burn, runway, prior period. Do not drop a metric because you missed it.
3. **Cash is the health check.** Cash, burn, runway where a scanner cannot miss them.
4. **Asks are a product.** Named intro, hire, or decision. High on the page. "Any intros welcome" is not an ask.
5. **Monthly is the default.** Quarterly is a declared choice, not a skip. Never skip a bad month.
6. **The body is the artifact.** Phone, no click. PDF optional.
7. **Not a board paper.** No resolution, no vote. Split artifacts if you need a decision.
8. **Do not invent numbers.** Holes over theatre. Proxy metrics get their conversion.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake letter.

1. Audience — cap-table blast (default) / named chair / PE lead — load-bearing
2. Period — month or quarter — load-bearing
3. Metrics they actually have — north star, cash, burn, runway, prior period — load-bearing
4. The miss — if they say "nothing missed," ask once, then require at least one lowlight — load-bearing
5. Wins they can name (customer, hire, ship)
6. Asks — specific, or "none this period"

Voice overlay: load [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md) if one exists. Do not invent a founder voice.

## Output shape

```
Subject: [Company] update — [Month Year] — [headline; miss in here if any]

TL;DR: [1–2 sentences. Miss in here if there is one.]

METRICS (same as last period — do not rotate vanity)
- [North star]: [n] (prior: [n], [+/-]%)
- Cash: [n]
- Burn: [n] / month
- Runway: [n] months
- [1–3 other consistent KPIs]: [n] (prior: [n])
Definition change this period: [none | what changed]

LOWLIGHTS (never empty)
- [Miss]. Cause if known: […]. Plan: [owner] by [date].

WINS (2–4, named)
- […]

ASKS (1–3, a reply can do this week)
1. [Intro to [person / company / role] for [reason] by [date]]
2. […]

NEXT PERIOD
- [2–3 lines of focus]

Holes: [unknown — need cash / burn / runway / prior]
```

Quarterly: same spine + one paragraph on what the quarter means. Chairman: add one judgement line — "The call I am making is [X] because [Y]." Neither becomes a decision memo.

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK line present, or explicit "no ask this period."
2. Miss exists → it is in the subject and TL;DR.
3. Cash, burn, runway present, or labelled holes.
4. Lowlights not empty.
5. Asks are specific, or none.
6. No invented numbers.
7. Not a board paper, pitch, or operating-review dump.
8. One page / phone length.

If 2, 3, 6, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want a board resolution inside the letter → [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md).
- They want the fundraising deck labelled as an update → [Fundraising One-Pager](../../strategy/fundraising-one-pager/SKILL.md).
- They will not send a miss month → refuse the skip.
- Audited / covenant numbers → finance/counsel own the file.
