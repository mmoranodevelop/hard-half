# Principal Brief

**Staff-to-Principal Brief — FOR DECISION** — one page they can act after one rapid reading: question, recommendation, three facts, ASK yes/no with owner and date. Same spine becomes FOR NOTING (zero ask) or a 30-second verbal. Default: venture MD or program sponsor; still not a board paper.

Method origin: BLUF / ministerial-submission / Army decision-memo public cousins.

If they want a BLUF lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A principal must decide or note this week
- "Put this on one page"
- A rambling email that *is* a staff brief (not a pyramid rewrite of long prose)

## When not to use

- Board / ELT decision paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- Rewrite a long email / Slack / deck into a pyramid — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- Live incident first words — [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md)
- Designing the room that decides — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- Recap after they already decided — [Meeting Notes to Decisions](../../writing/meeting-notes-to-decisions/SKILL.md)
- Weekly status with no question — [Operating Review](../../management/operating-review/SKILL.md)
- Investment case — [Business Case](../../strategy/business-case/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | One-pager in the chosen pack + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a draft | Same facts, this spine; holes labelled |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Classify: FOR DECISION or FOR NOTING.** Never smuggle an approval into noting.
2. **The question is the unit.** A topic is not a brief.
3. **Recommendation is a complete-sentence claim** they can accept or reject. "Discuss" fails.
4. **Three facts.** If all three are true, the recommendation follows. Do not invent a fourth story.
5. **BLUF.** Type, question, recommendation, deadline in the first five lines.
6. **Yes/no packaging.** If they accept, you have saved a meeting.
7. **One page.** Annex numbers, legal, history. Two principals or two decisions: two briefs.
8. **Do not invent numbers.** Voice overlay only if [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md) exists.

## Intake

If **principal** and **the question** are both missing after one round: issues list.

1. Principal — name and role — load-bearing
2. Type — FOR DECISION (default) or FOR NOTING
3. The question they actually have — load-bearing
4. The recommendation they will own — or HOLE
5. Three facts they actually have
6. Deadline and what "yes" triggers

Refuse FOR DECISION with no question and no ask (unless noting).

## Output shape

```
PRINCIPAL BRIEF  |  To: [principal]  |  From: [staff]  |  [date]
Type: FOR DECISION     Deadline: [date] — why this week: [cost of wait]
Question: [the question in their mind]
Recommendation: [complete-sentence claim they can accept or reject]

THREE FACTS (if all true, the recommendation follows)
1. [fact — source or date]
2. [fact]
3. [fact]

Risk / what breaks if we wait: [one line]
Options (only if real): A recommended | B | do-nothing
Coordination: seen by [legal / finance / none]
Not asking: [one line]

ASK: Approve / decline by [date]. Owner of next action: [name].
Holes: [ ]
```

FOR NOTING — line 1: FOR NOTING — no decision required. ASK: none.
Verbal (30s): Question. Recommendation. Ask + date. Then stop.

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK (decision pack) + owner + date — or zero ask on noting.
2. One real question, not a topic.
3. Recommendation is a disagreeable sentence (or explicit note).
4. First five lines are enough to act.
5. Three facts; fact vs opinion labelled.
6. They can say yes or no without a meeting.
7. Noting pack does not smuggle an ask.
8. No invented numbers. One page.

If 1, 2, 3, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They pasted a rambling email and want it clearer → [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md) unless they asked for this spine.
- Fiduciary / minutable board paper → [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md).
- Live incident to the outside → [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md).
