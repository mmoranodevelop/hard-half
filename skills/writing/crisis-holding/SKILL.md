# Crisis Holding

**Crisis Holding — First Hour** — one page: holding statement (what we know, what we do not, what we are doing) + who-must-know + next-update clock with timezone + named spokesperson. Default: venture, primary channel internal; same spine for F500 (legal is blocking on who-must-know before any external send).

Method origin: CDC CERC public — say what you know, what you do not, what you are doing to fill the gaps.

If they want a crisis-comms lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- First 60–90 minutes of a live issue
- "We have to say something and we do not know everything"
- Team, customers, or media are already asking
- Zero confirmed facts is still a page: awareness + verifying + clock

## When not to use

- Working-backwards product narrative — [PR/FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md)
- Designed social posts — [Social Carousel](../../branding/social-carousel/SKILL.md)
- Incident is closed, learn from it — [After Action Review](../../management/after-action-review/SKILL.md)
- Principal must decide an operational move — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- How it sounds — [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md)
- Named angry client, not a company incident — [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md)
- After a decision, the company narrative — [All-Hands](../../writing/all-hands/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Live issue | Holding note + who-must-know + clock + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a draft | Same confirmed facts; strip speculation; add the clock |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or invented cause | Issues list. Stop |
| **diagnose** | Optional | Name every unverified claim; still no essay |

## Hard rules

1. **Clock is 60–90 minutes.** Next update has a timezone and a channel. If nothing new, still hit it: "no material change." "Soon" is a fail.
2. **Confirmed facts only**, with who confirmed. Unknowns stated, not implied.
3. **Zero confirmed facts is still producible:** awareness line + verifying + clock. Do not fill with guesses.
4. **One named spokesperson.** Team before media unless safety forces otherwise.
5. **Who-must-know table before publish:** legal, principal, people at risk, team, customers, media.
6. **Do not say:** cause, blame, "no comment," humour, unproven apology, invented CEO quote.
7. **Same facts, different length** for customer / media / social skins. Never give media a fact the team has not had.
8. **Do not invent facts, quotes, or a cause.** Holes stay holes.
9. **Not a 48-hour comms grid.** Produce the holding line or stop.

## Intake

If **two** of 1 and 6 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. What is confirmed — and who confirmed it (or explicit "none yet") — load-bearing
2. What is not confirmed
3. Who may be affected / any safety instruction
4. What we are already doing
5. Spokesperson and who has been told
6. Clock they will actually hit — timezone + channel — load-bearing
7. Primary channel (internal default unless customers or media are already asking)

## Output shape

```
HOLDING  |  [incident, one line]  |  time now: [clock, tz]
From: [incident lead]     Spokesperson: [name]
Approvers: legal [name] / principal [name]
Next update: [clock] [tz] via [channel] — if nothing new, still hit it: "no material change."

WHAT WE KNOW (confirmed only)
- [fact — confirmed by]

WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW YET
- [gap]

WHAT WE ARE DOING
- [action] — owner: [name]

PEOPLE AT RISK / SAFETY (first if physical danger)
- [instruction or "none confirmed"]

WHAT YOU SHOULD DO / SAY IF ASKED
- Use this note. Do not speculate. Point press to [spokesperson].

WHO MUST KNOW (before publish — team before media unless safety)
| Who | Why | Owner of notify | Status |
| Legal / counsel | [ ] | [name] | told / not |
| Principal / incident lead | [ ] | [name] | told / not |
| People at risk | [ ] | [name] | told / not |
| Team | [ ] | [name] | told / not |
| Customers | [ ] | [name] | told / not |
| Media / social | [ ] | [name] | told / not |

Do not say: cause, blame, "no comment," humour, unproven apology, invented CEO quote.

ASK: Publish internally by [clock]. Legal+principal sign-off by [clock]. Next update [clock, channel]. Owner: [spokesperson].
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK + spokesperson + clock with timezone.
2. No factual sentence that is not confirmed and not marked unknown.
3. Unknowns stated, not implied.
4. Team notified before media (unless safety).
5. No cause, blame, "no comment," or humour.
6. Not an incident narrative / speculation essay.
7. Not a PR/FAQ or carousel disguised as a holding line.
8. No invented CEO quote.
9. One page.

If 1, 2, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Counsel's filing or regulator notice → counsel writes it; this page does not freelance.
- Full 48-hour comms grid / war-room playbook → refuse; produce the holding line or stop.
- Speculative "if this leaks" with no live issue → template only; do not invent facts.
- Tweet / social first before the team → refuse.
- Unverified claims they insist on shipping → refuse.
