# All-Hands

**All-Hands Narrative Pack** — one pack: 8–12 minute spine (decision, why, what it means, will-nots, one ask), hostile-first Q&A, manager cascade, sequence (hit people before the room). Default: venture all-hands; same spine for a program town hall in F500.

Method origin: IABC-public cascade sequence (who hears first) + Kotter-public (one town hall is a declaration; managers must retell it) + Google TGIF-public (write what can live outside). Reconstruct. Do not invent a firm's kit.

If they want a town-hall lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A decision is made and the company or program must hear it together
- Reorg, strategy shift, kill/keep, pricing, a ship, a hire at the top
- "We need an all-hands" and the draft is a slide novel or a pep talk
- Program town hall after a gate (same artifact, smaller room)

## When not to use

- First 60–90 minutes of a live incident — [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md)
- An email or Slack that must be acted on — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- Investor letter — [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md)
- Board paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- The decision paper itself — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Affected-person 1:1s on a people action — [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md) (those happen **before** this room)
- Behaviours after the announcement — [Change Adoption](../../management/change-adoption/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Decision exists | Narrative + Q&A + manager kit + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a script or slides | Cut pep, bury, and legal fog; restore the decision |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or announce a firing in the room first | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **The decision is already made.** This skill narrates it. If they are still deciding, route to [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md) / [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md). Do not fake certainty.
2. **People who are personally hit hear it before the room.** Reorg, exit, role-kill: 1:1 first. All-hands is for everyone else the same day.
3. **Open with the decision in one sentence.** Not a founding story. Not "difficult but necessary" as sentence one.
4. **Why, then what it means for you, then what we will not do.** If you cannot name a will-not, you do not yet know the decision.
5. **One ask of the room** (what they should do Monday). Named owner of the follow-up. Clock.
6. **Q&A is written, hostile first.** 5–8 questions. 30-second answers. "I don't know, back by [date]" is allowed. Do not invent.
7. **Manager kit is mandatory.** 5 bullets; what not to speculate; where questions go.
8. **Same facts inside and outside.** Write as if it will be screenshotted.
9. **Do not invent facts, numbers, or a second unmade decision.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1–2 are missing after one round: issues list, not a pep talk.

1. The decision in one sentence (or "not decided" → refuse) — load-bearing
2. Who is hit, and have they been told — load-bearing
3. Room: company / program; who speaks; length
4. What people already believe (the rumour)
5. What you will not do / will not claim
6. Date of the all-hands and the manager cascade
7. Language / voice overlay if one exists

## QA (must pass)

1. Decision is a complete sentence at the top.
2. People-impacting: hit people before the room, or the page says DO NOT RUN.
3. "What we will not do" is non-empty.
4. One ask, owner, next clock.
5. Q&A includes at least two hostile questions with 30-second answers.
6. Manager kit exists.
7. Not a holding statement (no "we do not know what happened").
8. Not an investor letter.
9. No invented facts or numbers.
10. First 60 seconds contain the decision.

If 1, 2, or 9 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Decision not made → refuse. Offer a principal-brief, not a pep all-hands.
- They want the room to be the first time someone learns they are out → refuse.
- They want "no Q&A so we control the message" → refuse the silence; written Q&A still exists.
- Live incident, cause unknown → [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md).
- Legal has not seen a people action → do not run; put legal on sequence as blocking.
