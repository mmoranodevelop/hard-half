# Difficult Conversation

**Conversation prep card** — purpose, facts vs story, third-story opening they can say in 30–60 seconds, one ASK with a date, fallback if no. They walk in with it. They do not email it. Default: venture MD with a report. F500: same card; if HR/legal already owns the case, prepare only the human opening — do not draft findings.

Method origin: Harvard Negotiation Project / Stone–Patton–Heen (public): start from the third story. Kim Scott public: care and challenge together.

If they want a difficult-conversation lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Performance, conflict, or a no that must be said in the room
- Opening is a verdict, or they want to send it as email
- Need one checkable ask plus a fallback independent of mood

## When not to use

- Recurring 1:1 operating cadence — [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md)
- Group decision with a D — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- Yes/no from a principal, no dialogue — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Formal HR investigation, grievance, or legal hold — stop; counsel / HR own it
- Written warning / PIP legal text — HR owns the artifact; this card may still prep the talk
- Scheduling note only — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- Public call-out — refuse (challenge privately unless safety requires otherwise)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Filled prep card + verbatim opening + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a planned speech | Strip story-as-fact; rewrite the opening; keep their facts |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing answers missing, or investigation / public call-out | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Facts vs story.** Dates, numbers, seen behaviour vs interpretation. Do not say the story as fact.
2. **30–60s third-story opening then stop.** Invitation / question. First question is theirs.
3. **One dated ASK.** Checkable. What "yes" looks like in a week.
4. **Fallback independent of mood.** What they will do anyway, by when.
5. **Care and challenge together.** Care without challenge, or challenge without care, fails.
6. **HR/legal live → do not draft findings.** Opening only.
7. **Do not invent quotes.** Holes stay holes. Do not send this as email unless geography or safety forces it.

Voice overlay: load [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md) if one exists. Do not invent a "coach voice."

## Intake

If **who** and **purpose** are both missing after one round: issues list. No facts, only a feeling → issues list of what to gather; do not prosecute.

1. Who — role, relationship, power tilt — load-bearing
2. Purpose — outcome in one sentence, not "have a chat" — load-bearing
3. Facts they actually have — dates, numbers, quotes, seen behaviour
4. The ask and by when
5. Fallback if no — what they will do anyway, by when
6. HR / legal already live? yes → do not draft findings

## Output shape

```
CONVERSATION PREP  |  with [name], [role]
Date of talk: [date]    Purpose (one sentence): [agree a four-week recovery plan for the Friday misses]
Identity threat (yours, private — do not say): [competent / decent / fair?]

FACTS vs STORY
| Facts (observable — date, quote, number) | Story (interpretation — do not say as fact) |
| [On [date], [X] happened. Quote: "…"] | [The story I am telling myself is …] |
Their likely story (generous, specific): [ ]
Third story (bystander; no villain): [ ]
Contribution (yours first): [ ]

OPENING (30–60 seconds, then you stop)
"[Purpose in one line.] [Third story in two sentences.] [Invitation / question.]"
First question: [What happened from your side?]

ASK
What: [checkable]
By when: [date]
What "yes" looks like in a week: [ ]

FALLBACK if no (does not depend on their mood): [I will [X] by [date].]
Do not say: [intent you cannot know] / [gotcha] / [diagnosis of their character]

ASK (the ship line): [Name] agrees to [checkable thing] by [date], or fallback [X] by [date].
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK + owner (them or you) + date.
2. Purpose is not "give feedback" / "have a chat".
3. At least three actual facts (or labelled holes — then do not ship a prosecution).
4. Opening is not a verdict still talking at minute three.
5. No asserted intent ("you don't care").
6. Fallback present and independent of mood.
7. Care and challenge both present.
8. Not an email verdict, not a 1:1 cadence agenda, not scripted HR findings. No invented quotes.

If 1, 2, 4, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Investigation → stop; HR / counsel own it.
- Cadence → [One-on-One](../../management/one-on-one/SKILL.md).
- Group D → [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md).
- Public call-out → refuse.
- Feeling-only → issues list of facts to gather.
