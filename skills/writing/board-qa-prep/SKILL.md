# Board Q&A Prep

**Board Q&A Pack** — 8–12 real questions (hostile first) with 30-second answers, one number each, "I don't know by DATE" where true. Default: venture board / IC; same spine for F500.

Method origin: FRC UK Corporate Governance Code (directors owe constructive challenge) + NACD-public (strategy, risk, evidence, what is missing) + Army BLUF (answer first, then one fact). Not a consulting "hostile Q playbook".

If they want a "how boards work" lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Board / IC / ELT sitting is on the calendar and Q&A will happen
- The paper exists (or a draft) and you need answers, not more slides
- A director is known-hostile on cash, related parties, a miss, a person
- Rehearsal: they want to be asked the ugly versions

## When not to use

- Writing the paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- Designing the decision meeting — [Decision Meeting](../../management/decision-meeting/SKILL.md)
- Staff-to-principal one-pager — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Investor letter — [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md)
- Live incident holding line — [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md)
- After the board decides, the company narrative — [All-Hands](../../writing/all-hands/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Paper or decision exists | Question set + 30-second answers + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted talking points | Kill hedge, pivot, and speeches; force 30s |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or they want guessed numbers | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Answer in the first sentence.** BLUF. Then one number or one fact. Then stop. ~30 seconds spoken (~70–90 words). A second minute is a speech.
2. **Hostile first.** Write the unkind version. If you only prep the polite FAQ, you will fail the real room.
3. **A number where a number is required.** Cash, runway, margin, NPS, headcount, a date. If you do not have it: "I don't have that in the room; I will send it by [date]" — not a guess.
4. **Match the altitude.** Premise → premise. Magnitude → a band. People → a name.
5. **Name what you do not know.** Plus the date you will close it.
6. **Do not argue the tone.** Acknowledge the question; do not thank them for "a great question" as filler.
7. **Same facts as the paper.** If the answer needs a fact not in the memo, add it to the memo or label it new.
8. **No invented diligence, customers, or "the board loves this".** Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–2 are missing after one round: issues list, not a guessed FAQ.

1. Sitting (board / IC / ELT), date, the decision or noting item — load-bearing
2. Paper pointer or the one-sentence recommendation — load-bearing
3. Known hostile topics / named directors if it changes the question
4. Numbers you actually have (and holes)
5. What you will not claim
6. Who answers in the room (CEO / CFO / you)
7. Venture or F500 (venture default)

## Output shape

```
BOARD Q&A PREP  |  [sitting]  |  [date]  |  item: [decision / noting]
Paper: [pointer]    Speaker: [ ]
Recommendation they must not contradict: [one sentence]

HOW TO ANSWER (every row)
Sentence 1 = answer. Sentence 2 = number or fact. Optional: "I don't know — back by [date]". Stop.

HOSTILE (write these first)
| # | Question (their words, unkind) | 30-second answer | Number | Hole? |
| 1 | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ]

REAL (the ones that sound polite and still kill you)
| # | Question | 30-second answer | Number | Hole? |
| [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ]

WILL NOT CLAIM
- [ ]

PARK (too long for the room)
| Question | What you will send | By when | Owner |
| [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ]

DO NOT SAY
- [hedge, pivot to the slide, blame a named director, a number you do not have]

ASK: [speaker] rehearses hostile Qs by [date] before the sitting. Paper holes closed by [owner] by [date].
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. ≥8 questions, at least 3 hostile.
2. Every answer's first sentence is the answer.
3. Every magnitude question has a number or a dated hole.
4. Spoken length is ~30 seconds, not a paragraph.
5. Facts match the paper or are labelled new.
6. "I don't know + date" used instead of guesses.
7. ASK + owner + date.
8. Not a rewritten board memo, not a meeting agenda.
9. No invented numbers.
10. One page of Qs + one page of answers max.

If 1, 3, 7, or 9 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want you to invent a margin or a customer logo for the room → refuse.
- They want to bury the miss and "pivot to strategy" → refuse the pivot; answer the miss first.
- Counsel must own a pending investigation / related-party / insider answer → park with counsel; do not freelance.
- No paper yet → write the memo first, or a principal-brief; this skill is Q&A on a paper.
- They want 40 FAQ slides → refuse; this is 30-second answers.
