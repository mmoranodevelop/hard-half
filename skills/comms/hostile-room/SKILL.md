# Hostile Room

**HOSTILE-ROOM SPINE — live, after the cards exist.** One page they walk in with *in addition to* the Q cards. Do not abandon the 8-minute narrative for combat. Default: venture board / ELT under fire. Same spine for F500.

Method origin: Duarte, *4 ways to prepare for a hostile audience* (28 Mar 2024): study what drives them / how they grade / what creates buy-in; **change the questions** from defence (“Why haven’t you…”) to information; control prep/message/delivery — you cannot control their response. STATE (Share facts, Tell story, Ask, Tentative, Encourage testing) is a **route to 1:1** — [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md) — not a board performance.

If they want a hostile-audience lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Sitting is dated, hostility is expected, and Q cards already exist
- Need the live spine: opening lines that produce information-questions, pause rules, stop time
- The plan is “just be authentic and wing it”

## When not to use

- The Q card — [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md)
- First 60–90 min of an incident — [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md)
- 1:1 hard talk — [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md)
- 8 min spoken in a normal board — [Board Spoken](../../comms/board-spoken/SKILL.md)
- 8–12 min in an operating room — [Room Narrative](../../comms/room-narrative/SKILL.md)
- All-hands Q&A (friendly-enough company room with a kit) — [All-Hands](../../writing/all-hands/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Cards exist; sitting dated | Live spine + rephrased claims + stop rules + ASK |
| **redline** | They walked in with cards only, or a combat script | Restore the 8-min spine; change defence-questions to information |
| **refuse** | No cards yet; wing-it; 1:1 STATE as a board act; live incident | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Cards first.** If [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md) does not exist, write it, then this.
2. Keep the **8-minute spine**. The hostile sitting still needs a beginning, a point, and an ask.
3. Rewrite opening lines so they invite **information-questions**, not “Why haven’t you…?”
4. Rehearse interruption. The first hostile question will come **before minute three**. Answer in 30 seconds, land the fact, **return to the spine**. Do not follow them into the swamp.
5. Pause. Do not match heat. Do not joke away a fiduciary question. “I’ll confirm by [date]” beats a guess.
6. Name ally / timekeeper. Name when you will stop.
7. After: 1:1s with the hostile principal are [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md), not a second performance.
8. If a material incident just broke → [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md). Leave this room.
9. Do not invent motives or facts. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **Q cards exist** and **sitting date** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake spine.

1. Sitting; date; who is hostile (named) (load-bearing)
2. Q cards exist? Y / write them first (load-bearing with 1)
3. The 8-minute spine you will still deliver (headline + ask)
4. Three motives you believe are in the room (drive / how they grade / buy-in) — labelled as belief, not fact
5. Opening lines to rewrite (defence → information)
6. Ally / timekeeper; stop time
7. Personal-attack sentence; “I don’t know, back by [time]” sentence


## QA (must pass)

1. Q cards exist (or this page says write them first — then do not ship this as the live kit).
2. 8-minute spine still present. One ASK + owner + sitting date.
3. Rephrased claims + don’t-know sentence + stop time.
4. Not the Q card. Not crisis-holding. Not 1:1 STATE.
5. Motives labelled belief, not fact. No invented facts.

If 1, 2, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- No cards → write [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md) first.
- Wing-it / “just be authentic” → refuse.
- Live incident → [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md).
- Hallway 1:1 after → [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md).
