# Demo Narrative

**Demo Narrative — Governing Thought + Discovery-Mapped Acts** — one page the presenter runs: governing thought the EB could repeat, Situation Slide (their goals / pain / asked capability / value / timeline) in ≤2 minutes, last-thing-first order mapped to discovery time spent, cut list, peel prompts, wrap = price window + ~4-minute next-step + paper-process ask. Default: venture (one founder in the room; still last-thing-first). Same spine for F500 (more stakeholders; same four acts).

Method origin: Gong Labs (vendor) demo anatomy + Great Demo! (training vendor) “Do the Last Thing First.” https://www.gong.io/blog/sales-demos

If they want a demo-methodology lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Discovery notes exist and this week’s meeting is a screen-share / live demo
- The default plan is “walk them through the platform”
- Payoff is currently saved for minute 40
- Next steps have no reserved time

## When not to use

- Internal product Working Backwards — [PR-FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md)
- External content tiles — [Social Carousel](../../branding/social-carousel/SKILL.md)
- Strategy / options deck — [Consulting Deck](../../documents/consulting-deck/SKILL.md)
- Briefing *our* principal — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Prose rewrite of buried slides — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- No discovery yet — [Discovery Call](../../comms/discovery-call/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Discovery map exists; governing thought writable | Run-of-show + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a feature agenda or 30-slide tour | Invert order; force cut list + 4-minute wrap |
| **refuse** | Feature tour requested; or no discovery mapping and they will not run discovery first | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **this demo**. Governing thought first. Everything else is cut.
2. **No disco-demo.** Discovery map (what they spent time on, in order) is load-bearing. Missing map → [Discovery Call](../../comms/discovery-call/SKILL.md), not this page.
3. Act I **≤2 min**: *their* situation, not logos, not architecture (Great Demo! Situation Slide, training vendor). Gong (vendor, 67,149 demos): contextual overview **<2 minutes**.
4. Act II: **last-thing-first** mapped to discovery time spent. Saving “the best for last” is the named failure. Gong: *zero* closed-won demos with **>76 seconds** of uninterrupted pitching (unsuccessful up to **106s**); **21%** more speaker switches.
5. Cut list is mandatory. Feature-dump makes a follow-up less likely (Gong vendor; exact % not extracted — do not invent).
6. Act IV: price simply; winning demos spend **12.7%** more time on next steps (~**4 minutes**). UFC at the open so the wrap is permitted. Gong: unsuccessful demos spend **8%** more time on price.
7. Talk/listen on a winning demo is **65:35** (rep-heavy — unlike discovery). Do not import discovery’s listen-ratio as a demo rule.
8. Do not invent discovery topics. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake run-of-show.

1. Governing thought (one sentence tied to this buyer’s pain / metric) — load-bearing
2. Discovery map (topics they spent time on, in order) or “no discovery — do not demo” — load-bearing
3. Cut list: features we will not show — load-bearing
4. Next-step ask reserved at the end (date + paper-process owner) — load-bearing
5. Who is in the room (EB present Y/N; leave-early risk)
6. Price window (say / defer) and UFC at open
7. Demo date this week

## Output shape

```
DEMO NARRATIVE  |  [account]  |  date: [day]  |  room: [names]  |  EB present: Y/N
Governing thought (EB could repeat): [one sentence]

ACT I  Situation Slide  ≤2 min — their goals / pain / asked capability / value / timeline
[four bullets, their words]     NOT: logos, architecture

ACT II  Last-thing-first (mapped to discovery time spent)
1. [highest-time discovery topic → show the end result first]
2. [next]
3. [next]
Uninterrupted pitch cap: 76 seconds. Stop. Let them peel.

ACT III  Peel prompts (buyer questions, not our tour)
[prompt 1]  [prompt 2]     We will not show: [cut list]

ACT IV  Wrap ~4 min
Price window: [say / defer]     Next step: [buyer action + date]
Paper-process owner: [name]     UFC at open: Y/N

NOT THIS PAGE: feature tour (refuse); PR-FAQ → pr-faq; carousel → social-carousel; strategy deck → consulting-deck; no discovery → discovery-call

ASK: [presenter] runs this order on [date]. Buyer next step: [ ]
Owner: [ ]  Date: [ ]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Governing thought is one sentence, buyer-tied.
2. Discovery map exists — or the page is refused.
3. Cut list is on the page.
4. Wrap has a dated next-step (~4 min reserved).
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not a feature tour, PR-FAQ, carousel, or consulting deck.
7. One page.

If 2, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- “Walk them through the platform” → refuse (feature tour).
- No discovery mapping → issues list; route [Discovery Call](../../comms/discovery-call/SKILL.md).
- Payoff saved for minute 40 while the EB may leave → redline to last-thing-first.
- They want a methodology class → one paragraph then produce or stop.
