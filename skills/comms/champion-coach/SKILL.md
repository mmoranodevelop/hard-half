# Champion Coach

**Champion Brief — What They Will Say When We Leave** — one page *for the Champion*: Three Whys in *their* voice, the two objections they will hear and the next line *they* should use, who they must convene (Mobilizer test / EB intro), the metric they will defend, the this-week internal ask, the test we will use to confirm they did it. Default: venture (founder-Champion is common — still test the intro). Same spine for F500 (Coach vs Champion is the usual trap).

Method origin: MEDDICC (training vendor) Champion vs Coach (“NO CHAMPION, NO DEAL”) + CEB/Challenger Mobilizer vs Talker vs Blocker. https://meddicc.com/what-is-meddpicc/champion

If they want a Champion / Challenger lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A named person at the buyer must sell in the room we are not in
- The friendly Talker is being treated as a Champion
- We need their words, not our deck, for a closed-room meeting this week
- The Champion-test (will they introduce the EB?) has not been run

## When not to use

- *Our* exec on the pursuit — [Exec Sponsor Live](../../commercial/exec-sponsor-live/SKILL.md)
- Briefing *our* principal — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- The ask *to* a customer for a reference — [Reference Ask](../../commercial/reference-ask/SKILL.md)
- 30 minutes *with* the EB — [Economic Buyer](../../comms/economic-buyer/SKILL.md)
- Joint close dates — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- Named objections *we* will say — [Objection Map](../../comms/objection-map/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named candidate + script in their voice + internal ask writable | Leave-behind + test ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted our pitch deck labelled “champion brief,” or named a Talker | Force their voice; label Coach vs Champion |
| **diagnose** | Unclear Contact / Coach / Champion | Label with evidence; do not ship a fake Champion |
| **refuse** | Two of named candidate / their-voice script / internal ask / test missing; or they want our exec briefed and called Champion work | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **what they will say this week when we leave**. Person + date + the internal ask. Friendship is not the test.
2. Labels: **Contact / Coach / Champion**. MEDDICC (training vendor): Champion = power + internal seller + vested interest. A Coach gives insight; a person with power who will not sell internally is not yet a Champion. **“NO CHAMPION, NO DEAL.”**
3. **Champion ≠ our exec-sponsor.** Seller-side exec is [Exec Sponsor Live](../../commercial/exec-sponsor-live/SKILL.md).
4. CEB 21 Nov 2016 PR (load-bearing): **51%** of customers willing to buy are **not** willing to advocate; stakeholders **5.4 (2014) → 6.8 (2016)**; **57%** of purchase process before a rep; **39%** overwhelmed. Brent Adamson: willingness to buy ≠ willingness to advocate. CEB 20 Oct 2015: purchase likelihood **30%** when >5 stakeholders; execs care about **organizational buy-in**, not the offering; the friendly eager advocate is “the last person suppliers need.”
5. Challenger, Inc. (training vendor): Talker / Blocker / Mobilizer. A Talker is a Coach, not a Champion. Equip the Mobilizer. Consensus buy ~**10–11** (also “8 to 11”) — label as vendor.
6. Three Whys in *their* voice so they can carry it. Salesforce State of Sales 6th ed.: **59%** of buyers say reps don’t understand their goals — they cannot carry a goal we never wrote down.
7. Test: did they introduce the EB, or run a meeting without us? MEDDICC: a Champion who will not introduce the EB is not a Champion. If they fail, they are a Coach — find or build.
8. Do not invent their words or a closed-room outcome. Holes stay holes. No 20-slide dump they cannot repeat.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake brief.

1. Named Champion candidate + evidence of power *or* a plan to test/build (Coach vs Champion labelled) — load-bearing
2. Script in *their* voice (Three Whys) — load-bearing
3. Internal ask they will make this week (meeting they call, EB they introduce) — load-bearing
4. Test we will use (intro happened Y/N; closed-room next step appeared) — load-bearing
5. Two objections they will hear + the line *they* should use
6. Metric they will defend
7. Who else is in the buying group (count, functions)

## QA (must pass)

1. Candidate named; Coach vs Champion labelled with evidence.
2. Script is their voice, not our deck.
3. Internal ask + date this week.
4. Test is specified (EB intro or closed-room step).
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not our exec brief, not a principal brief, not a reference ask.
7. One page.

If 1, 2, 3, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Brief *our* exec and call it Champion work → refuse; [Exec Sponsor Live](../../commercial/exec-sponsor-live/SKILL.md).
- Friendly Talker treated as Champion → diagnose; do not ship.
- Two load-bearing fields missing → issues list.
- They want a methodology class → one paragraph then produce or stop.
