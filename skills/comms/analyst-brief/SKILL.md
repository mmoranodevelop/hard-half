# Analyst Brief

**ANALYST / JOURNALIST BRIEFING BOOK — 30–45 minutes, mostly one-way.** Named analyst in the chair. Default: venture AR slot, 1–2 briefings a year with a given non-client analyst. Same spine for F500. Visual in the room; text they can file.

Method origin: ARInsights *Analyst Briefings 101* (briefing = **30 min–1 hour**, **one-way** vendor → analyst, not a sales call / media ambush / investor presentation; skip 10-minute intros) + IIAR Nov 2021 public landing (plan, both parties’ objectives, explanatory not just promotional, AR manager moderates, follow up). SEC FD as the refuse for MNPI to sell-side / holders.

If they want an AR lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A named analyst (or journalist) is booked, 30–60 minutes, education not a pitch
- Need a briefing book the night before: objective, spine, do-not-say, leave-behind
- Someone is about to wing it or turn the slot into an inquiry

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named chair + date | Briefing book + spine + do-not-say + follow-up ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a sales deck or an inquiry script | Force one-way education; kill intros, pitch, rival research |
| **refuse** | No named analyst; wing-it; inquiry; FD-selective MNPI | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Named chair.** Firm, beat, last coverage. Non-client: time the 1–2 slots a year to a real milestone.
2. **30–45 minutes** (60 is the ceiling). Narrative in the first five minutes. Do not spend **10 minutes** on intros.
3. One-way education, not a dialogue-as-consulting, not a sales call, not a media ambush, not an investor presentation.
4. Do **not** turn a briefing into an **inquiry** (asking the analyst what we should do). That is a paid/client inquiry.
5. Do not show competitive research from a rival firm to this analyst.
6. **FD:** sell-side / holders + MNPI = refuse (simultaneous public or do not say). Industry analysts (Gartner-type) are usually not Rule 100(b)(1) persons; still no second set of facts. Journalists are outside FD’s enumerated list — **not a leak channel**. When in doubt for a reporting issuer, public-first or do not say it.
7. Avoid NDA if possible. Follow-ups with named owners. Do not turn the thank-you into a second pitch.
8. Do not invent coverage or numbers. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **named analyst/journalist** and **our objective for this slot** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake book.

1. Named person, firm, beat, last coverage (load-bearing)
2. Date; 30–45 / 60 min; spokespeople; AR moderator
3. Our objective + their objective; success = a fact they can reuse (load-bearing with 1)
4. What we solve; differentiation we can defend; direction (what’s next and why)
5. One customer story we may tell
6. Reporting issuer Y/N; FD check: everything said already public or immaterial
7. Leave-behind; follow-ups we will actually send

## QA (must pass)

1. Named chair. 30–60 min. One-way education.
2. Our objective + their objective. Success is a reusable fact.
3. ASK + owner + date (follow-ups).
4. Do-not-say includes MNPI / inquiry / rival research.
5. Not a thesis, not a carousel, not investor-spoken, not a sales pitch.
6. No invented coverage or numbers. Reporting issuer: no MNPI.

If 1, 2, 3, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Wing-it / no plan → refuse (IIAR: recipe for disaster).
- Inquiry disguised as briefing → refuse.
- MNPI to sell-side or holder → refuse. Journalist leak → refuse.
