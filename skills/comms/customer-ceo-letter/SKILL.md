# Customer CEO Letter

**LETTER TO [CUSTOMER CEO NAME] — sendable, signed, one ask.** One page they could forward internally without embarrassment. Default: venture MD/account exec. CEO-to-CEO only for CEO-level news. Same spine for F500.

Method origin: Public letter craft — Bezos 1997/2017 (direct, one idea, named facts, we/you, no brochure) + Jassy 2024 (strategy *and* a point of view) + Paul Stansik 2024 (CEO letter as sense-making at a **big-deal** event; short half-life; human voice, not a company newsletter). Steal the **craft**, not the shareholder audience. Pyramid for the governing thought.

If they want a CEO-letter lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A named customer CEO must get one idea this week (launch, change, a fact about *their* business)
- The draft is last quarter’s QBR with “as discussed”
- A peer on our side should sign

## When not to use

- Angry / at-risk customer this week — [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md)
- Period one-pager to *our* sponsor — [Sponsor Status](../../project/sponsor-status/SKILL.md)
- Quarterly business review — [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md)
- Partner construct — [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md)
- Closing an offer — [Offer Conversation](../../writing/offer-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Warm intro who-asks-whom — [Intro Ask](../../comms/intro-ask/SKILL.md)
- “Dear CEO” mail-merge, rate card, RFP, or a letter actually for procurement — refuse

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named CEO + why this week | One-page letter + one dated ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a QBR cover or a brochure | Force one idea, one proof, one ask, their language |
| **refuse** | No named CEO; generic fact; angry-this-week; procurement theatre | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **this week**, to a **named** CEO. If you cannot say why *this* person, this week, do not write.
2. **One page, one ask.** 15 minutes with them or their named deputy, or a decision they alone can take.
3. Sentence one: why this person, why now — a specific fact about **their** business, not ours. Governing thought in sentence two.
4. One proof (a number, a named outcome, a named person on our side).
5. **Peer signature.** Junior sender + CEO addressee is a tell.
6. They could forward it without embarrassment. No brochure. No invoice chase. No apology-as-escalation.
7. If they are angry this week → [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md). Do not mix.
8. Do not invent their facts or our numbers. Holes stay holes.
9. Voice overlay from [Voice Blueprint](../../writing/voice-blueprint/SKILL.md) if one exists. Do not invent a consulting voice.

## Intake

If **named recipient** and **why this week (a fact about their business)** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake letter.

1. Customer CEO name, title, firm (load-bearing)
2. Why *this* person, *this week* — one fact about their business (load-bearing with 1)
3. Our sender (peer) and channel (letter / email their PA will not delete)
4. Governing thought — one idea
5. One proof (number / named outcome / named person)
6. One ask + date
7. What this letter is *not* (not QBR, not invoice, not apology)

## Output shape

```
TO: [Name], CEO, [firm]
FROM: [Name], [title — peer]
SUBJECT: [PA will not delete — specific]
DATE: [this week]

[Name] —

WHY YOU, WHY NOW
[one specific fact about their business]. [Governing thought — sentence two.]

WHAT WE ARE DOING / PROPOSING (their language)
[ ]

ONE PROOF
[number / named outcome / named person on our side]

ONE ASK  |  date: [ ]
[15 min with you or [named deputy] / the decision only you can take]

This letter is not a QBR, not an invoice chase, not an apology-as-escalation.

[First name]
[mobile]  |  [assistant name, email]
ASK owner: [sender]     Date: [ ]
HOLES: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Named CEO. Named peer sender. This week.
2. A fact about *their* business in the open. One idea. One proof. One ask.
3. ASK + owner + date.
4. One page. Forwardable without embarrassment.
5. Not client-escalation, not QBR, not sponsor-status, not mail-merge.
6. No invented facts or numbers.

If 1, 2, 3, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Angry this week → [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md).
- Generic “Dear CEO” → refuse.
- Letter actually for procurement, CEO as theatre → refuse.
