# Proposal Narrative

**Proposal Cover Narrative — Answer First** — one page (the first page the EB reads): recommendation in sentence one, Why anything / Why us / Why now, 2–3 permissioned proof points, how they buy (paper process), this-week ask, map of what the rest of the proposal contains. Default: venture (short cover; founder-EB). Same spine for F500 (still one page the EB will read; body stays in the file).

Method origin: APMP executive-summary-as-compass (publicly restated by a CP APMP practitioner) + answer-first buyer cognition. https://www.bidwrite.com.au/insights/a-pragmatists-view-on-executive-summaries/

If they want an APMP / Pyramid / Challenger lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Bid/no-bid is already yes and the cover has not been written
- The 40-page response is underway and “we’ll summarize later”
- The EB will not read the body; sentence one is still background
- Proof, paper process, or the ask is missing from the first page

## When not to use

- Scope, deliverables, terms — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)
- Whether to pursue — [Bid No-Bid](../../commercial/bid-no-bid/SKILL.md)
- Buried prose to rewrite — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- The proposal *file* (body, exhibits, price book) — [Sales Proposal](../../documents/sales-proposal/SKILL.md)
- Offer architecture — [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Joint close calendar — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- Internal product Working Backwards — [PR-FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Answer, why-now, one proof, and an ask are writable | Cover + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a company-history cover or a section recap | Force sentence-one answer; kill history and SOW paste |
| **refuse** | Two of answer / why-now / permissioned proof / ask missing; or they want the body first | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **the cover the EB reads this week**. Sentence one = the recommendation. Not background.
2. Bid/no-bid is already yes. If not → [Bid No-Bid](../../commercial/bid-no-bid/SKILL.md).
3. Storyboard the cover *before* drafting sections (APMP best practice as restated on BidWrite, 3 Feb 2021). Cover letter is the persuasive cousin, same sitting.
4. Three Whys on the cover (MEDDICC, training vendor): Why anything? Why us? Why now? Why-now needs a buyer-owned date or critical event.
5. Proof is permissioned (M1). “Proof pending — do not invent” is allowed. RAIN Group (training vendor, >700 B2B purchases, $3.1B): winners “persuaded me we would achieve results” and “depicted purchasing process accurately”; **ROI case is not enough**.
6. JOLT (training vendor): extra options freeze indecision — the cover *prescribes*. CEB 2016 PR: leading orgs **Prescribe**; “responsive” extra options exacerbate the struggle to buy.
7. Paper process belongs on the cover (how they buy), not a pasted SOW or price table.
8. Do not invent metrics, signatures, or dates. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake cover.

1. The answer in one sentence (what we recommend they buy / do) — load-bearing
2. Why now: buyer-owned date or critical event — load-bearing
3. At least one permissioned metric, or “proof pending — do not invent” — load-bearing
4. Named ask (decision, signature step, or paper-process start) — load-bearing
5. Why us (two facts, not a capabilities dump)
6. Paper process: Process / People / Timing as known
7. What the rest of the file contains (map, not recap)

## Output shape

```
PROPOSAL COVER NARRATIVE — ANSWER FIRST  |  [buyer]  |  as-of: [date]  |  EB: [name]
Sentence one (the answer): [what we recommend they buy / do]

WHY ANYTHING  [cost of inaction in their metric]
WHY US        [two facts — not company history]
WHY NOW       [buyer-owned date / critical event]

PROOF (permissioned M1 — or [proof pending — do not invent])
1. [customer / anonymized]  [metric + window]  permission: [written / anon / internal]
2. [ ]
3. [ ]

HOW THEY BUY (paper process — RAIN: depict it accurately)
Process / people / timing: [ ]     Next paper step: [owner + date]

MAP OF THE FILE (not a recap)
SOW → vendor-sow     Price → pricing-one-pager / sales-proposal     Dates → mutual-action-plan

NOT THIS PAGE: pursuit gate → bid-no-bid; buried prose rewrite → pyramid-principle; the file itself → sales-proposal

ASK: [decision / signature step / paper-process start]
Owner: [EB or named]  Date: [this week]  Yes/no: [ ]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Sentence one is the recommendation, not background.
2. Why-now has a buyer-owned date or critical event.
3. Proof is permissioned or explicitly pending — never invented.
4. One ASK + named owner + date.
5. Not a SOW, bid gate, pyramid rewrite, or the proposal file.
6. One page.

If 1, 3, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- “Write the 40 pages first, summarize later” → refuse (APMP failure mode).
- Two of answer / why-now / proof / ask missing → issues list.
- Company history or SOW/price paste as the cover → redline or refuse.
- They want a methodology class → one paragraph then produce or stop.
