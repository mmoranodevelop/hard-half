# Negotiation Trades

**Negotiation Trades — Give / Get Table** — one private page (memo to self, then used live): columns = Give (item, cost-to-us, value-to-them), Get (item, value-to-us), if-then sentence, status. Price discount is *not* row 1; mix / scope / term / timing / access / proof rights come first. A “never give” line at the bottom. Named authority if a give crosses a zone. Default: venture (mix is the lever). Same spine for F500 (procurement in the room; still no vacuum haircut).

Method origin: Sandler (training vendor) Negotiation Matrix — written give/get, do not discount the base price; change the mix. https://sandler.com/blog/the-sandler-negotiation-matrix/

If they want a negotiation lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- This week’s conversation will horse-trade and we have no written matrix
- The buyer asked for a lower number and the reflex is “take 10% off”
- We need if-then sentences with dated gets
- Authority for what can be traded in the room is unclear

## When not to use

- One named discount vs list with authority — [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md)
- Non-standard price + legal + delivery as one package — [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md)
- Offer architecture for the motion — [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Named money objection, not yet trading — [Objection Map](../../comms/objection-map/SKILL.md)
- The proposal cover’s commercial facts after trades land — [Proposal Narrative](../../comms/proposal-narrative/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. At least two non-price gives with matched gets | Give/get table + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a discount ladder or “what if I take 10% off” | Kill base-price discount as a row; force mix trades |
| **refuse** | Two of matched give/get pairs / cost-vs-value labels / if-then sentence / authority missing; or they want discounting the base price listed as an option | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **this week’s table**, written *before* the call. Person + date + if-then. Lack of preparation is the named failure (Sandler, training vendor).
2. **Do not list discounting the base quoted price as an option.** Sandler official sample matrix omits it: change the product and service mix instead. RAIN Group (training vendor) objections: don’t drop price in a vacuum — trade.
3. No unilateral concession. Every give has a parallel get. Sandler: “You should not grant concessions from your side without a parallel concession offer from your negotiating counterpart.”
4. Label **cost-to-us vs value-to-them**. Do not trade high-cost / low-value first.
5. If-then is a live sentence: “If we __, then you __ by [date].” Gets include term, volume, intro to EB, case-study permission, faster signature, multi-year — dated.
6. RAIN Group (training vendor, n=713; 449 buyers, $2.59B): most sellers cave on pure price; buyers admit they could pay more; #1 separator = understanding **leverage**; top negotiators **9.3×** more likely to have had extremely effective negotiation training; procurement cites cost **12×** more often than quality as a success metric.
7. Force Management (training vendor): give/gets, anchors, multiple options; negotiate on value not price. JOLT (training vendor): two paths max — extra commercial options increase indecision. Zendesk “paid for itself” quote on FM’s page is a **vendor case, not a pack standard**.
8. Authority: what we can trade in the room vs [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md) / [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md). Do not invent list price, cost, or authority. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake table.

1. At least two **non-price gives** with a matched **get** — load-bearing
2. Cost-to-us vs value-to-them labelled — load-bearing
3. If-then sentence for the live conversation — load-bearing
4. Authority: in-room vs deal-desk vs discount-exception — load-bearing
5. Never-give line
6. Buyer leverage we actually hold (and theirs)
7. Call date this week + who is in the room

## QA (must pass)

1. At least two non-price give/get pairs.
2. Cost-to-us vs value-to-them labelled.
3. If-then has a date.
4. Base-price discount is not listed as an option.
5. Authority in-room vs routed is on the page.
6. ASK + owner + date.
7. Not a discount ticket, deal-desk package, or price card.
8. One page.

If 1, 4, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- “What if I take 10% off?” → refuse (not on the matrix).
- Give with no get → refuse.
- Two load-bearing fields missing → issues list.
- They want a methodology class → one paragraph then produce or stop.
