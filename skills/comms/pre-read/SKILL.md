# Pre-Read

**PRE-READ — 2–5 pages, reader-complete, no voice-over.** The MD sends it before the sitting. The room discusses; it does not receive a tour. Default: venture exec/program hour. Same spine for F500 (seven days out; Amazon six pages is the ceiling, not the target).

Method origin: Bezos 2017 + Jassy 2024 (narrative, max six pages, silent study hall, PowerPoint stopped **2004**; a high-standard memo takes **a week or more**) + Duarte Slidedocs (handed out and **read**, not projected) + Army/Air Force BLUF + NACD/Board Intelligence 2024 (**13%** packs extremely effective; **59%** three+ concerns). Pyramid is the thinking route — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md) — not this pack.

If they want an Amazon-memo lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Sitting is dated and the pack must be finishable alone before anyone speaks
- A live deck is being emailed “as the pre-read”
- A 40-page working file is labelled pre-read, or “we’ll walk them through it”

## When not to use

- FOR DECISION paper with a minutable resolution — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- SteerCo sitting, up to 3 decisions — [Steering Pack](../../project/steering-pack/SKILL.md)
- Staffer → principal one-pager — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Pyramid email / Slack — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- What you will *say* in 8–12 minutes — [Room Narrative](../../comms/room-narrative/SKILL.md)
- Spoken board 8 minutes — [Board Spoken](../../comms/board-spoken/SKILL.md)
- Product working-backwards — [PR/FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Sitting dated; a governing thought exists (or a hole) | 2–5 page pre-read + one ASK of the sitting |
| **redline** | They pasted a deck or a 40-page file labelled pre-read | Force reader-complete prose/slidedoc; kill voice-over |
| **refuse** | Live deck as pre-read; “we’ll walk them through it”; no sitting date | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **before the room**. The meeting uses a different artifact. Do not re-present this pack.
2. **Reader-complete.** If they need you in the room to understand it, it is not a pre-read.
3. **2–5 pages.** Amazon six is the ceiling. A 40-page working file is refuse.
4. No live deck emailed as pre-read. NACD: slides used as information must be understandable **without a voice-over**, or they travel with a memo.
5. Governing thought in **sentence one** (BLUF). Then: why now; recommendation or frame; 3–5 numbered evidence points a director can cite; the ask of the sitting; what is *not* in this pack.
6. Author **team**, not a hero. Still name an owner on the ASK.
7. Do not invent facts. Holes stay holes.
8. High-standard memo: **a week or more** (Bezos). Tonight is a labelled draft or an issues list — not a fake pack.

## Intake

If **sitting date + purpose** and **the ask of the sitting (or “inform / no ask”)** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake pack.

1. Meeting name; date of sitting; decide / discuss / inform (load-bearing)
2. Governing thought / recommendation — or hole
3. Why this is on the agenda *now*
4. 3–5 evidence points a director/exec can cite — or “not listed”
5. Explicit ask of the sitting (load-bearing with 1) — or labelled “inform”
6. Audience and how they read (prose vs slidedoc); days until sitting
7. What is *not* in this pack (appendix / live narrative / Q cards)

## Output shape

```
PRE-READ  |  [meeting]  |  sitting: [date]  |  purpose: decide / discuss / inform
From: [team]     Owner of ASK: [name]     Distributed: [date]  (target: 7 days prior)

GOVERNING THOUGHT (sentence one)
[one sentence]

WHY THIS, WHY NOW
[2–4 sentences]

RECOMMENDATION / FRAME
[what you want the room to do or how to hold the issue]

EVIDENCE (3–5, citable in the room)
1. [fact — source]
2. [ ]
3. [ ]

ASK OF THIS SITTING
[decide X by [date] / discuss Y / note Z]     Owner: [ ]     Date: [ ]

NOT IN THIS PACK
Live narrative → room-narrative / board-spoken. Q cards → board-qa-prep. Appendix: [ ].

HOLES: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. 2–5 pages, reader-complete, no voice-over required.
2. Governing thought is sentence one.
3. One ASK (or labelled inform) + named owner + sitting date.
4. Not a live deck, not 40 pages, not “we’ll walk them through it.”
5. Not an executive-board-memo, steering-pack, or principal-brief.
6. No invented facts.

If 1, 3, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Live deck emailed as the pre-read → refuse.
- FOR DECISION resolution → [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md).
- “We’ll walk them through it” → refuse; rewrite until it stands alone, or stop.
- They did not read: silent study hall at the start — not a 40-slide read-aloud.
