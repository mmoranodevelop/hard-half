# Economic Buyer

**Economic Buyer 30-Minute Agenda — Ask + Proof** — one page both sides can run: UFC (purpose, time, outcomes including no), *their* economic criteria (cost, time-to-value, team confidence) — 4 questions max, one permissioned proof, one ask with date, paper-process confirmation, what we will not cover (product tour, full demo, QBR). Default: venture (founder often *is* the EB — still run 30 minutes, not a tour). Same spine for F500 (scarce EB time; same one-ask).

Method origin: MEDDICC (training vendor) Economic Buyer + 30-minute UFC + one proof / one ask. https://meddicc.com/what-is-meddpicc/economic-buyer

If they want an EB / MEDDICC lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A named (or evidenced) EB meeting is this week and needs an agenda
- The Champion has offered an intro and we would otherwise demo the product
- Proof and one ask are ready; 30 minutes is the clock
- Discovery named a *suspected* EB and we now have the meeting

## When not to use

- Notes from one discovery call, suspected EB only — [Discovery Call](../../comms/discovery-call/SKILL.md)
- Briefing *our* principal — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- *Our* exec joining the pursuit — [Exec Sponsor Live](../../commercial/exec-sponsor-live/SKILL.md)
- Quarterly account review — [QBR](../../management/qbr/SKILL.md)
- Champion leave-behind — [Champion Coach](../../comms/champion-coach/SKILL.md)
- Demo as story — [Demo Narrative](../../comms/demo-narrative/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named EB (or evidenced guess), one ask, one proof | 30-min agenda + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a demo agenda or QBR scorecard labelled “EB meeting” | Kill the tour; force criteria / proof / one ask |
| **refuse** | Two of named EB / single ask / one proof / Champion path missing; or they want a product tour | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **30 minutes**. One ask. Not a tour. Person + date + yes/no or paper step.
2. MEDDICC (training vendor): EB = overall authority; yes when others say no and no when others say yes; P&L / discretionary funds / veto. Unlikely to self-identify. Speak **costs, time-to-value, team confidence** — “resist the urge to talk about how great your solution is.” Do not invent MEDDICC’s “five things to avoid” (titles only on the fetched page).
3. **Champion is the path in.** Unwilling or unable to introduce = not a true Champion. Stop and run [Champion Coach](../../comms/champion-coach/SKILL.md).
4. Gong (vendor): C-suite discovery — win rates drop after ~**8** questions; optimal ~**4**. This is not a demo.
5. Salesforce State of Sales 6th ed. (Connected Customer on-page): **86%** more likely to buy when goals understood; **59%** say most reps don’t. The 30 minutes are their goals, our proof, one ask.
6. CEB 2015: the solo “senior decision-maker” is gone; execs care about **organizational buy-in**, not the offering. Agenda includes proof the group can buy. CEB 2016: **Prescribe**. CEB 2016 PR: stakeholders **5.4 → 6.8**; **51%** will not advocate.
7. Miller Heiman four buying influences are widely published summaries only. Korn Ferry owns the IP — **no KF-only numbers**. Do not mash MEDDICC Champion with Miller Heiman Coach; name the person and the *function*.
8. Do not invent the EB’s name, proof, or ask. If proof is not ready, do not take the meeting. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake agenda.

1. Named EB, or evidence we are guessing (first ask then becomes “are you the person who can say yes when others say no?”) — load-bearing
2. Single ask that fits 30 minutes (decision, intro, or paper-process start) — load-bearing
3. One proof with a metric, or “proof not ready — do not take the meeting yet” — load-bearing
4. Champion path: who set the meeting and what they need from it — load-bearing
5. Their economic criteria as known (cost / TTV / confidence)
6. Paper process confirmation needed Y/N
7. Meeting date this week + who attends (our exec Y/N → still this agenda, not [Exec Sponsor Live](../../commercial/exec-sponsor-live/SKILL.md) as a substitute)

## Output shape

```
ECONOMIC BUYER 30-MINUTE AGENDA  |  [account]  |  date: [day]  |  EB: [name / title]
Champion path: [name] — introduced Y/N     If N: stop — not a Champion
UFC: purpose [ ]  time 30 min  outcomes including no: [ ]

MINUTE 0–5  UFC — purpose, time, possible yes / no
MINUTE 5–20  Their criteria (≤4 questions) + one proof + paper process
  Cost: [ ]     Time-to-value: [ ]     Team confidence: [ ]
  Proof (permissioned M1): [customer / metric / window / permission]
  Paper process: [process / people / timing]
MINUTE 20–30  One ask + who else must be in the room (CEB consensus)
  Ask: [one sentence + date]
  Who else: [names / functions]     Buying group: [n]

WILL NOT COVER: product tour, full demo, QBR scorecard, price horse-trade

NOT THIS PAGE: suspected EB notes → discovery-call; our principal → principal-brief; our exec’s calendar → exec-sponsor-live; quarterly review → qbr

ASK: [speaker] runs this 30 min on [date]. EB response: [yes / no / paper step]
Owner: [ ]  Date: [ ]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. EB is a name, or the first ask tests whether they are the EB.
2. One ask, 30 minutes, not a tour.
3. One proof with metric — or explicit “proof not ready, do not meet.”
4. Champion path on the page; no intro → not a Champion.
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not discovery notes, not a principal brief, not a QBR, not a demo.
7. One page.

If 2, 3, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Demo the product to the EB → refuse.
- Champion will not introduce → stop; [Champion Coach](../../comms/champion-coach/SKILL.md).
- Two load-bearing fields missing → issues list.
- They want a methodology class → one paragraph then produce or stop.
