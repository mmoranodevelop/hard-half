# Follow-Up 48h

**48-Hour Follow-Up — Decision / Owners / Next Date** — a sendable note (email length, one screen): decision(s) in their words, owner–action–date for *both* sides, next date on the calendar, open objection or paper-process step if any, one ask (confirm / send / introduce). No transcript. No CRM dump. Default: venture (same-day is typical). Same spine for F500 (vendor-eval may use the 48h outer bound).

Method origin: G2 executive timing windows (2h / 24h / 48h) + owner–action–date as the sendable record. https://learn.g2.com/follow-up-email-after-meeting

If they want a follow-up lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- A live sales meeting just happened and the buyer-facing note is not sent
- Owners in the room were “the team”
- The last note was “great to connect” with no decision and no next date
- We are still inside 48 hours of the meeting

## When not to use

- Internal decision minutes — [Meeting Notes To Decisions](../../writing/meeting-notes-to-decisions/SKILL.md)
- Delivery / account incident — [Client Escalation](../../accounts/client-escalation/SKILL.md)
- Buried prose to rewrite — [Pyramid Principle](../../writing/pyramid-principle/SKILL.md)
- Post-signature start — [Kickoff Pack](../../project/kickoff-pack/SKILL.md)
- Multi-week joint plan — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- The live ask itself — [Close Ask](../../comms/close-ask/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Meeting this week; decision (or explicit none) + owners + next date writable | Sendable note + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a transcript, CRM dump, or “just circling back” | Force decision / owners / next date; kill the novel |
| **refuse** | Two of decision / named owners+dates / next date / 48h window missing; or they want internal minutes labelled as the buyer note | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. Clock is **48 hours outer bound** of the same meeting. G2 (public, compiled from business executives): high-stakes / hot sales **within 2 hours**; regular client **24 hours**; partnership / vendor-evaluation **48 hours**. If later, it is a new touch, not this skill.
2. Person + this-week date + yes/no, ask, or number. A note that only says “great to connect” is a Continuation in writing (SPIN, Huthwaite training vendor).
3. At least one **decision** — or explicit “no decision — we declined to pretend there was one.” Recap rejected alternatives when they exist (G2).
4. **Named owners + dates on both sides.** Assign to persons, not teams (G2). SPIN: if the buyer owns no action, it is a Continuation. HBR (Paul Axtell, 30 Mar 2017): assigning tasks and deadlines in the room is **usually not enough** — the sendable note is the extra loop.
5. **Next date** (a day) on the calendar.
6. Gong (vendor): next steps must be discussed on the call; winning demos reserve ~**4 minutes**; feature-dump reduces follow-up-call likelihood. The email is the written twin of that 4 minutes, not a transcript. Salesforce State of Sales 6th ed.: reps spend **30%** of the week selling and **10%** on quotes/approvals — this note is a talk artifact, not a CRM novel. Unanswered objections get an owner ([Objection Map](../../comms/objection-map/SKILL.md)).
7. MEDDICC (training vendor): engagement ≠ progress. The note must show progress against Decision Process or Paper Process.
8. Do not invent decisions, owners, or dates. Holes stay holes. If the thread goes quiet: one dated park-it line — not five check-ins (no invented statistic).

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake note.

1. At least one decision, or explicit “no decision” — load-bearing
2. Named owners + dates on both sides (buyer must own an action) — load-bearing
3. Next date (day) — load-bearing
4. Send-time still inside **48 hours** of the live meeting — load-bearing
5. Meeting type: hot sales / regular / vendor-eval (sets 2h / 24h / 48h)
6. Open objection or paper-process step, if any
7. One ask (confirm / send / introduce)

## Output shape

```
48-HOUR FOLLOW-UP — DECISION / OWNERS / NEXT DATE
To: [buyer names]     From: [us]     Meeting: [day + time]     Send by: [2h / 24h / 48h]
Subject: [decision in the subject line — not “great to connect”]

DECISIONS (their words)
- [decision 1]
- Rejected alternatives: [ ] or none

OWNER – ACTION – DATE (both sides; persons, not teams)
| Owner | Side (buyer/us) | Action | Date |
| [name] | buyer | [ ] | [day] |
| [name] | us | [ ] | [day] |

NEXT DATE ON THE CALENDAR: [day]
Open objection / paper step: [owner + next line] or none
SPIN ending we are recording: Order / Advance / Continuation (failure) / No-sale

ONE ASK: [confirm / send / introduce] by [date]

NOT THIS PAGE: internal minutes → meeting-notes-to-decisions; incident → client-escalation; rewrite tool → pyramid-principle; post-signature → kickoff-pack; the live ask → close-ask

ASK: [sender] sends this note by [deadline inside 48h].
Owner: [ ]  Date: [ ]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. A decision, or explicit “no decision.”
2. Named persons + dates on both sides; buyer owns an action.
3. Next date is a day.
4. Send-time inside 48 hours — else not this skill.
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not internal minutes, an escalation, a kickoff pack, or a transcript.
7. One screen.

If 1, 2, 3, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- “Just circling back” / CRM novel / transcript → refuse.
- Owners = “the team” → refuse.
- Outside 48 hours → not this skill; new touch.
- They want a methodology class → one paragraph then produce or stop.
