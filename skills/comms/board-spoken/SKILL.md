# Board Spoken

**BOARD SPOKEN — 8 minutes, directors-as-directors.** One page. Assume they have the paper. Say so. Remaining time is theirs. Default: venture board / IC. Same spine for F500.

Method origin: NACD / Board Intelligence 2024 (**>500** members; **13%** packs extremely effective; **59%** three+ concerns) + Whitepage recap of the same study (pre-read carries context, **live time carries debate**; **50%** of packs still slides-to-present-live; distribute **seven days** prior). BLUF for the first 30 seconds. Sparkline only as contrast, not TED. Pyramid is the thinking route, not this page.

If they want a board-presentation lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Board / IC sitting is dated, the paper exists (or is in flight), and you have ~8 minutes before challenge
- The draft is a walkthrough of exhibits or last quarter’s tour
- Need a minutable ask spoken, matching the paper

## When not to use

- The decision / noting paper — [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md)
- Hostile questions, 30-second answers — [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md)
- Monthly investor letter — [Investor Update](../../writing/investor-update/SKILL.md)
- 8–12 min in an *operating* room — [Room Narrative](../../comms/room-narrative/SKILL.md)
- Live hostile sitting after cards exist — [Hostile Room](../../comms/hostile-room/SKILL.md)
- The 2–5 page pack before the room — [Pre-Read](../../comms/pre-read/SKILL.md)
- Startup “board update” as a pitch — refuse

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Paper exists or is named; sitting dated | 8-minute spoken page + minutable ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a 20-slide live deck or a paper read-aloud | Cut exhibits; restore headline + judgement + ask |
| **refuse** | No paper and no sitting; pitch-as-board; reading the paper aloud | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Paper first.** Spoken does not substitute for the record directors discharge their duty on.
2. **Eight minutes, then sit down.** If you fill a 30-minute slot with 28 minutes of talking, you stole the challenge.
3. Open: “You’ve seen the analysis. I’ll use eight minutes on the three points that need discussion.” **No walkthrough of exhibits.**
4. Same facts as the paper. Write as if a court will read the spoken claims against the minutes.
5. Shape: 30s headline (recommendation / resolution sought) → 2 min why this agenda, why now → 5 min judgement (options including do-nothing, material risk, stop/start/do differently) → 30s restated minutable ask.
6. Q cards in parallel — [Board Q&A Prep](../../writing/board-qa-prep/SKILL.md). This page is not Q&A.
7. Do not invent numbers. Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **sitting date** and **the resolution / noting ask** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake spine.

1. Board / IC; sitting date; minutes on the clock (load-bearing)
2. Paper: [Executive Board Memo](../../management/executive-board-memo/SKILL.md) or [Pre-Read](../../comms/pre-read/SKILL.md) — exists / in flight
3. Resolution or noting ask (load-bearing with 1)
4. What changed — why this report, why now
5. Options including do-nothing; material risk; what management will stop / start / do differently
6. Q cards exist? Y/N
7. Speaker; the one number directors should remember — or hole

## Output shape

```
BOARD SPOKEN  |  [board / IC]  |  sitting: [date]  |  8 minutes then sit
Speaker: [ ]     Paper: [memo / pre-read title]     Q cards: exist / write first

0:00–0:30  HEADLINE
Resolution / noting: [minutable sentence].
Line: “You’ve seen the analysis. Eight minutes on the three points that need discussion.”

0:30–2:30  WHY THIS AGENDA, WHY NOW
[what changed]

2:30–7:30  JUDGEMENT
Options (incl. do-nothing): [ ]
Material risk: [ ]
Stop / start / do differently: [ ]
The number: [ ]

7:30–8:00  ASK RESTATED (minutable)
ASK: [approve / note / send back]     Owner: [ ]     Date: [ ]
NOT SAID: exhibit tour, org history, last-quarter walkthrough, upside pitch.
HOLES: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. 8 minutes with time marks. Sit down after.
2. Headline + minutable ASK + owner + sitting date.
3. Assumes the paper. No exhibit walkthrough.
4. Same facts as the paper (or labelled hole).
5. Not executive-board-memo, not Q cards, not a pitch, not room-narrative.
6. No invented numbers.

If 1, 2, 3, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Reading the paper aloud → refuse.
- No paper → write the paper first, or stop.
- Pitch-as-board-update → refuse.
