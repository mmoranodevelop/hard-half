# Partner Exit

**Partner / channel exit pack — one page** — named partner, contract path (convenience / cause / non-renew), notice & cure, customer continuity, IP & data, economics wind-down, message trio, one ASK. Default: SaaS / ISV channel or alliance exit; same spine for distributor / reseller / technology partnership.

Method origin: public channel-exit playbooks (Umbrex lifecycle & exit: notice/cure, customer continuity, replacement coverage) + high-level termination checklists (read the master agreement first; convenience vs cause vs expiry) + SaaS/public partner terms patterns (e.g. HubSpot Solutions Partner: notice periods, effects on end-user subscriptions) + exit/transition-assistance clause primers (data return, transition services). Reconstruct the operator pack. Do not practise law.

If they want a termination-law lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Decision is made (or nearly made) to exit a **named** partner / channel relationship
- Notice window or auto-renew is approaching and exit must be planned
- Performance failure, strategy change, or M&A forces unwind
- Customers, IP, MDF/rebates, and message are still unowned
- Transition assistance is undefined and leverage is about to hit zero

## When not to use

- Two partners fighting over **one account this week** — [Channel Conflict](../../commercial/channel-conflict/SKILL.md)
- Whether to **enter / hire / vendor / partner** with a counterparty — [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md)
- Saving a **customer** renewal — [Churn Save](../../accounts/churn-save/SKILL.md)
- Live vendor SOW change mid-delivery — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md) / [Change Control](../../project/change-control/SKILL.md)
- Post-M&A contract move — [Contract Novation](../../ma/contract-novation/SKILL.md)

If the partner is unnamed or exit is still "maybe later" with no date, stop.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named partner + agreement access | Exit pack + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted "just stop paying / cut access" | Force notice path, customers, IP; kill cowboy exit |
| **refuse** | No agreement, or dual-quote war mislabelled as exit | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Read the termination clause first.** Path: convenience / cause (+ cure) / non-renewal. Wrong path = wrongful-termination risk (public primers). Not legal advice — flag for counsel.
2. **Notice and cure on the page** with calendar dates. Auto-renew windows are load-bearing.
3. **Customers first.** Named top accounts, who owns the relationship post-exit, one customer message, replacement coverage or HOLE.
4. **IP, data, access.** Return/destruction, brand/license wind-down, portal keys, MDF creatives — owner + date.
5. **Economics wind-down.** Open orders, unpaid commissions/rebates, clawbacks, transition services fees — numbers from books or HOLE. Do not invent.
6. **Message trio:** partner notice (factual), internal field script, customer continuity note. No smear.
7. **One ASK** — named commercial lead / GC / MD to authorize notice on date X under path Y.
8. **Do not** run a live two-quote conflict here — that is [Channel Conflict](../../commercial/channel-conflict/SKILL.md).
9. **Do not** re-open "should we partner" — that was [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md); this is exit.
10. **One page** (+ optional customer annex).

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake exit.

1. Named partner + agreement (or clear HOLE that counsel must fetch) — load-bearing
2. Exit reason (performance / strategy / cause evidence / convenience) — load-bearing
3. Desired effective date / notice deadline — load-bearing
4. Top customers touched by this partner — load-bearing
5. Open economics (orders, rebates, MDF, commissions)
6. IP / data / portal inventory
7. Replacement route (direct, other partner, none)
8. Who signs the notice (and whether GC engaged)

## QA (must pass)

1. Named partner; path and notice dates (or HOLE + counsel fetch).
2. Customer continuity table or explicit HOLE on top accounts.
3. IP/data/access wind-down owned.
4. Economics from books or labelled HOLE — no invented $.
5. Message trio named.
6. One ASK: authorize notice, path, date.
7. "Not legal advice" on page.
8. Not channel-conflict, not partner-memo, not churn-save.

If 1, 2, 6, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Cause evidence thin but they insist on cause → counsel; prefer convenience if available.
- Partner may sue / regulated channel (franchise, insurance, health) → GC before notice.
- Customers already churning → still this pack for partner exit; parallel [Churn Save](../../accounts/churn-save/SKILL.md) per customer.
- Live quote war this week → [Channel Conflict](../../commercial/channel-conflict/SKILL.md) first; exit later.
