# Bid No-Bid

**Pursuit Brief — Bid / Conditional / No-Bid** — one page before anyone opens the response template: opportunity, buyer, deadline, fit, Pwin evidence, competitive position, hours/$ to respond, delivery cost if won, expected margin, named decision-maker, decision, conditions if conditional. Default: venture RFP; same spine for F500.

Method origin: APMP-NCA Go/No-Go (final decision point, mixed-incentive decision-maker, costed qualification) + Shipley-style weighted bid/no-bid matrix as summarized on a public proposal-ops page.

If they want a capture-process lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- An RFP / RFI / bid invitation just arrived and nobody has spent proposal hours
- The hunter wants to bid; capacity, fit, or margin is in doubt
- Three live responses are already in flight and this one would lower their win rate
- A prior "we'll decide while we draft" burned a week of SMEs

## When not to use

- After the outcome — [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md)
- Joint close plan on a deal you already chose to pursue — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- How a named rival wins (input to the scorecard, not the decision) — [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md)
- Non-standard terms once you have already bid — [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md)
- This week's Commit on a live pursuit — [Forecast Call](../../commercial/forecast-call/SKILL.md)
- Named exec on a live pursuit you already bid — [Exec Sponsor Live](../../commercial/exec-sponsor-live/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Opportunity registered; deadline known | Bid / Conditional / No-Bid brief + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a "we should bid" email or a half-filled scorecard | Force the six criteria + hours + named D; kill sunk-cost |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or drafting has already started "while we decide" | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Clock is before proposal hours.** RocketDocs: a single enterprise response can consume 20–40 hours across sales, SMEs, legal, and proposal. A no-bid after 30 hours of drafting is waste, not this skill.
2. **One final decision point.** APMP-NCA: after the gate the decision cannot be challenged, or teams spin until the deadline makes a quality response impossible. No friendly-exec override of a no-bid.
3. **Named D, not the hunter alone.** Account owner + proposal lead complete the scorecard; a council (sales + finance + proposal-resource owner) or a named person issues bid / conditional / no-bid. Decision-makers must not be paid solely on revenue.
4. **Six criteria, no single row decides it.** Relationship, solution fit, competitive position, resources/timeline, strategic value, profitability. Shipley-style weighted matrix; threshold agreed *in advance*.
5. **Cost the response and the win.** Fit score, contract size, labor hours, $ cost of response, implementation cost if won, expected profitability. Missing hours is a hole, not "we'll figure it out."
6. **Conditional names 2–3 gaps** to close *before* proposal kickoff (teaming, past performance, price-to-win). Conditional is not "start drafting."
7. **No-bid is communicated** with the data, credit to the hunter for the relationship, and a record leadership can reuse.
8. **Incumbent renewal is not an automatic bid.** Ignoring capacity is a named mistake.
9. **Do not invent Pwin, hours, or margin.** Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake brief.

1. Named decision-maker (person or council) who can issue a final no-bid — load-bearing
2. Scored read across relationship / fit / competitive / capacity / strategic / profit (or fit score + cost-to-respond) — load-bearing
3. Response hours and $ and RFP deadline — load-bearing
4. A decision date that is before drafting starts — load-bearing
5. Buyer, opportunity size, incumbent / competitive position
6. Other in-flight responses that this bid would starve
7. Implementation cost if won, or "unknown — hole"

## QA (must pass)

1. Named D who can issue a final no-bid.
2. Decision date is before drafting.
3. Hours and $ to respond are numbers or HOLE — not "we'll see."
4. Six criteria scored, or an equivalent fit + cost. No single-row override.
5. Conditional has 2–3 named gaps, or the page is bid / no-bid.
6. ASK + owner + date.
7. Not a win-loss, not a MAP, not a teardown.
8. No invented Pwin, hours, or margin.
9. One page.

If 1, 2, 6, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- "Start writing while we decide" → refuse. That is the sunk-cost trap APMP-NCA and RocketDocs both name.
- Loudest voice overrides the score → refuse; council or named D decides.
- Incumbent renewal as automatic bid with no capacity read → refuse.
- They already submitted → [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md) after the outcome, not this page.
