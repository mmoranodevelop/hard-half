# Exec Sponsor Live

**Live Pursuit — Named Exec Sponsor Brief** — one page: pursuit, amount, close clock, named seller-side exec, named buyer-side Economic Buyer and peer altitude, Mobilizer/Champion status, one ask for the meeting, what the exec must not commit (price, SLA, dates), meeting date this cycle, last contact. Default: venture pursuit; same spine for F500.

Method origin: Miller Heiman Economic Buyer (peer altitude) + MEDDICC "No Champion, no deal" / Champion must introduce the EB + CEB/Challenger consensus buying groups (5.4→6.8; 30% likelihood above five stakeholders) + Gartner 2025 buying-group conflict.

If they want an exec-sponsorship lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Live pursuit, buying group larger than one, Economic Buyer not yet engaged at altitude
- Champion exists but has not introduced the EB
- One matching-altitude seller exec can take *this* meeting this cycle
- Forecast is treating the deal as sponsored when no peer meeting has happened

## When not to use

- Program / org alignment, not one pursuit — [Stakeholder Alignment](../../management/stakeholder-alignment/SKILL.md)
- Briefing a principal for a general appearance — [Principal Brief](../../writing/principal-brief/SKILL.md)
- Post-close coverage owner — [Named Account Day 1](../../ma/named-account-day1/SKILL.md)
- Joint close plan once the EB is engaged — [Mutual Action Plan](../../accounts/mutual-action-plan/SKILL.md)
- Price / SLA / dates the exec must not commit — [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md)
- This week's Commit number — [Forecast Call](../../commercial/forecast-call/SKILL.md)
- Year-horizon account design — [Account Plan](../../accounts/account-plan/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Live pursuit; both sides named or nameable | Brief + one ask + do-not-commit + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a RACI or "CRO is sponsor" on the opp | Force a scheduled peer meeting; kill silent names |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or they want the C-suite sprayed | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Clock is this pursuit.** One named exec, one meeting, one ask. Using a CRO on every deal is a program, not this skill.
2. **Both names.** Seller-side exec who has *accepted* the meeting (not a title on a RACI). Buyer-side Economic Buyer or Mobilizer the meeting is for (MEDDICC EB / Challenger Mobilizer). Miller Heiman: the EB releases funds; a Coach who "basically makes the call" is usually not the signer.
3. **Silent is worse than none.** A seller-side name on the deal record who has never spoken to the buyer EB is a false Champion in reverse. If the exec cannot do the meeting this cycle, **remove the name**.
4. **Champion must introduce the EB.** MEDDICC: "No Champion, no deal." A Champion who will not introduce the EB is not a Champion. Seller exec is the peer door, not a substitute for a Champion.
5. **One ask.** Not a product demo the AE should have run. CEB: executives on the buy side care whether you have organizational buy-in — *not* the offering.
6. **Do not commit price, SLA, or dates.** Those go to [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md). Write the do-not-commit list on the page.
7. **Do not pitch one stakeholder's personal win.** Gartner 2025: buying groups 5–16 people; 74% show unhealthy conflict; individual-level relevance has a 59% *negative* impact on consensus; buying-group relevance helps +20%. CEB: purchase likelihood drops to 30% when more than five stakeholders are involved; 51% willing to buy are not willing to advocate.
8. **Meeting this cycle.** Recap in writing to AE + legal/finance if any commercial topic arose.
9. **Do not invent buyer names or meeting dates.** Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **two** of 1–4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake brief.

1. Named seller-side executive who has accepted the meeting — load-bearing
2. Named buyer-side Economic Buyer or Mobilizer the meeting is for — load-bearing
3. One ask and a date in this pursuit's clock — load-bearing
4. Evidence this is a live pursuit (open opportunity, active buying group) — load-bearing
5. Champion / Mobilizer status (will they introduce the EB?)
6. Amount, close clock, last contact
7. Do-not-commit list (price / SLA / dates) already in play, or "none yet"

```
LIVE PURSUIT — NAMED EXEC SPONSOR BRIEF  |  [pursuit]  |  $ [ ]  |  close: [date]
Seller exec (accepted): [name / title]     Buyer EB / Mobilizer: [name / title]
Peer altitude: [matched Y/N]     Champion: [name] — will introduce EB? Y/N
Meeting: [date this cycle]     Last contact: [date / none]

ONE ASK (not a demo): [one sentence]
Buying group (named if known): [n] across [functions]     Conflict flags: [ ]

DO NOT COMMIT (deal-desk, not this meeting)
Price: [ ]     SLA: [ ]     Dates: [ ]

NOT THIS PAGE
Program map → stakeholder-alignment    General appearance → principal-brief    Post-close owner → named-account-day1    Terms → deal-desk

ASK: [AE] + [seller exec] complete the peer meeting by [date this cycle]. Recap in writing.
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Seller exec is a name who accepted, not a RACI title.
2. Buyer EB or Mobilizer is a name, not "the committee."
3. One ask, one sentence.
4. Meeting date this cycle — or the name is removed.
5. Do-not-commit list is on the page.
6. ASK + owner + date.
7. Not a program stakeholder map, not a principal appearance brief, not Day-1 coverage.
8. No invented names or dates.
9. One page.

If 1, 2, 4, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- CRO's name on the opportunity with no scheduled peer meeting → refuse (silent sponsor). It pollutes [Forecast Call](../../commercial/forecast-call/SKILL.md) and burns C-level scarcity.
- Spray the C-suite → refuse; one matching-altitude exec.
- Exec wants to "flex on price" in the room → route terms to [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md); do not ship a brief that authorizes it.
- Post-close coverage assignment → [Named Account Day 1](../../ma/named-account-day1/SKILL.md).
