# Portfolio Keep-Kill

**Keep/kill/combine/harvest card** — every SKU or module gets one label, a date, a named owner, and the sentence customers will hear. No "under review." Default: venture close; same spine for F500.

Method origin: West Monroe public (invest A, ramp B, shut C, integrate D into E) + BCG software PMI (do not pass product decisions to the future).

If they want a portfolio-rationalisation lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

**Clock.** Week 1 = continuity (what is **not** dying this month). Quarter 1 = labels. Do not sunset in week 1.

## When to use

- Two product lines meet after a close and SKUs overlap or sit side-by-side
- Sales is quoting both; customers ask "are you killing X?"
- Q1 (by Day 90): every SKU needs a label, owner, and customer sentence

## When not to use

- Stop rule for **one venture** — [Kill Criteria](../../strategy/kill-criteria/SKILL.md)
- Gate sitting across bets — [Portfolio Stage-Gate](../../management/portfolio-stage-gate/SKILL.md)
- Combined 2Q ships / freeze — [Joint Roadmap Q1](../../ma/joint-roadmap-q1/SKILL.md)
- Hold / absorb / merge the **company** — [Operating Model Choice](../../ma/operating-model-choice/SKILL.md)
- 100-day outcomes + kill-old-path — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- Week-1 who owns the logo — [Named Account Day 1](../../ma/named-account-day1/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Q1 labels, or week-1 "not dying" sentence | Filled card + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a "synergy" deck or "under review" list | Force one label per SKU; kill empty cells |
| **refuse** | Week-1 sunset; kill with no path; two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Four labels only.** Keep (invest; named on the 2Q roadmap). Kill (stop selling; last-sell + last-support + migration path). Combine (one go-forward name; features of D into E). Harvest (sell, do not build; freeze the roadmap).
2. **No "under review."** A hole is a hole with an owner and a decide-by. It is not a fifth label.
3. **Kill without a path is a refuse.** Last-sell, last-support, and what the customer moves to — or do not ship the Kill row.
4. **Customer sentence is load-bearing.** What we will say. Do not say "synergy", "rationalise", or "nothing will change."
5. **Week 1: do not sunset.** Produce only: what is not dying this month + date of the Q1 card. Live Kill rows in Day 0–7 → refuse.
6. **Overlap in one line:** complementary / substitute / neither. Christensen public: if you bought a growth trajectory, do not mash the target's products into the buyer's process.
7. **Do not invent SKU lists or ARR.** Hole stays hole.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake card.

1. Deal + close date (or Day n) — load-bearing
2. SKU / module list, both sides — load-bearing
3. Overlap: complementary / substitute / neither (guess labelled is fine)
4. What you will say to customers this month — load-bearing
5. Any already-sold commitments that block a Kill
6. Owner of the ASK (CPO / GM) and decide-by

## QA (must pass)

1. Every SKU has one of Keep / Kill / Combine / Harvest — not "under review".
2. Every Kill row has last-sell, last-support, and a path.
3. Customer sentence present; no synergy / nothing-will-change.
4. No week-1 live sunset.
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not a kill-criteria card or a stage-gate paper.
7. No invented ARR or SKU names.
8. One page.

If 1, 2, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Sunset / rebrand / reprice / rewrite the stack in week 1 → refuse; week 1 is continuity.
- Kill with no migration → refuse.
- They want the 18-month vision rewritten here → [Joint Roadmap Q1](../../ma/joint-roadmap-q1/SKILL.md).
- They cannot name SKUs after one ask → issues list.
