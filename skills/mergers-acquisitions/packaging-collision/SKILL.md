# Packaging Collision

**Packaging collision map** — two price lists, every collision, the quote rule until [date], grandfather, first date we may change. Default: venture close.

Method origin: BCG clean-team public (product pricing differences ready for Day 1, or customers stall) + public grandfather craft (same-or-better until a dated window, not a surprise invoice).

If they want a price-harmonisation lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

**Clock.** Week 1 = freeze list, metric, and discount policy for named accounts; grandfather. **Do not reprice.** Quarter 1 = first change at **renewal**, with a date. New ladder is [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md), not this week's invoice.

## When to use

- Same job, two metrics / two "Pro" fences / bundle vs à la carte
- Two reps can quote the same RFP differently tomorrow
- Close week needs a freeze so nobody "harmonises" on a call

## When not to use

- Architecture of **one** offer (metric, GBB, fences) — [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Yes/no on **one** named discount — [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md)
- Day-1 owner + script — [Named Account Day 1](../../ma/named-account-day1/SKILL.md)
- Partner program / what still pays — [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md)
- SKU keep / kill — [Portfolio Keep-Kill](../../ma/portfolio-keep-kill/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Collision map + freeze + grandfather + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted "we will align pricing" | Force freeze; kill "pricing review" as a message |
| **refuse** | Week-1 reprice; two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Map before a rep quotes.** Collision types: same job / two metrics; same SKU name / two fences; bundle vs à la carte; channel vs direct; two uplift clauses. Do not true-forward mid-term.
2. **Week 1 freeze.** List, metric, discount policy. Grandfather named accounts. First change timed to a **renewal**, not a surprise invoice.
3. **Do not announce a "pricing review."** Announce a dated change, or say nothing. Customers hear "synergy" as price-up.
4. **Quote rule until [date]** is a sentence a rep can use. Two quotes for one RFP is a fail.
5. **Who may exception** is a name (and [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md) for the one-deal paper).
6. **Grandfather has a date**, like a public same-or-better window. Undated freeze becomes the new list — or it gets walked. Write the clause.
7. **Do not invent list prices or partner margins.** Hole stays hole.
8. New combined ladder is a later pricing-one-pager. This page does not design the offer.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 3 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake freeze.

1. Deal + close date — load-bearing
2. Both price lists (or "we cannot find target list") — load-bearing
3. Named accounts already on a contract — load-bearing
4. Collision types already seen (metric / fence / bundle / channel) or "unknown"
5. Exceptions already promised (commitment register)
6. Partner / list vs street (or "no channel")
7. Owner of ASK (CRO + product ops) and freeze-until date

## QA (must pass)

1. Two offers that do the same job have a quote rule.
2. No week-1 reprice or "harmonise now".
3. Grandfather has a date if any named account is in-term.
4. Message is not "pricing review".
5. ASK + owner + date.
6. Not a pricing-one-pager or a discount-exception.
7. No invented list prices.
8. One page.

If 2, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Reprice / sunset / rebrand / rewrite in week 1 → refuse.
- Quiet price-up on the next invoice → refuse; first change is a renewal.
- One-deal off-list → [Discount Exception](../../strategy/discount-exception/SKILL.md).
- Design the combined ladder → [Pricing One-Pager](../../strategy/pricing-one-pager/SKILL.md).
- Channel rebate math in the close week → [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md).
