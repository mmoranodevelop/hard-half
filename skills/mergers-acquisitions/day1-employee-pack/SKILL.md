# Day-1 Employee Pack

**Legal-cleared Day 1 employee pack** — CEO note + manager cascade (may say / must escalate) + FAQ + next-decision calendar. Three buckets only: **changing / not changing this week / not yet decided + date**. Default: venture close; same spine for F500 with counsel blocking.

Method origin: McKinsey public — do not go dark; do not happy-talk; Day 1 is what's changing and what's not; unions/works councils need legal.

If they want a comms-in-mergers lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close / Day 1 is dated and employees need words they can screenshot
- Announce Day already happened; the close-morning pack is still blank
- Managers are improvising payroll, boss, and "are we safe?"
- Legal has not seen the employee text

## When not to use

- Town hall after a decision already made — [All-Hands](../../writing/all-hands/SKILL.md) (this pack may feed it)
- First hour of a live incident — [Crisis Holding](../../writing/crisis-holding/SKILL.md)
- Who runs the unit — [Day-1 Leadership Slate](../../ma/leadership-day1/SKILL.md) first
- Stay / walk-away lists — [Key Talent Slate](../../ma/key-talent-slate/SKILL.md)
- Payroll/access/systems actually running — [Day-1 Continuity](../../ma/day1-continuity/SKILL.md)
- Ways-of-working collisions — [Culture Fault Lines](../../ma/culture-fault-lines/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Close dated; public why exists | CEO note + cascade + FAQ + calendar + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted happy talk or "nothing changes" | Force three buckets; kill job-security fiction |
| **refuse** | Close date and legal reviewer both missing, or they want "your jobs are safe" the model cannot keep | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Three buckets.** Changing this week / not changing this week / not yet decided **with a date**. A fourth bucket called "we'll see" is a fail.
2. **Never invent certainty.** If the org is unstaffed, say the date you will name the boss (from the leadership slate). If you do not know, say the date — not "nothing will change."
3. **No job-security fiction.** If the synergy model includes headcount movement, do not write "your jobs are safe." Allowed: **"no changes before [date]."**
4. **Same one-sentence why as the press release.** Do not get ahead of the 8-K / public line.
5. **Who runs this unit today** is a name (or named interim + date) from [Day-1 Leadership Slate](../../ma/leadership-day1/SKILL.md). People not knowing their boss on Day 1 is a fail.
6. **Legal-cleared before send.** Named reviewer. Works councils / unions / TUPE / WARN = counsel. Pre-close: plan, do not implement or direct the still-independent target.
7. **Hit people before the room.** Walk-aways and new bosses hear it 1:1 first. Then cascade. Then all-hands if you run one.
8. **Manager cascade is mandatory:** three bullets they may say; what they must escalate; where questions go (staffed mailbox + owner).
9. **Do not go dark** between announce and Day 1. A clocked update with "no material change" is still an update.

## Intake

If **Day 1 / close date** and **legal reviewer (or "none — issue")** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Close / Day 1 date (load-bearing)
2. Public one-sentence why (press-release line)
3. Who runs the unit today — name or "slate not signed"
4. What is unchanged this week (payroll, manager, customers, benefits) — facts only
5. What will change, and the date of the next decision
6. Legal reviewer; works council / union / TUPE / WARN — or "none in this jurisdiction"
7. Who is personally hit and have they been told

## Output shape

```
DAY-1 EMPLOYEE PACK  |  [deal / unit]  |  Day 1: [date]
Public why (same as press release): [one sentence]
Legal reviewer: [name]  cleared: Y/N     Pre-close / post-close: [ ]
Unit boss today: [name or interim + date]     Hit people told before send: Y/N

CEO NOTE (send this)
1. What happened, who owns us, the one-sentence why
2. Who runs this unit today
3. NOT CHANGING this week: [payroll date / manager if true / customers / benefits if true]
4. CHANGING: [item] on [date]
5. NOT YET DECIDED: [org / titles / sites] — next decision date [ ]
6. Questions: [staffed mailbox] + your manager

DO NOT SAY
- "Your jobs are safe" / "nothing will change" / a second strategy speech
- Any fact ahead of the public line
- A boss who is TBD

MANAGER CASCADE
You may say: [3 bullets, same facts]
You must escalate (do not improvise): [roles under review, legal, visa, equity, sites]
Questions you cannot answer go to: [owner + mailbox]

FAQ (payroll, benefits, manager, visa, equity, sites)
| Q | A (confirmed) | If unknown, the date we will answer |
| [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

NEXT DECISION CALENDAR
| Topic | Date | Owner | What we will not pretend until then |
| Org / titles / sites / mapping | [ ] | [ ] | [ ]

NOT THIS PAGE
Town hall spine → all-hands
Live incident → crisis-holding
Systems/payroll running → day1-continuity
Stay/walk lists → key-talent-slate

ASK: Legal+[CEO] clear this pack by [date]. Managers briefed [date]. Send [clock, tz].
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Three buckets are non-empty; every "not yet" has a date.
2. Unit boss is a name or interim + date — not TBD.
3. No job-security sentence the model cannot keep.
4. Public why matches the press line (or hole).
5. Legal reviewer named; uncleared pack is not shipped as final.
6. Manager cascade exists (may say / must escalate / where questions go).
7. Hit people before send, or the page says DO NOT SEND.
8. ASK + owner + clock.
9. Not an all-hands pep talk, not a holding statement.
10. No invented facts.

If 1, 3, 5, or 10 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want "nothing changes" while finance models surplus → refuse.
- Pre-close and they want to direct target employees → refuse gun-jumping.
- Decision not made on the boss → finish [Day-1 Leadership Slate](../../ma/leadership-day1/SKILL.md) first; this pack can still say the date.
- Live incident, cause unknown → crisis-holding.
