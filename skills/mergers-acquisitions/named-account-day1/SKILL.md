# Named Account Day 1

**Named-account Day 1 card** — one owner per logo, the sentence that is true / not true / not yet decided, the SLA that still binds. Default: venture close; same spine for F500.

Method origin: BCG public (one Day-1 account owner + specialists) + Bain customers-first (retention in the deal model; honour the stricter contract).

If they want a customer-PMI lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

**Clock.** This **is** the week-1 page. Do not steal AMs the same week as a CRM move. Do not reprice, rebrand, or sunset on this call.

## When to use

- Close is dated (or this week) and named accounts need a call in 8–48h
- Two AMs think they own the same logo, or nobody does
- Customers will hear a rumour unless a script exists

## When not to use

- BAU sales → CS transfer of one account — [Account Handoff](../../accounts/account-handoff/SKILL.md)
- Logo already leaving — [Churn Save](../../accounts/churn-save/SKILL.md)
- 12-month plan for one account — [Account Plan](../../accounts/account-plan/SKILL.md)
- Close go/no-go (payroll, bank, access, comms) — [Day-1 Continuity](../../ma/day1-continuity/SKILL.md)
- Partner owner / what still pays — [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md)
- Price-list collisions — [Packaging Collision](../../ma/packaging-collision/SKILL.md)
- 100-day outcomes — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Close week | Owner map + script + SLA freeze + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a blast email or dual-owner list | One name; kill "nothing will change" |
| **refuse** | Dual owners left standing; two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One owner.** A name, not "the team." Specialists sit behind that name. Dual-call is a fail.
2. **Script is contract-grade, not marketing.** Reasons in the customer's units. What will **not** change before [date]. What is **unknown**, with a date we will know. How to reach us this week (a path that works).
3. **Do not say** "nothing will change", "synergy", "rationalise", "your AM may change", "we'll get back to you."
4. **SLA freeze:** honour the **stricter** of the two contracts until a written novation. Temporary workaround beats a broken promise.
5. **Tier 1 = call**, not a blast. Tier 2 = letter + named backup. Tier 3 = site FAQ.
6. **Giving owner** named + intro date. Silence is how customers assume the worst.
7. **Do not invent ARR or competitor risk.** Hole stays hole.
8. Week-1: freeze product, price, support path. Q1 coverage model is not this page.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake map.

1. Deal + close date — load-bearing
2. Named-account list (Tier 1 at minimum) — load-bearing
3. Current owner on each side (or "unknown")
4. Contract / SLA that still binds (stricter of the two, or hole) — load-bearing
5. What is already decided vs not-yet (roadmap / brand / packaging)
6. Support path that actually works this week
7. Retention KPI already in the deal model (or "none")

## QA (must pass)

1. Every Tier-1 row has exactly one owner.
2. Script does not say "nothing will change" or "synergy".
3. SLA freeze has a clause or a hole.
4. ASK + owner + date.
5. Not an account-handoff, churn-save, or year plan.
6. No invented ARR.
7. One page.

If 1, 2, or 4 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Dual owners after one ask → refuse the map; do not ship "shared".
- Reprice / rebrand / sunset on the Day-1 call → refuse; those are Q1 cards.
- Logo already leaving → [Churn Save](../../accounts/churn-save/SKILL.md).
- Partner overlay asked as this page → [Partner Memo](../../strategy/partner-memo/SKILL.md).
