# Title Grade Map

**Title / grade / pay mapping matrix** — cover sheet (Day-1 freeze + mapping go-live date + exceptions) plus rows: legacy title → family / level → band → red-circle / green-circle / hold → communication date. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500 with Comp/CHRO as D.

Method origin: Deloitte public Day-1 HR — payroll continuity first, then dated harmonisation. Mercer public — mapping takes months, not a close-morning reset.

If they want a job-architecture lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is dated and two title systems will meet in the same meeting
- Someone wants to "fix" titles on Day 1
- Mapping is drifting past 180 days with no go-live
- Red-circle / green-circle incumbents are unnamed

## When not to use

- Run-rate heads, freeze vs hire, loaded cost — [Headcount Plan](../../management/headcount-plan/SKILL.md)
- Opening one role — [Hiring Scorecard](../../management/hiring-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- Stay awards — [Key Talent Slate](../../ma/key-talent-slate/SKILL.md)
- Ways-of-working collisions (travel class as culture, not as a band) — [Culture Fault Lines](../../ma/culture-fault-lines/SKILL.md)
- Surplus process — counsel + [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Steady-state boxes — [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Day 1 dated | Freeze cover + mapping matrix + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a title dump or a Day-1 reset | Force freeze; date the map; mark red/green/hold |
| **refuse** | Freeze confirmation and mapping date both missing, or they want a Day-1 title reset without a legal reason | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Day 1 is continuity of payroll, not a title reset.** Titles and pay stay as they were unless a named legal/entity reason says otherwise. Write the freeze on the cover.
2. **Mapping has a go-live date** (often 60–180 days, not 12 silent months). No date = no map.
3. **Map roles → job family → level/grade → pay band** using scope, decision rights, people leadership — **not** inherited titles. Title inflation on one side is expected; do not copy it.
4. **Red-circle** (above the new band): freeze / lump-sum / time-bound hold — pick one per person or class. **Green-circle** (below): bring-up schedule with dates. "We’ll sort it" is a fail.
5. **Grandfather only on purpose.** Selected benefits can be kept if identity, not if nobody decided. Name the exception log.
6. **Managers do not "fix" inequity with title inflation.** Off-map promotions fail. Visible inequity starts job searches; the answer is the dated map, not a new label.
7. **Do not invent bands.** Hole the $ and the grade if Comp has not issued them.
8. **Do not use this page to surplus people.** Headcount movement is counsel + difficult-conversation, not a grade trick.

## Intake

If **Day-1 freeze (yes/no + reason)** and **mapping go-live date** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Day 1 / close date
2. Freeze confirmation: titles and pay stay on Day 1 — yes / legal exception (load-bearing)
3. Mapping go-live date (load-bearing)
4. Two-side title samples / families they already know — or "unknown"
5. Red-circle / green-circle default policy
6. Who has D (CHRO / Comp)
7. Benefits to grandfather — named or "none"

## QA (must pass)

1. Day-1 freeze is yes or a named legal exception — not a silent reset.
2. Mapping go-live is a date.
3. Rows map to family/level, not "keep their old title forever" unless grandfathered and logged.
4. Red/green/hold is named per row or as a default that applies.
5. No invented bands — HOLE if unknown.
6. ASK + D + date.
7. Not a headcount cost table, not a stay slate.
8. One page + table.

If 1, 2, 5, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want to reset titles on close morning with no legal reason → refuse.
- SPA / works-council / equal-pay lock → counsel; freeze holds; mapping date still required.
- They want this map to select surplus → stop; that is not this skill.
