# Operating Model Choice

**One-page operating-model choice for one deal** — hold / absorb / merge / harvest **per function**, owner, by-when, what must stay autonomous, Day 1 interim vs end-state. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500.

Method origin: Haspeslagh/Jemison 2×2 public summary (strategic interdependence × organisational autonomy → holding / preservation / absorption / symbiosis). Harvest is an operator cell, not a Haspeslagh cell.

If they want to explain Haspeslagh / teach org design: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is coming and "we'll integrate everything" is the plan
- Sales, payroll, product, and IT would get the same answer
- Day 1 interim vs end-state is unnamed
- Hold-separate / ring-fence may apply (remedy, not a value thesis)

## When not to use

- Boxes, spans, vacancies of one team — [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- IMO as value vehicle — [IMO Charter](../../ma/imo-charter/SKILL.md)
- First 100-day outcomes + kill-old-path — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- Who sits in the top two layers Day 1 — [Day-1 Leadership Slate](../../ma/leadership-day1/SKILL.md)
- SKU keep / kill / combine — [Portfolio Keep-Kill](../../ma/portfolio-keep-kill/SKILL.md)
- Close go/no-go (payroll, bank, comms) — [Day-1 Continuity](../../ma/day1-continuity/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named deal, functions listed | One-pager per-function choice + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted "full integration" or one org chart | Force per-function cells + Day 1 vs end-state |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a 2×2 lecture | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One cell per function:** hold / absorb / merge / harvest. Same answer for sales and payroll is a fail unless you write why.
2. **Map the public 2×2, do not dump the book.** hold = holding or preservation; absorb = absorption; merge = symbiosis (slowest). harvest only if the thesis is take cash / cost / capability and **not** build a combined company. Growth thesis + harvest = wrong cell.
3. **Write Day 1 interim vs end-state.** Interim may last; every other plan hangs on the choice.
4. **Hold-separate is a remedy, not a value cell.** If it applies: ring-fence, deny commercial data, separate antitrust team. Do not absorb a hold-separate unit.
5. **Name owner and by-when per row.** What will not change. Holes stay holes.
6. **Top two layers:** point at [Day-1 Leadership Slate](../../ma/leadership-day1/SKILL.md); do not draw the org here.
7. **Do not invent functions, names, or a cell you cannot defend.**

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 4 are missing after one ask: issues list, not a fake 2×2.

1. Deal thesis — growth / capability vs scale / efficiency vs cash takeout — load-bearing
2. Functions in scope — at least finance, HR, product, engineering, sales, IT, legal — load-bearing
3. What must stay autonomous and why (capability you bought)
4. Day 1 interim vs end-state — date or wave for end-state — load-bearing
5. Hold-separate / antitrust remedy? yes / no / unknown
6. Top two layers named? if no, hole — do not invent; route leadership
7. Venture or F500 (venture default)

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK (D, lock cells, date).
2. Per-function rows, not one cell for the whole company.
3. Same cell for sales and payroll has a written why, or they differ.
4. Harvest not used on a growth/capability thesis.
5. Day 1 interim vs end-state present.
6. Hold-separate not treated as a value thesis.
7. No invented org boxes (that is org-one-pager).
8. Not an essay about Haspeslagh.

If 1, 2, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Two of thesis / functions / Day-1 vs end-state missing after one ask → issues list.
- "We'll integrate everything eventually" → refuse.
- They want the org chart of N people → [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md).
- They want SKU keep/kill → [Portfolio Keep-Kill](../../ma/portfolio-keep-kill/SKILL.md).
