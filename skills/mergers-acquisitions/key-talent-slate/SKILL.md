# Key Talent Slate

**Key talent slate** — one page the CEO/CHRO signs: three columns (Retain / Walk-away / Watch), why (thesis / customer / system / Day-1), stay-award yes/no + amount + vest, owner of the 1:1, conversation date. Stay-pool strip on the same page. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500.

Method origin: McKinsey public — stay money for typically **<2% of staff**; nominate on impact × flight probability, not title.

If they want a talent-retention lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is dated (or this week) and nobody has named who must stay
- Stay cheques are about to go to the org chart
- Function heads have a list; the D has not signed three columns
- Hidden mid-level names appear after announce (second wave)

## When not to use

- Opening or scoring an inbound hire — [Hiring Scorecard](../../management/hiring-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- Closing a candidate offer — [Offer Conversation](../../writing/offer-conversation/SKILL.md)
- Run-rate heads, freeze vs hire — [Headcount Plan](../../management/headcount-plan/SKILL.md)
- Who runs the combined unit Day 1 — [Day-1 Leadership Slate](../../ma/leadership-day1/SKILL.md)
- Ongoing 90-day watch after the lists exist — [Flight Risk 90](../../ma/flight-risk-90/SKILL.md)
- Surplus selection / a termination meeting — counsel + [Difficult Conversation](../../writing/difficult-conversation/SKILL.md). Do not write redundancy criteria here.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Thesis exists; names or nominators exist | Three-column slate + stay-pool strip + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted an org chart or a stay-bonus spreadsheet | Force three columns; kill walk-away cheques; cap the pool |
| **refuse** | Thesis and names both missing, or they want stay money for the whole company | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Three lists.** Every named person is Retain, Walk-away, or Watch. Unnamed is a hole, not "everyone stays."
2. **Retain = the deal breaks if they leave** (thesis, named customer, named system, Day-1 continuity). Title is not the test. Below-exec names are expected.
3. **Do not stay-bonus a walk-away.** Walk-away gets a clean-exit owner and a date, not a cheque.
4. **Stay awards are a thin slice.** Typical public bar: **<2% of staff**. If the yes-list is an org layer, it is not a stay list.
5. **Both companies nominate.** Function heads + HRBPs of acquirer and target; CEO / CHRO / integration leader vet.
6. **Waves are allowed.** Hidden talent appears post-announce. Wave 2 is a dated add, not "we'll cover everyone later."
7. **Stay = yes needs mechanics:** amount, vest dates, cash vs other, qualifying-termination rule, 1:1 owner, conversation **this week**. Personal outreach is not optional colour.
8. **Watch is a conversation, not a cheque.** Owner + date this week. Feed [Flight Risk 90](../../ma/flight-risk-90/SKILL.md).
9. **Pre-close: plan, do not direct** the still-independent target's people. Label pre-close vs post-close. Works-council / HSR = counsel.
10. **Do not invent names or amounts.** Holes stay holes.

## Intake

If **deal thesis** and **any named people (or a named nominator + date)** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Deal / combined unit; close date (or sign-to-close)
2. Deal thesis in one sentence — what you bought people for (load-bearing)
3. Nominated names from both sides, or who nominates by when (load-bearing)
4. Who has D on stay awards (CEO / CHRO / IMO)
5. Stay-pool budget if any ($ or % of deal) — or "not set"
6. Who already knows they are critical or walking
7. Pre-close constraints (HSR / works council) or "already closed"

## QA (must pass)

1. Thesis is a sentence, not "keep the team."
2. Every row is Retain, Walk-away, or Watch — no fourth bucket.
3. No walk-away has Stay = Y.
4. Stay-yes count is a thin slice, or the page flags the <2% miss as a hole.
5. Each stay-yes has amount (or HOLE), vest, 1:1 owner, date this week.
6. Each watch has a conversation owner and date.
7. ASK + named D + date.
8. Not a hiring scorecard, not an offer script, not a headcount cost table.
9. No invented names or dollars.
10. One page.

If 1, 3, 7, or 9 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want stay money for a layer / "everyone who is nervous" → refuse; that is not a stay list.
- They want you to write redundancy criteria or a firing script → stop; counsel + difficult-conversation.
- Pre-close and they want you to direct target employees → refuse gun-jumping; plan only.
- Names exist but nobody will have the 1:1 this week → do not ship a cheque-only page.
