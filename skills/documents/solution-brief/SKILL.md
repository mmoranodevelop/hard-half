# Solution Brief

**4–8 page industry/persona brief** a buyer forwards: warmer → reframe → sourced drowning → required capabilities → your solution mapped 1:1 (persona chapters if needed), architecture a practitioner can reject, one permissioned proof, one CTA. Default: one industry *or* one persona. Chrome from [Document Kit](../../documents/document-kit/SKILL.md) (MM optional or consulting-neutral).

Method origin: Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud Pharma FY26 (5 pp — brief-shaped) + Challenger commercial teaching (Dixon & Adamson 2011; public vendor explainers, **not** a Gartner reprint) + Force Management Value Framework (training vendor) + Trailhead customer-centric discovery. Steal structure, not Cloud blue / Salesforce Sans / Lightning. 12–27 pp Salesforce guides are TOC-only, not this clock.

If they want a Challenger lecture: one paragraph, then produce or stop.

## When to use

- One industry or one persona needs a forwardable brief, not a 2-page sheet
- Commercial teaching on paper: insight before product
- Persona chapters (e.g. clinical / commercial) are the expander

## When not to use

- McKinsey action-title exec pack — [Consulting Deck](../../documents/consulting-deck/SKILL.md)
- Working Backwards press release + FAQ — [PR/FAQ](../../strategy/pr-faq/SKILL.md)
- 1–2 pp, no persona chapters — [Sales Datasheet](../../documents/sales-datasheet/SKILL.md)
- Named-customer FILE with commercial — [Sales Proposal](../../documents/sales-proposal/SKILL.md)
- Permissioned customer *story* as the artifact — [Proof Story](../../comms/proof-story/SKILL.md)
- Rival strategy memo — [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md)
- 12+ page enablement guide — cut to 8 or refuse this slug

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. One industry *or* persona | 4–8 page brief |
| **redline** | Industry adjectives on a product brochure, or a consulting-deck | Force Challenger order; product last; cut to 8 |
| **refuse** | Swap-the-industry still reads; or they want a McKinsey pack / PR-FAQ / 12+ pp | Issues list + route |

## Hard rules

1. **One industry or one persona.** Not both sprawling. If you can swap "Healthcare" for "Manufacturing" and it still reads, it is a datasheet in costume — refuse.
2. **Challenger order (training vendor / book):** Warmer → Reframe → Rational drowning (sourced) → Emotional/local impact → A new way (vendor-agnostic Required Capabilities — Force Management: these *are* the decision criteria) → **Your solution last**, mapped 1:1.
3. **H1 is an industry/persona outcome**, not the product name.
4. **Drowning numbers sourced** or `[HOLE]`. No unsourced "50% productivity."
5. **One permissioned proof:** named customer + metric + source, *or* named executive quote with no invented number.
6. **Architecture caption** a practitioner can reject; asterisk add-ons and not-GA (Pharma-style unreleased disclaimer).
7. **4–8 pages.** Pharma 5 pp is the public boundary. 2 pp = datasheet. 12–27 pp = enablement guide — steal TOC, cut to 8.
8. **Gartner / Forrester: reprint or silence.** No fake MQ/Wave. No "No. 1 / winner."
9. **Kit, not clone.** Never Cloud blue, Salesforce Sans, Lightning, Trailhead characters.

## Intake

If **industry-or-persona** and **the reframe** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a generic brochure.

1. One industry *or* one persona (load-bearing)
2. The misframe you will challenge — in their language (load-bearing)
3. Cost of the current model, with source — or hole
4. 3–5 vendor-agnostic Required Capabilities they would accept as decision criteria
5. Offer map 1:1 + add-ons / not-GA
6. One permissioned proof with source, or "none — HOLE"
7. CTA + owner / date to ship; kit (default consulting-neutral)

## Output shape

Fill `assets/artifact.md`. Spine:

```
H1: [industry/persona outcome]
WARMER → REFRAME → DROWNING (sourced) → LOCAL IMPACT
NEW WAY: [vendor-agnostic capabilities]
SOLUTION: [offer mapped 1:1]  [persona chapters if needed]
ARCHITECTURE (rejectable; *add-on; †not GA)
PROOF: [named + source] or [HOLE]
CTA: [one]   Owner/date: [ ]   Kit: [ ]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. One industry or persona; H1 is an outcome. Swap-test fails (cannot swap the industry).
2. Product named only after Required Capabilities.
3. Drowning / proof numbers sourced or `[HOLE]`.
4. Architecture rejectable; add-ons / not-GA marked.
5. One CTA + owner + date to ship.
6. 4–8 pages. Not a consulting-deck, PR-FAQ, datasheet, or proposal.
7. No fake MQ/Wave. No Cloud-blue clone.

If 1, 2, 3, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want action titles / pyramid slides as the spine → [Consulting Deck](../../documents/consulting-deck/SKILL.md).
- Cover + situation + commercial for a named logo → [Sales Proposal](../../documents/sales-proposal/SKILL.md).
- Unsourced drowning they will not source → HOLE the number or stop.
