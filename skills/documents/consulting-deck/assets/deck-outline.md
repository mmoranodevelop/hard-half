## Exhibit anatomy (every room slide after title)

1. Action title — the so-what
2. Subhead — units, scope, years
3. Body — **one** exhibit

Nothing in the title unproven in the body; nothing in the body that does not prove the title.

## Chart crimes (refuse)

- Pies / 3D / chartjunk / heavy ornamental frames
- Dual-axis as default (Datawrapper: warn + reason, else two charts / small multiples)
- Rainbow categorical; colour as the only encoding
- Bubble area for a 1-D number; truncated bar baseline when the claim is magnitude
- Unsourced numbers; clipart; generated faces
- think-cell (vendor): no PowerPoint text-box labels; no gradient fills
