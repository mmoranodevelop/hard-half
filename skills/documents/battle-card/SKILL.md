# Battle Card

**One-page card for one named rival:** who they actually target, landmines (verifiable gaps), traps (their scoring frame), 3–7 landmine questions, 3–4 objection tracks, one sourced proof, do-not-say list. For the seller in the room (and a champion who forwards it). Default: venture / mid-market bake-off. Chrome from [Document Kit](../../documents/document-kit/SKILL.md) (MM optional or consulting-neutral).

Method origin: Salesforce *Sales Battle Cards 2026* (public marketing page: 1–2 pages; "You don’t need to trash the other company"; update quarterly; battle card ≠ script) + Force Management Command of the Message (training vendor: differentiate without bad-mouthing or overstating). Gartner Content Compliance + Forrester Wave Citation Guidelines: reprint or silence.

If they want a competitive-intel lecture: one paragraph, then produce or stop.

## When to use

- The buyer named an alternative; the AE needs a scannable card *this meeting*
- PMM refresh (quarterly) of a named-rival card from win/loss + top sellers
- Champion will forward a one-pager — questions, not a smear sheet
- Quarterly PMM refresh is due and the rival is still in live deals

## When not to use

- Rival’s TAM / hiring / "how we beat them as a company" — [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md)
- Why *this deal* was won/lost — [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md) (use it to *build* the card; the debrief is not the card)
- Industry commercial teaching — [Solution Brief](../../documents/solution-brief/SKILL.md)
- Offer sheet — [Sales Datasheet](../../documents/sales-datasheet/SKILL.md)
- Permissioned story as the artifact — [Proof Story](../../comms/proof-story/SKILL.md)
- Ten-page competitive bible — Salesforce public: they will not read it; refuse this slug

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Rival named | 1-page card |
| **redline** | Teardown / smear / fake MQ pasted as a "card" | Force questions; kill slander; one page |
| **refuse** | No named rival; false claims; invented competitor prices; fake MQ/Wave | Issues list + legal stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Named rival.** A "generic competitive card" is a value-prop card, not this slug — refuse.
2. **Landmines = facts the buyer can verify**, not insults. "Highlight what they actually lack" (Salesforce public). Force Management (training vendor): no bad-mouthing, no overstating.
3. **Traps = their frame** (price-only, point-solution, "we already have X"). How they want the bake-off scored — not their strategy system.
4. **Landmine questions, 3–7.** The buyer discovers the gap. Not a script to read word-for-word.
5. **Proof with source.** Permissioned analogue or named study. Buyers want receipts.
6. **Do-not-say:** false prices, fake Magic Quadrant / Wave, "they will go bankrupt," unsourced "everyone who switches." **Legal refuse** on false claims, invented competitor price lists, and fake analyst graphics. Gartner: no "No. 1 / ranked / scored" for MQ; no research as a competitive weapon. Forrester: no "winner / best-in-class / No. 1"; do not name other vendors in a Wave; no physical-placement claims.
7. **One page.** 2 pages only if the reverse is the question list. Update quarterly (as-of date on the card).
8. **Kit, not clone.** Never Cloud blue, Salesforce Sans, Lightning.

## Intake

If **the rival** is unnamed after one round: refuse. Do not invent a collage.

1. Named rival + who they actually target (load-bearing)
2. When this card is used (buyer named them / bake-off) (load-bearing)
3. Verifiable gaps (landmines) with how the buyer checks
4. Their scoring frame (traps)
5. 3–7 questions + 3–4 repeated objections
6. One proof with source — or hole
7. As-of / next quarterly refresh + owner; kit (default consulting-neutral)

## Output shape

Fill `assets/artifact.md`. Spine:

```
BATTLE CARD  |  vs [Rival]  |  as-of [date]  |  refresh [quarter]
Who they target vs who we target
LANDMINES (verifiable)  |  TRAPS (their frame)
LANDMINE QUESTIONS (3–7)
OBJECTIONS (3–4): validate → reframe to TCO / required capability
PROOF: [analogue + source] or [HOLE]
DO NOT SAY: [false prices / fake MQ / bankruptcy / unsourced switchers]
ASK: use in [meeting date]  |  PMM refresh by [date]
Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Rival named. One page. As-of + next refresh.
2. Landmines are verifiable; questions not slander.
3. Proof sourced or `[HOLE]`.
4. Do-not-say present. No invented competitor prices. No fake MQ/Wave. No "No. 1 / winner."
5. Not a teardown, not a win-loss, not a ten-page bible, not a word-for-word script.
6. ASK: meeting to use it *or* PMM refresh date + owner.
7. No Cloud-blue clone.

If 1, 2, 4, or 5 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They will not name a rival → refuse.
- False claim / invented price / fake MQ they insist on keeping → legal refuse; stop.
- Strategy memo they call a battle card → [Competitive Teardown](../../strategy/competitive-teardown/SKILL.md).
- One deal’s autopsy → [Win Loss](../../strategy/win-loss/SKILL.md).
- They want a word-for-word script → refuse; questions and talk tracks only.
