# Staffing Mix

**Program mix card** — onshore / contractor / vendor on **one named program**, hours-weighted blend, cost vs sold price, margin test pass/fail. Default: venture program; same spine for F500.

Method origin: Umbrex global-delivery primer + ConsultBase engagement-weighted blend — mix is this program's hours, not a slogan.

If they want an offshoring lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop. Labor in primary offshore hubs can be **40–70%** cheaper (Umbrex); many contracts *illustrate* **30% onshore / 70% offshore**. That is not a target to copy. Deloitte Global (500+ execs): **70%** brought previously outsourced work in-house in five years; **67%** use outcome-based outsourcing. "Offshore first" is a slogan. The artifact is this program's split versus the margin the SOW must clear.

## When to use

- Staffing this week's seats on a named program and the blend has no margin test
- Sold rate cannot clear cost of the current mix
- Someone is averaging three roles with equal weights, or copying last year's firm mix

## When not to use

- Source a capability (build / buy / ally) — [Make vs Buy](../../strategy/make-vs-buy/SKILL.md)
- Buy one vendor: SOW, SLA, exit — [Vendor SOW](../../management/vendor-sow/SKILL.md)
- Score a vendor across programs — [Vendor Scorecard](../../management/vendor-scorecard/SKILL.md)
- Roles, start dates, cost of FTEs — [Headcount Plan](../../management/headcount-plan/SKILL.md)
- Named people idle vs billed — [Utilization Bench](../../delivery/utilization-bench/SKILL.md)
- The list of rates the team may quote — [Rate Card](../../delivery/rate-card/SKILL.md)
- Pre-quote yes/no on price + legal + delivery as one package — [Deal Desk](../../commercial/deal-desk/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named program + sold price + roles exist | Mix card + margin test + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted "offshore first" or an equal-weight blend | Force hours-weighted mix; kill the slogan |
| **refuse** | Firm slogan; two load-bearing answers missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **One named program.** Firm-wide delivery slogans are not a mix card.
2. **Weight by this engagement's hours**, not equal thirds, not last year's firm mix (ConsultBase). A premium-only engagement is not blended down.
3. **Margin test before the week.** Cost of the mix vs sold price. If it cannot clear the SOW margin, change the mix or change the price — do not hope.
4. **30/70 is an illustration, not a target.** Work that cannot go offshore (proximity, domain, client rule) stays onshore — a program fact.
5. **Buckets:** FTE onshore / FTE near-offshore / contractor / named vendor. SPI 2026: subcontractors **10.4%** of PS revenue — a measured share, not a vibe.
6. **Do not** open a headcount req, sign a vendor SOW, or rewrite make-vs-buy from this card.
7. **Do not invent rates or hours.** Hole stays hole. Blend sits on top of [Rate Card](../../delivery/rate-card/SKILL.md); do not average a new list in a live week.

## Intake

If **named program** and **sold price / target margin** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake card. Two of 1–3 missing → stop.

1. Named program + client (load-bearing)
2. Sold rate or blended price + target project margin (load-bearing)
3. Roles × location × type × hours on **this** engagement (load-bearing)
4. What cannot go offshore (proximity / domain / client rule) or "none"
5. Named vendors / contractors already on the SOW, or "none"
6. Who has D on mix this week
7. In-flight actual hours by bucket vs plan — or "not started"

## QA (must pass)

1. Named program, not a firm slogan.
2. Hours-weighted — not equal thirds.
3. Margin test is PASS or FAIL with a number (or HOLE), not "should be fine."
4. FAIL has a mix change or a price change, not hope.
5. ASK + named D + date.
6. Not a make-vs-buy, not a vendor SOW, not a headcount plan.
7. No invented rates or hours.
8. One page.

If 1, 3, 5, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- "Offshore first" / copy 30/70 as policy → refuse; illustration, not a target.
- They want a vendor contract or an HC req from this card → route; do not write it here.
- Sold price missing and they still want a blend → issues list.
