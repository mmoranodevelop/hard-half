# Customer letter spine (annex)

Companion to [SLA Credit](sand-workflow:sla-credit). Counsel reviews when MSA risk is material.

```
SUBJECT: Service credit for [product] — [period]

[Customer name],

1. FACTS
During [period], [metric] measured [result] against our commitment of [commitment]
(source: [monitoring / contract method]). Exclusions applied: [none / list].

2. CREDIT
We will apply a service credit of [%$ / $] against [next invoice / stated method],
per [contract section].

3. WHAT WE ARE CHANGING
[Prevent-repeat action in one to three sentences.] Owner: [ ]. Target date: [ ].

4. CONTACT
[Name, role, channel.]

Optional — chronic failure / termination rights status: [only if counsel-approved].
```
