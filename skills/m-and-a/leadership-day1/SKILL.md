# Day-1 Leadership Slate

**Day-1 leadership slate** for one combined unit — name, title, reports-to, solid vs dotted, **interim vs end-state**, effective date, who tells them, who tells their teams. Empty box = named interim + decision date, never TBD. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500.

Method origin: Bain public (step 7) — pick people from both sides who can deliver the vision; ambitious deadline for the top levels. Deloitte public — Day-1 blueprint may differ from the end-state model.

If they want an org-design lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is dated and the all-hands needs a named boss
- CEO–1 of the combined unit is unstaffed or dual
- End-state org is months out; Day 1 still needs a name
- "We'll announce the org once we know them"

## When not to use

- Steady-state boxes, spans, layers for a team of N — [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- Retain / walk-away / stay-bonus — [Key Talent Slate](../../ma/key-talent-slate/SKILL.md)
- Legal-cleared employee words — [Day-1 Employee Pack](../../ma/day1-employee-pack/SKILL.md)
- IMO as the value vehicle — [IMO Charter](../../ma/imo-charter/SKILL.md)
- 100-day outcomes / kill-old-path — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- Opening a vacant end-state role to the market — [Hiring Scorecard](../../management/hiring-scorecard/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Unit + Day 1 dated | Slate table + empty-box interims + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted an org chart or "TBD" slide | Kill TBD; force interim + date; mark interim vs end-state |
| **refuse** | Unit and Day 1 date both missing, or they want "announce after we get to know them" as the plan | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Name who runs the unit on Day 1 before the all-hands.** Delay is when customers and talent are poached.
2. **Empty box = interim + decision date.** "TBD" is a fail. The interim is a person who will take the call on Day 1.
3. **Every row is interim or end-state (or both, dated).** Day 1 is allowed to differ from the leaner model. Do not pretend they are the same if they are not.
4. **Pick from both sides when the thesis requires it.** Slotting one org into the other with no thesis is a fail.
5. **Reports-to, solid vs dotted, effective date** on every named row. Hidden dual solid lines fail.
6. **Who tells them, who tells their teams** — named, sequenced. Hit people before the room. Then [Day-1 Employee Pack](../../ma/day1-employee-pack/SKILL.md) / [All-Hands](../../writing/all-hands/SKILL.md).
7. **Do not announce an org you have not staffed.** If the box is empty, announce the interim and the date, not the fantasy chart.
8. **Do not invent names.** Holes stay holes. A vacant end-state box that will be hired is a dated search, not a Day-1 silence.

## Intake

If **combined unit** and **Day 1 date** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Combined unit name and Day 1 / close date (load-bearing)
2. Deal thesis in one sentence — who must be able to run this
3. Current CEO and CEO–1 of both sides (names or "unknown")
4. Which boxes are locked vs open as of today
5. End-state org date if different from Day 1 — or "same"
6. Who has D (CEO / sponsor / IMO)
7. Who already knows; who must be told before the all-hands

## QA (must pass)

1. Unit and Day 1 date named.
2. Unit CEO (or equivalent) is a person, or a named interim + date.
3. Every empty box has an interim person and a decision date — no TBD.
4. Interim vs end-state labelled on every row.
5. Reports-to and solid/dotted present.
6. Who-tells-whom is named; hit people before the room.
7. ASK + D + date.
8. Not a spans-and-layers org one-pager.
9. No invented names.
10. One page.

If 2, 3, 7, or 9 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- "We'll announce after we get to know them" → refuse; that is the poaching window.
- They want you to publish the end-state chart with empty boxes → refuse; this page is Day 1.
- Works-council / appointment rules block a name → still name the interim; counsel on the process; do not ship TBD.
- The real job is retain/walk-away → route to key-talent-slate.
