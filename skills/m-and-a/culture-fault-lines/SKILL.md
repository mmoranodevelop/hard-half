# Culture Fault Lines

**Ways-of-working collision map** — one page, 6–8 rows: Acquirer today / Target today / **Day-1 rule** / End-state intent / owner. Plus a do-not-touch list if the thesis depends on the acquired operating culture. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500.

Method origin: Bain public — diagnose innate fault lines (purpose, decision-making, engagement); IMO as a culture lab before Day 1. Not a values poster.

If they want a culture-integration lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is dated and the two companies already collide on meetings, decisions, or tools
- "Best of both" is the current plan and nobody can say the Day-1 rule
- Deal thesis requires **preserving** the acquired way of working in a named domain
- IMO needs a 30-day guardrail page, not a survey

## When not to use

- Observable behaviours at go-live, ADKAR, leading indicators — [Change Adoption](../../management/change-adoption/SKILL.md)
- The room that narrates a decision already made — [All-Hands](../../writing/all-hands/SKILL.md)
- Legal-cleared Day 1 employee words — [Day-1 Employee Pack](../../ma/day1-employee-pack/SKILL.md)
- Steady-state boxes — [Org One-Pager](../../management/org-one-pager/SKILL.md)
- 100-day outcomes / kill-old-path — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- Title/pay freeze and mapping date — [Title Grade Map](../../ma/title-grade-map/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Two ways of working can be named | 6–8 row map + do-not-touch + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted values, a survey, or "best of both" | Kill slogans; force Day-1 rule per row |
| **refuse** | No collision examples and no preserve-vs-absorb call | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Do not write a values poster.** Rows are operating collisions: how a decision gets made, meeting load, who is in the room, tools, hybrid, expenses/travel class, performance rituals, what "done" means.
2. **6–8 rows, not 40.** If a row has no Day-1 consequence, cut it.
3. **Every row has a Day-1 rule** (what people do Monday) and an owner. "TBD / best of both" is a fail.
4. **End-state intent is labelled and dated** if it differs from Day 1. Do not pretend Day 1 is the destination.
5. **Preserve vs absorb is a thesis call**, domain by domain. If you bought the target's way of working, it goes on **do-not-touch**.
6. **IMO is the lab, not the slogan factory.** Fault lines you will not decide still get a 30-day guardrail (which rule holds until the date).
7. **Do not invent how the other company works.** If unknown, hole the cell; do not write "collaborative."
8. Behaviours to install at a go-live live on [Change Adoption](../../management/change-adoption/SKILL.md). This page is the collision, not the adoption pack.

## Intake

If **any concrete collision** and **preserve vs absorb (or "undecided")** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Deal / unit; Day 1 date
2. Preserve vs absorb the acquired operating culture — or "undecided by domain" (load-bearing)
3. Collisions already seen: decisions, meetings, tools, hybrid, travel, "done" (load-bearing)
4. Who owns Day-1 ways of working (IMO / function heads)
5. Do-not-touch candidates (what you bought)
6. Pulse date (6–8 weeks default) or "none yet"

## QA (must pass)

1. 6–8 rows; each has a Day-1 rule that a manager can say Monday.
2. No row is a values slogan.
3. Preserve vs absorb (or mix-by-domain) is on the page.
4. Do-not-touch is either named or explicitly "absorb on purpose."
5. Every row has an owner.
6. ASK + date.
7. Unknown cells are holes, not "collaborative."
8. Not an ADKAR pack, not an all-hands script.
9. One page.

If 1, 2, 6, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want "best of both" with no Day-1 rule → refuse; that is how fault lines become missed deadlines.
- They want this page to be the change-adoption behaviours → route.
- Works-council / policy lock on hybrid or travel → counsel; still name the Day-1 rule as a hole until cleared.
