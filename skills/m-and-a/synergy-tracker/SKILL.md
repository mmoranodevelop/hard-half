# Synergy Tracker

**Exec synergy tracker** — announced vs locked target vs run-rate realised vs in-year P&L; cost vs revenue; top initiatives with proof source; leakage; one-time vs run-rate; ASK this week. First combined close calendar is an **annex**. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500.

Method origin: McKinsey public baseline-before-claims and cost vs revenue split + BCG public monthly tracking with finance reconciliation.

If they want a synergy lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is dated or done and the deal model has numbers with no living tracker
- "On track" with no baseline or proof source
- Revenue synergies tracked only as total revenue (hides the leak)
- First combined close needs synergy lines finance will actually sign

## When not to use

- Period vs this year's budget — [Budget Variance](../../management/budget-variance/SKILL.md)
- CSM account value / QBR — [Value Realization](../../accounts/value-realization/SKILL.md)
- 100-day outcomes + kill-old-path — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- TSA markup / dual-stack catalog — [TSA Schedule](../../ma/tsa-schedule/SKILL.md)
- Weekly steering sitting — [Steering Pack](../../project/steering-pack/SKILL.md)
- Named-account owner/script at close — [Named Account Day 1](../../ma/named-account-day1/SKILL.md)
- SKU keep/kill as the synergy idea — [Portfolio Keep-Kill](../../ma/portfolio-keep-kill/SKILL.md)

First combined close calendar: annex on **this** page. Do not invent a `first-close` skill.

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Named deal | Exec tracker + leakage + ASK; close annex if period-end is near |
| **redline** | They pasted a PMO "on track" or a budget pack labelled synergy | Rebuild from baseline; kill total-revenue theatre |
| **refuse** | Two load-bearing facts missing, or a synergy lecture | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Baseline first.** No baseline = no claims. Holes stay holes.
2. **Split cost and revenue.** Never one blob. Line leaders own dollars; IMO owns the single tracker.
3. **Four numbers on page 1:** announced / locked / run-rate realised / in-year P&L. One-time cost vs run-rate.
4. **Top initiatives:** owner, proof source, status. PMO "on track" with no proof = fail.
5. **Name leakage:** dual stack, TSA markup, stranded, overestimated savings, attrition, delayed cross-sell. Do not invent amounts.
6. **Cadence:** weekly IMO value review; monthly finance recon; CEO monthly on **revenue** synergies until they sit in BAU budgets.
7. **First-close annex:** entity close → IC → consol → pack. Synergy claims only if finance-reconciled. Extra days named. Do not silently slip board/lender dates.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 3, 4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake tracker.

1. Locked baseline — deal model / board pack, as-of date (or "no baseline") — load-bearing
2. Announced vs locked target (they may differ)
3. Cost vs revenue split — load-bearing
4. Top initiatives — owner, not a workstream name — load-bearing
5. Proof source / last finance recon
6. Known leakage — dual stack, TSA markup, attrition, delayed cross-sell
7. Next period-end (for the first-close annex)

## QA (must pass)

1. ASK (owner, verb, amount or initiative, date).
2. Baseline present or labelled hole.
3. Cost and revenue split.
4. Initiative has named owner and proof source.
5. Revenue is not total revenue only.
6. No synergy claimed that finance has not reconciled.
7. Not a budget-variance pack or a CSM value page.
8. No invented amounts.

If 1, 3, 6, or 8 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Two of baseline / cost-vs-revenue / named initiatives missing after one ask → issues list.
- Synergy number with no baseline → refuse.
- PMO "on track" with no proof source → refuse.
- They want period vs budget → [Budget Variance](../../management/budget-variance/SKILL.md).
- They want a first-close skill → keep the annex here.
