# Brand Architecture Deal

**Brand architecture card** — Day-1 signal **and** 12–24 month end-state, separately. Default: venture close; endorsed is the usual 90-day skin.

Method origin: McKinsey brand-in-M&A public (Science = fact-based equity, not the winner's logo; Scope = tackle less on Day 1; 80% transition within 18 months).

If they want a brand-architecture lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

**Clock.** Week 1 = endorsed or hidden + FAQ. **No fleet, packaging, or product-UI rebrand.** Quarter 1 = end-state on facts + first migration date.

## When to use

- Two names now sit on the same invoice, site, or sales deck
- Close week needs a signal (what appears on the email footer)
- Day 45: end-state must be chosen on equity facts, not the winner's logo

## When not to use

- This/not-that **messaging** — [Positioning House](../../branding/positioning-house/SKILL.md)
- Day-1 customer script — [Named Account Day 1](../../ma/named-account-day1/SKILL.md)
- Two price lists / grandfather — [Packaging Collision](../../ma/packaging-collision/SKILL.md)
- SKU keep / kill (the offer, not the mark) — [Portfolio Keep-Kill](../../ma/portfolio-keep-kill/SKILL.md)
- Hold / absorb the company — [Operating Model Choice](../../ma/operating-model-choice/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default | Day-1 signal + end-state + first migration date + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted "we will rebrand on Day 1" | Split signal vs end-state; kill the fleet swap |
| **refuse** | Week-1 full rebrand; two load-bearing facts missing | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **Two clocks, two decisions.** Day-1 signal is not the end-state. Mixing them is how you put the winner's logo on a product you cannot support.
2. **Architectures:** branded house (one master name). Endorsed ("[Brand], a [Parent] company" / powered-by) — default **bridge**. Hidden / house of brands (no parent on the box). Co-brand is rare and **time-boxed** with an exit date.
3. **Week 1: endorsed or hidden.** FAQ. Do-not-touch: product UI, invoices, support signature, fleet, packaging. Full rebrand in week 1 → refuse.
4. **End-state by Day 45**, on equity facts (awareness / preference / search / price premium / NPS) or labelled holes. Science, not the winner's logo.
5. **Switching cost** if we retire a name: write it or hole it.
6. **Migration is a calendar**, not a slogan. First touchpoint + date. Packaging/fleet later.
7. **Do not invent equity numbers.** Hole stays hole.
8. Christensen public: if you bought a distinct model, hidden / endorsed until the model is proven; house-absorb kills it.

## Intake

If **two** of 1, 2, 4 are missing after one round: issues list, not a fake architecture.

1. Deal + the two names — load-bearing
2. Close date / Day n — load-bearing
3. What already appears Day 1 (site, invoice, email, product UI)
4. Equity facts (awareness / search / NPS / none) — load-bearing; "none" is allowed if labelled
5. Channel conflict or distinct segments? (Y/N)
6. Owner of ASK (CMO) and decide-by (Day 1 for signal; Day 45 for end-state)

## Output shape

```
BRAND ARCHITECTURE  |  [deal]  |  names: [buyer] / [target]  |  as-of: [date]
ASK: [CMO] approves Day-1 signal + end-state + first migration date by [Day 1 / Day 45].
Owner of ASK: [CMO]    Decide-by: Day 1 (signal) / Day 45 (end-state)

Day-1 signal: endorsed | hidden | house | other [ ]
End-state (12–24 mo): branded house | endorsed | hidden | hybrid | co-brand until [date]
Equity facts used (awareness / preference / search / price premium / NPS): [or HOLE]
Switching cost if we retire [name]: [or HOLE]
Do-not-touch before [date]: product UI, invoices, support signature, fleet, packaging
Migration: [touchpoint] on [date]. Packaging/fleet later.

WEEK-1: no fleet/UI/packaging swap. Signal only.

NOT THIS PAGE
Messaging → positioning-house    Day-1 script → named-account-day1    Price lists → packaging-collision    SKUs → portfolio-keep-kill

Holes: [ ]
```

## QA (must pass)

1. Day-1 signal and end-state are separate cells, or already house and staying house with the date named.
2. No week-1 full rebrand / fleet / UI swap.
3. End-state "fact-based" has facts or a hole.
4. ASK + owner + date.
5. Not a positioning-house.
6. No invented awareness %.
7. One page.

If 2, 4, or 6 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- Winner's logo on Day 1 as the whole plan → refuse.
- Rebrand / reprice / sunset / rewrite in week 1 → refuse.
- They want this/not-that copy → [Positioning House](../../branding/positioning-house/SKILL.md).
