# Acquired Org Onboard

**Acquired-org onboarding plan** — one page: cohorts (execs / people-managers / customer-facing / plant / remote), Day-1 access/payroll/manager, 30/60/90 moments of truth, tool cutover, receiving-side buddy **by cohort**, date the receiving BU owns BAU. Default: venture deal; same spine for F500.

Method origin: McKinsey public — onboard the **merged entity** (tools, access, setup), not 400 copies of a new-hire plan; high-potentials in the room with senior leadership.

If they want an onboarding-theory lecture: one paragraph then produce or stop.

## When to use

- Close is dated and the acquired population has badge/laptop then silence
- 400 individual 90-day plans are the current "PMI"
- Receiving BU has no takeover date
- Customer-facing / plant / remote cohorts have different Day-1 risks

## When not to use

- One person's first 90 days — [Ninety-Day Onboarding](../../learning/ninety-day-onboarding/SKILL.md)
- 100-day outcomes / kill-old-path — [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md)
- Behaviours at a go-live — [Change Adoption](../../management/change-adoption/SKILL.md)
- Flight-risk names and this week's 1:1 owners — [Flight Risk 90](../../ma/flight-risk-90/SKILL.md)
- Legal-cleared Day 1 words — [Day-1 Employee Pack](../../ma/day1-employee-pack/SKILL.md)
- Systems/payroll actually running — [Day-1 Continuity](../../ma/day1-continuity/SKILL.md)

## Modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **produce** | Default. Close dated; population exists | Cohort plan + 30/60/90 + BU takeover + ASK |
| **redline** | They pasted a new-hire checklist × N | Collapse to cohorts; force takeover date; kill 400 copies |
| **refuse** | Day 1 and receiving-BU owner both missing, or they want 400 personal 90-day plans as the job | Issues list. Stop |

## Hard rules

1. **A population with a journey, not N copies of a hire plan.** If the output is 400 [Ninety-Day Onboarding](../../learning/ninety-day-onboarding/SKILL.md) cards, you are in the wrong skill.
2. **Cohorts, not one blob.** Minimum useful split: execs / people-managers / customer-facing / plant or sites / remote. Add one only if the journey actually differs.
3. **Day 1 is access, payroll, named manager, tools.** Each cohort: what must work Monday, owner, test. Badge without a manager is a fail.
4. **30/60/90 are moments of truth**, not activity lists. Example tests: first customer call on the new stack; first combined 1:1; first payroll on buyer calendar; first skip-level with receiving leadership.
5. **Receiving-side buddy is by cohort**, named role (not "HR will pair them"). Execs need a peer, not a buddy-app.
6. **High-potentials are in the room** with senior leadership on integration direction — named session + date. Not a later town hall.
7. **Receiving BU takeover is a date** with a test (BAU owner named, IMO steps back). Silence after laptop is the failure mode.
8. **Do not invent headcount, sites, or tool dates.** Holes stay holes.
9. **Do not run surplus on this page.** Counsel + difficult-conversation.

## Intake

If **close / Day 1 date** and **receiving BU (or owner)** are both missing after one round: issues list, not a fake page.

1. Close date; receiving BU / named owner (load-bearing)
2. Headcount, sites, remote mix — or "unknown"
3. Cohorts they already see (or "help me split")
4. Day-1 access / payroll / manager status
5. Tool cutover dates (or "unknown")
6. Date the receiving BU should own BAU
7. High-potentials who must be in the room — or "slate not done" → [Key Talent Slate](../../ma/key-talent-slate/SKILL.md)

## QA (must pass)

1. Cohorts exist; not one blob and not N personal plans.
2. Day 1: access, payroll, named manager — per cohort or a labelled hole.
3. 30/60/90 are tests, not training catalogues.
4. Buddy is a receiving role by cohort.
5. BAU takeover has a date and a test.
6. ASK + owner + date.
7. Not a ninety-day-onboarding card, not an integration-100 outcome list.
8. No invented headcount or cutover dates.
9. One page.

If 1, 5, 6, or 7 fail: do not ship.

## Escalate / stop

- They want 400 individual 90-day plans → refuse; offer this page; one senior hire may still get ninety-day-onboarding.
- Receiving BU will not take a date → do not ship a journey with no landing.
- Tool cutover with no kill-old-path → point at [Integration 100](../../management/integration-100/SKILL.md); still name the cohort impact here.
